# Buddhist Principles — หลักธรรมทางพระพุทธศาสนา

> *"The essence of Buddhism is not ritual but practice — understanding suffering, its cause, its end, and the path to end it."*

Buddhist Principles covers the core doctrines of Theravada Buddhism as taught in Thai schools. From simple moral stories in primary school to deep philosophical analysis in secondary, students progressively understand the Buddha's teachings and how to apply them in daily life.

## 2 | Four Noble Truths (อริยสัจ 4)

| Noble Truth | Thai | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Dukkha | ทุกข์ | Suffering/unsatisfactoriness exists | Birth, aging, illness, death, sorrow |
| 2. Samudaya | สมุทัย | Cause of suffering is craving (ตัณหา) | Desire for pleasure, existence, non-existence |
| 3. Nirodha | นิโรธ | Suffering can end | Nirvana — cessation of craving |
| 4. Magga | มรรค | Path to end suffering | Noble Eightfold Path |

### ม.4–6 Deepening: Analyzing Dukkha

| Type of Dukkha | Thai | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Dukkha-dukkha** | ทุกข์ทุกข์ | Obvious suffering (pain, illness) |
| **Viparinama-dukkha** | วิปรินามทุกข์ | Suffering from change (loss, aging) |
| **Sankhara-dukkha** | สังขารทุกข์ | Suffering from conditioned existence |

## 5 | Key Buddhist Doctrines (ม.1–3)

### Five Aggregates (ขันธ์ 5)

| Aggregate | Thai | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Form | รูป | Physical body |
| Feeling | เวทนา | Pleasant, unpleasant, neutral |
| Perception | สัญญา | Recognition, memory |
| Mental formations | สังขาร | Volition, mental activities |
| Consciousness | วิญญาณ | Awareness |

### Three Characteristics (ไตรลักษณ์)

| Characteristic | Thai | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Impermanence | อนิจจัง | Everything changes |
| Suffering | ทุกขัง | Unsatisfactoriness |
| Non-self | อนัตตา | No permanent self |

### Four Sublime States (พรหมวิหาร 4)

| State | Thai | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Loving-kindness | เมตตา | Wish for others' happiness |
| Compassion | กรุณา | Wish for others' freedom from suffering |
| Equanimity | อุเบกขา | Balanced mind, non-attachment |
| Sympathetic joy | มุทิตา | Joy in others' good fortune |

## 6 | Advanced Doctrines (ม.4–6)

### Dependent Origination (ปฏิจจสมุปบาท)

The 12 links of dependent origination explain how suffering arises:

| Link | Thai | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Ignorance | อวิชชา | Not understanding the truth |
| 2. Formations | สังขาร | Mental formations |
| 3. Consciousness | วิญญาณ | Awareness |
| 4. Name-and-form | นามรูป | Mind and body |
| 5. Six senses | สฬายตนะ | Eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind |
| 6. Contact | ผัสสะ | Sense contact |
| 7. Feeling | เวทนา | Pleasant, unpleasant, neutral |
| 8. Craving | ตัณหา | Desire, attachment |
| 9. Clinging | อุปาทาน | Grasping |
| 10. Becoming | ภูกำเนิด | Process of becoming |
| 11. Birth | ชาติ | Birth |
| 12. Aging and death | ชรามรณะ | Decay and death |

### Karma and Rebirth (กรรมและการเวียนว่ายตายเกิด)

| Concept | Thai | ม.4–6 Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| **Types of karma** | ประเภทของกรรม | By body, speech, mind; wholesome/unwholesome |
| **Rebirth realms** | ภูมิ | 31 planes of existence |
| **Karma and free will** | กรรมและเจตนา | Intention determines karma quality |
| **Karma and modern life** | กรรมในชีวิตปัจจุบัน | Ethical decision-making framework |

### Buddhism and Social Issues (พุทธศาสนากับสังคม)

| Issue | Buddhist Perspective |
|---|---|
| **Environmental crisis** | เมตตาต่อสิ่งแวดล้อม, interdependence |
| **Social inequality** | เสมอภาค, compassion for the disadvantaged |
| **Technology ethics** | สัมมาสติ in digital age, mindful consumption |
| **Mental health crisis** | Meditation as therapy, mindfulness-based approaches |
| **Global peace** | อหิงสา, interfaith dialogue, non-violence |
