# Religion, Morality, and Ethics — ศาสนา ศีลธรรม จริยธรรม

> **Strand:** สาระที่ 1 | **Concept Areas:** 7 | **Duration:** 12 years (ป.1–ม.6)
> **Focus:** Buddhism, world religions, moral reasoning, meditation, religious ceremonies

## Overview

This strand develops moral character and religious understanding. Buddhism receives primary emphasis as Thailand's state religion (~95% Buddhist), but students also learn about Islam, Christianity, Hinduism, and Sikhism. The goal is moral reasoning, not conversion.
