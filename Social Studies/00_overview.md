# Social Studies: สังคมศึกษา ศาสนา และวัฒนธรรม

> **Domain:** Social Studies for Basic Education (ป.1–ม.6): one integrated subject, 5 strands (สาระ)
> **BOK roadmap:** [[Social Studies - Overview]]
> **Strands:** 5 | **Topic notes:** 63 of 63 built | **Duration:** 12 years
> **Language policy:** English narrative with Thai terms in parentheses

## What Is This?

Social Studies, Religion, and Culture (สังคมศึกษา ศาสนา และวัฒนธรรม) is the subject that teaches how people live together: what we believe, how we govern, how we trade, where we come from, and where we stand. Unlike science, it never splits into specialized tracks at ม.4. Every student keeps all 5 strands for twelve years.

The subject carries a deliberate double job: academic knowledge and moral character. "Religion" and "Culture" sit in the name on purpose. In Thai education, understanding society is inseparable from ethical development and cultural identity.

Everything here follows one **spiral progression** across four grade bands: simple introductions in ป.1–3, deeper study in ป.4–6, analytical application in ม.1–3, and critical mastery in ม.4–6.

## How the Strands Connect

The strands are taught separately but they rhyme. These are the seams worth crossing:

- **Democracy under constitutional monarchy:** Civics ([[Social Studies/02 Civics/01_Democracy_and_Government|Democracy and Government]], [[Social Studies/02 Civics/05_Thai_Constitution|Thai Constitution]]) reads together with History ([[Social Studies/04 History/08_Rattanakosin_Period|Rattanakosin Period]], [[Social Studies/04 History/09_Important_Monarchs|Important Monarchs]]).
- **Sufficiency economy:** Economics ([[Social Studies/03 Economics/03_Sufficiency_Economy_Philosophy|Sufficiency Economy Philosophy]]) is a living policy idea, so it lands better after History (09_Important_Monarchs).
- **ASEAN:** three strands carry it at once: Civics (03_ASEAN_Studies), Economics (11_ASEAN_Economics), and Geography (12_World_Regions).
- **Environment and resources:** Geography ([[Social Studies/05 Geography/10_Environmental_Conservation|Environmental Conservation]]) meets Economics ([[Social Studies/03 Economics/06_Production_and_Consumption|Production and Consumption]]).
- **Religion and society:** Religion ([[Social Studies/01 Religion/07_Religious_Tolerance_and_Interfaith|Religious Tolerance and Interfaith]]) feeds straight into Civics (02_Thai_Culture_and_Traditions, 07_Social_Institutions).

## Where to Start

- **Following the curriculum:** 01 Religion then upward, the order the strands are listed.
- **Citizenship and law focus:** 02 Civics first, then 04 History for context.
- **Money basics for life:** [[Social Studies/03 Economics/02_Personal_Finance|Personal Finance]] (useful far beyond exams).
- **Thailand deep dive:** 04 History (05 to 08, the four periods) plus 05 Geography (01_Geography_of_Thailand, 11_Thailand_Regions).
- **Exam review:** each strand's `00_overview` has the concept-area matrix with grade bands: start from the column for your band.
