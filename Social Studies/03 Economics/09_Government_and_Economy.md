# Government and Economy — รัฐบาลและเศรษฐกิจ

> *"The art of taxation consists in so plucking the goose as to obtain the largest amount of feathers with the least possible amount of hissing."* — Jean-Baptiste Colbert

Government and Economy examines how governments intervene in markets through fiscal policy, taxation, and public spending. Students learn about the government's role in stabilizing the economy, redistributing income, and providing public goods.

## 2 | Role of Government in Economy (บทบาทของรัฐบาลในระบบเศรษฐกิจ)

| Function | Thai | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Allocation** | การจัดสรร | Provide public goods | Roads, schools, defense |
| **Distribution** | การกระจาย | Reduce inequality | Progressive taxes, welfare |
| **Stabilization** | การรักษาเสถียรภาพ | Control inflation, unemployment | Monetary and fiscal policy |
| **Regulation** | การกำกับดูแล | Protect consumers, environment | Food safety, pollution control |

## 3 | Fiscal Policy (นโยบายการคลัง)

| Tool | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Government spending** | การใช้จ่ายรัฐบาล | Infrastructure, education, healthcare |
| **Taxation** | การจัดเก็บภาษี | Income, consumption, property taxes |
| **Public debt** | หนี้สาธารณะ | Government borrowing |
| **Budget** | งบประมาณ | Plan for revenue and spending |

### Expansionary vs Contractionary Fiscal Policy

| Type | Goal | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Expansionary** | Stimulate economy | ↑ Spending, ↓ Taxes |
| **Contractionary** | Fight inflation | ↓ Spending, ↑ Taxes |

## 4 | Government Budget (งบประมาณแผ่นดิน)

| Component | Thai | Description | 2024 Budget (฿ billion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | รายได้ | Taxes, state enterprises | ~2,700 |
| **Expenditure** | รายจ่าย | Ministries, programs | ~3,350 |
| **Deficit** | ขาดดุล | Spending > Revenue | ~650 |
| **Public debt** | หนี้สาธารณะ | Cumulative borrowing | ~10.7 trillion (62% of GDP) |

### Thailand's Budget Allocation (2024)

| Ministry/Function | % of Budget |
|---|---|
| **Education** | 18% |
| **Defense** | 14% |
| **Healthcare** | 10% |
| **Infrastructure** | 12% |
| **Social welfare** | 8% |
| **Debt service** | 7% |
| **Other** | 31% |

## 5 | Taxation (การจัดเก็บภาษี)

| Type | Thai | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Direct taxes** | ภาษีทางตรง | Paid directly by taxpayer | Income tax, corporate tax |
| **Indirect taxes** | ภาษีทางอ้อม | Passed to consumers | VAT, excise tax |

### Thailand's Tax System

| Tax | Rate | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal income tax** | 0-35% | Progressive rates |
| **Corporate income tax** | 20% | On profits |
| **VAT** | 7% | On goods/services |
| **Specific business tax** | 0.1-3.3% | Banking, insurance |
| **Property tax** | 0.02-1% | On land and buildings |
| **Excise tax** | Varies | Alcohol, tobacco, fuel |

### Tax Principles

| Principle | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Equity** | ความเป็นธรรม | Fair burden (ability to pay) |
| **Efficiency** | ประสิทธิภาพ | Minimal distortion |
| **Simplicity** | ความง่าย | Easy to understand and collect |
| **Flexibility** | ความยืดหยุ่น | Adapts to economic changes |

| Type | Thai | Characteristics | Example |
|---|---|---|---|

## 8 | Government Failures (ความล้มเหลวของรัฐบาล)

| Type | Thai | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bureaucracy** | ระบบราชการ | Slow, inefficient | Red tape, delays |
| **Corruption** | การทุจริต | Misuse of public funds | Bribery, embezzlement |
| **Regulatory capture** | การยึดครองหน่วยงานกำกับ | Regulators serve industry | Weak enforcement |
| **Information failure** | ความล้มเหลวด้านข้อมูล | Poor decision-making | Ineffective policies |
| **Political cycles** | วัฏจักรการเมือง | Short-term focus | Election-year spending |

---

## 9 | Monetary vs Fiscal Policy (นโยบายการเงิน vs การคลัง)

| Aspect | Monetary Policy | Fiscal Policy |
|---|---|---|
| **Who controls** | Bank of Thailand | Ministry of Finance |
| **Tools** | Interest rates, reserves | Taxes, spending |
| **Speed** | Fast implementation | Slow (legislative process) |
| **Target** | Inflation, exchange rate | Growth, employment |
| **Independence** | Independent central bank | Political influence |

## 11 | Real-World Examples

- **30-baht healthcare** — Thailand's universal healthcare since 2002
- **VAT 7%** — Main consumption tax, raised from 0% during crisis
- **Progressive income tax** — 0% for first 150,000 ฿, up to 35%
- **EEC investment incentives** — Tax breaks for foreign investors
- **COVID stimulus** — "We Win" cash handouts, soft loans
