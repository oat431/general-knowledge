# Economics — เศรษฐศาสตร์

> **Strand:** สาระที่ 3 | **Concept Areas:** ~14 | **Duration:** 12 years (ป.1–ม.6)
> **Focus:** Scarcity/choice, sufficiency economy, supply/demand, personal finance, economic systems

## Overview

Economics teaches students how societies manage scarce resources. The Thai curriculum uniquely emphasizes King Rama IX's Sufficiency Economy Philosophy (ปรัชญาเศรษฐกิจพอเพียง) alongside standard economic concepts. Students learn both theoretical economics and practical personal finance.
