# Cooperatives — สหกรณ์

> *"Alone we can do so little; together we can do so much."* — Helen Keller

Cooperatives are member-owned enterprises that serve their members' needs rather than maximize profit. Students learn about cooperative principles, types, and their role in Thailand's rural economy.

## 2 | What is a Cooperative? (สหกรณ์คืออะไร?)

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| **Definition** | Organization owned and operated by members for their mutual benefit |
| **Purpose** | Serve members' needs, not maximize profit |
| **Ownership** | Members own the cooperative |
| **Control** | One member, one vote (democratic) |
| **Profit distribution** | Based on patronage (use), not investment |

### Cooperative vs Corporation

| Aspect | Cooperative | Corporation |
|---|---|---|
| **Ownership** | Members | Shareholders |
| **Control** | One member, one vote | One share, one vote |
| **Purpose** | Member service | Profit maximization |
| **Profit distribution** | Patronage dividends | Dividends per share |
| **Decision-making** | Democratic | Capital-based |

## 3 | Seven Cooperative Principles (หลักการสหกรณ์ 7 ข้อ)

| # | Principle | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Voluntary membership** | การสมัครใจเป็นสมาชิก | Open to all, no discrimination |
| 2 | **Democratic control** | การควบคุมโดยสมาชิก | One member, one vote |
| 3 | **Member economic participation** | การมีส่วนร่วมทางเศรษฐกิจของสมาชิก | Equal contribution, democratic control of capital |
| 4 | **Autonomy and independence** | การปกครองตนเองและความเป็นอิสระ | Self-governing, no external control |
| 5 | **Education and training** | การศึกษา ฝึกอบรม และสารสนเทศ | Educate members and public |
| 6 | **Cooperation among cooperatives** | ความร่วมมือระหว่างสหกรณ์ | Work together locally, nationally, internationally |
| 7 | **Concern for community** | ความเอื้ออาทรต่อชุมชน | Sustainable development of community |

## 4 | Types of Cooperatives (ประเภทของสหกรณ์)

| Type | Thai | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agricultural** | สหกรณ์การเกษตร | Farm credit, marketing, supplies | Rice farmers' cooperative |
| **Savings** | สหกรณ์ออมทรัพย์ | Member savings, loans | Teacher savings cooperative |
| **Consumer** | สหกรณ์ผู้บริโภค | Buy goods at lower prices | University cooperative store |
| **Credit union** | สหกรณ์เครดิตยูเนี่ยน | Financial services | Community credit union |
| **Housing** | สหกรณ์เคหสถาน | Affordable housing | Apartment cooperative |
| **Service** | สหกรณ์บริการ | Provide services to members | Taxi cooperative |
| **Land settlement** | สหกรณ์นิคม | Land distribution | Land reform cooperatives |

## 5 | Cooperatives in Thailand (สหกรณ์ในประเทศไทย)

| Aspect | Data |
|---|---|
| **First cooperative** | 1916 (Wat Chan Cooperative, Phitsanulok) |
| **Number of cooperatives** | ~8,000 |
| **Total members** | ~12 million |
| **Total assets** | ~3 trillion baht |
| **Regulator** | Cooperative Promotion Department (CPD) |

### Major Thai Cooperatives

| Cooperative | Members | Assets (฿ billion) | Sector |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KUK ( Kasikorn)** | 2.5M | 500 | Agricultural |
| **BAAC (Bank for Agriculture)** | 4.7M | 1,800 | Agricultural finance |
| **GSB (Government Savings Bank)** | 20M | 2,500 | Savings |
| **Teacher cooperatives** | 900K | 400 | Savings |
| **Dairy cooperatives** | 100K | 50 | Dairy farming |

### Cooperative Federation Structure

```
Cooperative Promotion Department (CPD)
    ↓
Cooperative League of Thailand (CLT)
    ↓
Provincial Cooperative Federations
    ↓
Individual Cooperatives
    ↓
Members
```

## 6 | Cooperative Management (การจัดการสหกรณ์)

| Body | Thai | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **General meeting** | ที่ประชุมใหญ่ | Highest authority, all members |
| **Board of directors** | คณะกรรมการ | Elected by members, makes decisions |
| **Manager** | ผู้จัดการ | Hired by board, runs daily operations |
| **Supervisory committee** | คณะกรรมการตรวจสอบ | Monitors management |
| **Auditor** | ผู้สอบบัญชี | External audit |

### Member Rights and Duties

| Rights | Duties |
|---|---|
| Vote in general meeting | Pay membership fees |
| Receive dividends | Participate in activities |
| Access services | Follow cooperative rules |
| Inspect records | Attend meetings |

## 7 | Community Enterprises (วิสาหกิจชุมชน)

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Community enterprise** | วิสาหกิจชุมชน | Local business owned by community |
| **OTOP** | หนึ่งตำบล หนึ่งผลิตภัณฑ์ | One Tambon One Product |

## 8 | Challenges of Cooperatives (ความท้าทายของสหกรณ์)

| Challenge | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Management** | การจัดการ | Lack of professional management |
| **Capital** | ทุน | Limited access to capital |
| **Technology** | เทคโนโลยี | Slow adoption of digital tools |
| **Governance** | ธรรมาภิบาล | Corruption, mismanagement |
| **Competition** | การแข่งขัน | Private sector competition |
| **Member engagement** | การมีส่วนร่วมของสมาชิก | Low participation |

### Cooperative Failures

| Issue | Description |
|---|---|
| **Klongchan Credit Union** | 2015 scandal, 4 billion baht fraud |
| **Rubber cooperatives** | Price manipulation, corruption |
| **Agricultural cooperatives** | Debt problems, poor management |

## 11 | Real-World Examples

- **Wat Chan Cooperative (1916)** — First cooperative in Thailand, Phitsanulok

- **OTOP** — Launched 2001, 80,000+ products nationwide
- **Doi Tung Development Project** — Royal project turned successful social enterprise
- **Klongchan scandal** — 2015 fraud case led to cooperative reforms
