# Personal Finance — การเงินส่วนบุคคล

> *"Financial literacy is not about getting rich — it is about making informed decisions with money throughout your life."*

Personal Finance teaches students practical money management skills. From saving coins in a piggy bank to budgeting, banking, and understanding taxes, students develop the financial skills needed for independent life.

## 2 | Money Basics

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Income** | รายได้ | Money received (allowance, salary) |
| **Expenses** | ค่าใช้จ่าย | Money spent |
| **Savings** | เงินออม | Income not spent |
| **Budget** | งบประมาณ | Plan for income and expenses |

### Budget Formula

$$\text{Savings} = \text{Income} - \text{Expenses}$$

---

## 3 | Saving and Banking

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Piggy bank** | กระปุกออมสิน | Simple saving at home |
| **Savings account** | บัญชีออมทรัพย์ | Bank account, earns interest |
| **Fixed deposit** | บัญชีฝากประจำ | Higher interest, fixed term |
| **Bank** | ธนาคาร | Keeps money safe, lends money |
| **Cooperative** | สหกรณ์ | Member-owned financial institution |

### Interest

| Type | Thai | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple interest** | ดอกเบี้ยอย่างง่าย | $$I = P \times r \times t$$ |
| **Compound interest** | ดอกเบี้ยทบต้น | $$A = P(1 + r)^t$$ |

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I | Interest earned |
| P | Principal (starting amount) |
| r | Interest rate (per year) |
| t | Time (years) |

## 4 | Taxation (ภาษี)

| Tax Type | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Income tax** | ภาษีเงินได้ | Tax on earnings |
| **VAT** | ภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม | 7% on goods/services |
| **Property tax** | ภาษีที่ดินและสิ่งปลูกสร้าง | Tax on property |
| **Excise tax** | ภาษีสรรพสามิต | Tax on specific goods (alcohol, tobacco) |

---

## 5 | Debt and Credit

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Debt** | หนี้สิน | Money owed |
| **Credit** | เครดิต | Ability to borrow |
| **Loan** | เงินกู้ | Borrowed money |
| **Collateral** | หลักประกัน | Asset pledged for loan |
| **Debt trap** | กับดักหนี้ | When debt grows faster than ability to pay |

## 6 | Financial Planning

| Step | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Set goals** | ตั้งเป้าหมาย | Short-term, long-term financial goals |
| **Track spending** | บันทึกรายรับรายจ่าย | Know where money goes |
| **Create budget** | จัดทำงบประมาณ | Plan for income and expenses |
| **Build emergency fund** | เงินฉุกเฉิน | 3-6 months of expenses saved |
| **Invest wisely** | ลงทุนอย่างมีสติ | Start early, diversify |

## 8 | Real-World Examples

- **Student savings** — Saving 10% of allowance builds good habits
- **Bank interest** — 10,000 ฿ at 2% for 1 year = 200 ฿ interest
- **VAT** — Buying a 100 ฿ item actually costs 107 ฿ with 7% VAT
- **Debt trap** — Credit card debt at 20% interest doubles in ~3.5 years
