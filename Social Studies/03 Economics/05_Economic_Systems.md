# Economic Systems — ระบบเศรษฐกิจ

> *"The market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent."* — John Maynard Keynes

Economic Systems examines how different societies organize production, distribution, and consumption. Students learn to compare market, command, and mixed economies, with Thailand as a case study.

## 2 | Types of Economic Systems

| System | Thai | Decision Maker | Ownership | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Market (Capitalist)** | ทุนนิยม / ตลาดเสรี | Individuals/businesses | Private | USA, Singapore |
| **Command (Socialist)** | สังคมนิยม / บัญชาการ | Government | State | Cuba, North Korea |
| **Mixed** | เศรษฐกิจผสม | Both government and private | Mixed | Thailand, China |
| **Traditional** | เศรษฐกิจแบบดั้งเดิม | Custom, tradition | Communal | Indigenous communities |

## 3 | Market Economy (เศรษฐกิจตลาด)

| Feature | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Private property** | ทรัพย์สินส่วนบุคคล | Individuals own resources |
| **Freedom of choice** | เสรีภาพในการเลือก | Consumers and producers choose |
| **Profit motive** | แรงจูงใจกำไร | Businesses seek profit |
| **Competition** | การแข่งขัน | Multiple sellers compete |
| **Price mechanism** | กลไกราคา | Supply/demand determine prices |
| **Limited government** | รัฐบาลจำกัด | Government protects property rights |

### Advantages and Disadvantages

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Efficiency through competition | Income inequality |
| Innovation and growth | Market failures |
| Consumer choice | Monopolies can form |
| Responsive to demand | Public goods underprovided |

---

## 4 | Command Economy (เศรษฐกิจแบบบัญชาการ)

| Feature | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **State ownership** | รัฐเป็นเจ้าของ | Government owns resources |
| **Central planning** | การวางแผนจากส่วนกลาง | Government sets production goals |
| **Price controls** | การควบคุมราคา | Government sets prices |
| **No profit motive** | ไม่มีแรงจูงใจกำไร | Production for need, not profit |
| **Full employment** | การจ้างงานเต็มที่ | Government provides jobs |

### Advantages and Disadvantages

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Reduced inequality | Inefficiency, waste |
| Full employment | Lack of innovation |
| Basic needs met | Shortages, surpluses |
| No monopolies | Bureaucracy, corruption |

---

## 5 | Mixed Economy (เศรษฐกิจผสม)

| Feature | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Private and public sectors** | ภาคเอกชนและภาครัฐ | Both coexist |
| **Market with regulation** | ตลาดกับการกำกับ | Government intervenes |
| **Welfare programs** | โครงการสวัสดิการ | Social safety nets |
| **Public goods** | สาธารณสมบัติ | Government provides infrastructure |

### Thailand's Mixed Economy

| Sector | Role | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Private** | 70% of GDP | Manufacturing, services, agriculture |
| **Public** | 30% of GDP | State enterprises, infrastructure, education |
| **Government role** | Regulation, welfare, infrastructure | Minimum wage, universal healthcare, roads |

---

## 6 | Economic Decision-Making

| Question | Market Economy | Command Economy | Mixed Economy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What to produce?** | Consumer demand | Central plan | Both |
| **How to produce?** | Profit-maximizing methods | Government directive | Both |
| **For whom?** | Those who can pay | Equal distribution | Both + welfare |

---

## 7 | Role of Government in Mixed Economy

| Function | Thai | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Regulation** | การกำกับดูแล | Protect consumers, environment | Food safety, pollution control |
| **Public goods** | สาธารณสมบัติ | Provide infrastructure | Roads, schools, hospitals |
| **Redistribution** | การกระจายรายได้ | Reduce inequality | Progressive taxes, welfare |
| **Stabilization** | การรักษาเสถียรภาพ | Control inflation, unemployment | Monetary and fiscal policy |
| **Competition** | ส่งเสริมการแข่งขัน | Prevent monopolies | Antitrust laws |

## 10 | Real-World Examples

- **Thailand** — Mixed economy: private businesses + government healthcare (30-baht scheme)
- **Singapore** — Market economy with strong government planning (HDB housing)
- **China** — "Socialism with Chinese characteristics" — state capitalism
- **Nordic countries** — Market economy with extensive welfare (Sweden, Norway)
