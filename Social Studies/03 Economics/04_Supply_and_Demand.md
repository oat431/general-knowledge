# Supply and Demand — อุปสงค์และอุปทาน

> *"Price is what you pay. Value is what you get."* — Warren Buffett

Supply and Demand is the foundation of market economics. Students learn how prices are determined through the interaction of buyers and sellers, and how markets reach equilibrium.

## 2 | Demand (อุปสงค์)

| Concept | Thai | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | อุปสงค์ | Quantity consumers want to buy at each price |
| **Law of Demand** | กฎของอุปสงค์ | As price ↑, quantity demanded ↓ |
| **Demand curve** | เส้นอุปสงค์ | Downward-sloping line showing price-quantity relationship |
| **Demand schedule** | ตารางอุปสงค์ | Table showing quantity at each price |

### Demand Determinants

| Factor | Thai | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Income** | รายได้ | Higher income → higher demand (normal goods) |
| **Tastes** | รสนิยม | Fashion, trends, advertising |
| **Price of related goods** | ราคาสินค้าที่เกี่ยวข้อง | Substitutes and complements |
| **Expectations** | ความคาดหมาย | Future price changes |
| **Number of buyers** | จำนวนผู้ซื้อ | More buyers → higher demand |

---

## 3 | Supply (อุปทาน)

| Concept | Thai | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Supply** | อุปทาน | Quantity producers want to sell at each price |
| **Law of Supply** | กฎของอุปทาน | As price ↑, quantity supplied ↑ |
| **Supply curve** | เส้นอุปทาน | Upward-sloping line showing price-quantity relationship |

### Supply Determinants

| Factor | Thai | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Input prices** | ราคาปัจจัยการผลิต | Higher costs → lower supply |
| **Technology** | เทคโนโลยี | Better tech → higher supply |
| **Expectations** | ความคาดหมาย | Future price changes |
| **Number of sellers** | จำนวนผู้ขาย | More sellers → higher supply |
| **Government policy** | นโยบายรัฐบาล | Taxes, subsidies, regulations |

---

## 4 | Market Equilibrium (ดุลยภาพตลาด)

| Concept | Thai | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Equilibrium** | ดุลยภาพ | Price where quantity demanded = quantity supplied |
| **Equilibrium price** | ราคาดุลยภาพ | Price that clears the market |
| **Equilibrium quantity** | จำนวนดุลยภาพ | Quantity bought/sold at equilibrium |
| **Surplus** | อุปทานส่วนเกิน | Quantity supplied > quantity demanded (price too high) |
| **Shortage** | อุปสงค์ส่วนเกิน | Quantity demanded > quantity supplied (price too low) |

### Price Adjustment

| Situation | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **Surplus** | Sellers lower price to sell excess inventory |
| **Shortage** | Buyers compete, driving price up |
| **Equilibrium** | No pressure to change price |

---

## 5 | Price Elasticity (ความยืดหยุ่นของราคา)

| Type | Thai | Formula | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price elasticity of demand** | ความยืดหยุ่นของอุปสงค์ | $$E_d = \frac{\%\Delta Q_d}{\%\Delta P}$$ | How sensitive demand is to price |
| **Price elasticity of supply** | ความยืดหยุ่นของอุปทาน | $$E_s = \frac{\%\Delta Q_s}{\%\Delta P}$$ | How sensitive supply is to price |

### Elasticity Categories

| Category | Value | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Perfectly inelastic** | E = 0 | Quantity doesn't change | Life-saving medicine |
| **Inelastic** | 0 < E < 1 | Quantity changes less than price | Rice, gasoline |
| **Unit elastic** | E = 1 | Quantity changes same as price | — |
| **Elastic** | E > 1 | Quantity changes more than price | Luxury goods |
| **Perfectly elastic** | E = ∞ | Any price change → zero demand | Perfect substitutes |

## 6 | Market Structures (โครงสร้างตลาด)

| Structure | Thai | Characteristics | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Perfect competition** | การแข่งขันสมบูรณ์ | Many sellers, identical products | Agricultural markets |
| **Monopolistic competition** | การแข่งขันแบบกึ่งผูกขาด | Many sellers, differentiated products | Restaurants, clothing |
| **Oligopoly** | ผู้ขายน้อยราย | Few large sellers | Airlines, telecom |
| **Monopoly** | การผูกขาด | One seller | Utilities, patents |

---

## 7 | Price Controls (การควบคุมราคา)

| Policy | Thai | Effect | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price ceiling** | เพดานราคา | Maximum legal price → shortage | Rent control |
| **Price floor** | ราคาขั้นต่ำ | Minimum legal price → surplus | Minimum wage |

---

## 8 | Market Failures (ความล้มเหลวของตลาด)

| Type | Thai | Description | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Externalities** | ผลกระทบภายนอก | Costs/benefits to third parties | Taxes, regulations |
| **Public goods** | สาธารณสมบัติ | Non-excludable, non-rival | Government provision |
| **Asymmetric information** | ข้อมูลไม่สมมาตร | One party has more information | Regulations, warranties |
| **Market power** | อำนาจตลาด | Monopolies, oligopolies | Antitrust laws |

## 10 | Real-World Examples

- **Rice price** — Government sets minimum price to protect farmers (price floor)
- **Gasoline** — Inelastic demand: people still buy even when price rises
- **Smartphones** — Oligopoly: Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi dominate
- **Street food** — Perfect competition: many sellers, similar products
