# ASEAN Economics — เศรษฐศาสตร์อาเซียน

> *"ASEAN is not just a geographical concept. It is a shared destiny."* — Lee Hsien Loong

ASEAN Economics examines economic cooperation among Southeast Asian nations. Students learn about the ASEAN Economic Community (AEC), regional trade agreements, and Thailand's economic role in ASEAN.

## 2 | ASEAN Economic Community (AEC) — ประชาคมเศรษฐกิจอาเซียน

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| **Established** | December 31, 2015 |
| **Goal** | Single market and production base |
| **Members** | 10 ASEAN countries |
| **Population** | 680 million people |
| **Combined GDP** | $3.6 trillion USD (2023) |

### AEC Four Pillars

| Pillar | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Single market** | ตลาดเดียว | Free flow of goods, services, investment, skilled labor |
| **Competitive region** | ภูมิภาคที่มีการแข่งขัน | Fair competition, consumer protection, IPR |
| **Equitable development** | การพัฒนาที่เท่าเทียม | Reduce development gaps |
| **Global integration** | การบูรณาการสู่โลก | ASEAN as player in global economy |

## 4 | ASEAN Free Trade Area (AFTA) — เขตการค้าเสรีอาเซียน

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| **Established** | 1992 |
| **Goal** | Eliminate tariffs among ASEAN members |
| **Achievement** | 99% of products tariff-free |
| **Mechanism** | Common Effective Preferential Tariff (CEPT) |

### Tariff Elimination Timeline

| Year | Achievement |
|---|---|
| 1992 | AFTA established |
| 2003 | 6 original members: 0-5% tariffs |
| 2010 | 6 original members: 0% tariffs |
| 2015 | All 10 members: 0% tariffs |
| 2018 | 98.6% of products tariff-free |

## 6 | ASEAN+3 and RCEP (อาเซียน+3 และ RCEP)

### ASEAN+3

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| **Members** | ASEAN + China, Japan, South Korea |
| **Established** | 1997 (after Asian Financial Crisis) |
| **Focus** | Financial cooperation, trade, investment |
| **Key initiative** | Chiang Mai Initiative (currency swap arrangement) |

### RCEP (Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership)

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| **Members** | ASEAN + China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand |
| **Signed** | November 2020 |
| **Effective** | January 2022 |
| **Population** | 2.3 billion (30% of world) |
| **GDP** | $28.5 trillion (30% of world) |
| **Coverage** | Tariffs, rules of origin, e-commerce, IP |

### RCEP Benefits for Thailand

| Sector | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Agriculture** | Reduced tariffs on rice, rubber, fruits |
| **Automotive** | Lower tariffs on parts and vehicles |
| **Electronics** | Simplified rules of origin |
| **Services** | Market access for tourism, healthcare |
| **E-commerce** | Harmonized digital trade rules |

## 7 | Challenges of ASEAN Economic Integration (ความท้าทายของการบูรณาการเศรษฐกิจอาเซียน)

| Challenge | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Development gap** | ช่องว่างการพัฒนา | Singapore vs Myanmar income gap |
| **Non-tariff barriers** | อุปสรรคที่ไม่ใช่ภาษี | Standards, regulations, bureaucracy |
| **Infrastructure** | โครงสร้างพื้นฐาน | Poor connectivity in CLMV countries |
| **Skilled labor mobility** | การเคลื่อนย้ายแรงงานฝีมือ | Mutual recognition agreements limited |
| **Competition from China** | การแข่งขันจากจีน | Cheaper Chinese products |
| **Political differences** | ความแตกต่างทางการเมือง | Democracy vs authoritarianism |

### Development Gap

| Indicator | Singapore | Myanmar | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GDP per capita** | $65,000 | $1,100 | 59:1 |
| **Life expectancy** | 84 years | 68 years | 1.2:1 |
| **Literacy rate** | 97% | 76% | 1.3:1 |

## 10 | Real-World Examples

- **AEC 2015** — ASEAN became single market, but integration still incomplete
- **RCEP 2022** — World's largest trade bloc, includes China
- **Thailand's automotive industry** — Exports vehicles to ASEAN with 0% tariffs
- **Grab** — Singapore-based ride-hailing expanded across ASEAN
- **Cross-border e-commerce** — Lazada, Shopee operate in all ASEAN countries
