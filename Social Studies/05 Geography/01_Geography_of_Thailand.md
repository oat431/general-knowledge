# Geography of Thailand — ภูมิศาสตร์ไทย

> *"Geography is the study of the Earth's surface — its physical features, climate, resources, and how humans interact with their environment."*

Geography of Thailand covers the physical and human geography of Thailand and the world. Students learn map skills, understand climate and weather, explore natural resources, and study how people live in different environments.

## 3 | Thailand's Physical Features

### Six Regions

| Region | Thai | Key Features | Capital |
|---|---|---|---|
| **North** | ภาคเหนือ | Mountains, cool climate, Lanna culture | Chiang Mai |
| **Northeast (Isan)** | ภาคตะวันออกเฉียงเหนือ | Khorat Plateau, dry climate, Khmer ruins | Nakhon Ratchasima |
| **Central** | ภาคเหนือ | Chao Phraya River basin, rice fields, Bangkok | Bangkok |
| **East** | ภาคตะวันออก | Coastal, fruit orchards, tourism | Chonburi |
| **West** | ภาคตะวันตก | Mountains, forests, dams | Kanchanaburi |
| **South** | ภาคใต้ | Peninsula, rubber, tin, tourism | Songkhla |

## 4 | Climate (ภูมิอากาศ)

### Thailand's Three Seasons

| Season | Thai | Months | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hot** | ฤดูร้อน | March–May | 30–40°C, dry |
| **Rainy** | ฤดูฝน | June–October | Monsoon, flooding |
| **Cool** | ฤดูหนาว | November–February | 15–25°C, dry |

### Climate Factors

| Factor | Thai | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Latitude** | ละติจูด | Distance from equator |
| **Altitude** | ระดับความสูง | Higher = cooler |
| **Ocean currents** | กระแสน้ำในมหาสมุทร | Warm/cold currents affect climate |
| **Monsoon** | มรสุม | Seasonal winds bring rain |

## 6 | Population Geography (ภูมิศาสตร์ประชากร)

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Population density** | ความหนาแน่นประชากร | People per km² |

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| Total population | ~70 million |
| Bangkok metro | ~15 million |

## 7 | Natural Disasters (ภัยธรรมชาติ)

| Disaster | Thai | Cause | Thailand Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flood** | อุทกภัย | Heavy rain, river overflow | High (annual) |
| **Drought** | ภัยแล้ง | Insufficient rain | Medium |
| **Earthquake** | แผ่นดินไหว | Tectonic movement | Low (but Chiang Mai at risk) |
| **Tsunami** | สึนามิ | Undersea earthquake | Low (Andaman coast) |
| **Landslide** | ดินถล่ม | Heavy rain on slopes | Medium (mountains) |

## 9 | Real-World Examples

- **Chao Phraya River** — Central Thailand's lifeline; irrigation, transportation, flooding
- **2011 Thailand Floods** — Worst in 50 years; affected Bangkok and industrial estates
- **2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami** — Devastated Phuket and Andaman coast
- **Bangkok sinking** — City sinking ~2cm/year due to groundwater extraction
