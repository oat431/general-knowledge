# Geography — ภูมิศาสตร์

> **Strand:** สาระที่ 5 | **Concept Areas:** ~14 | **Duration:** 12 years (ป.1–ม.6)
> **Focus:** Physical geography, human geography, maps/GIS, environment, natural disasters

## Overview

Geography develops spatial literacy from simple map reading through advanced geographic analysis using GIS and remote sensing. Students learn physical geography processes, human-environment interactions, and environmental stewardship.
