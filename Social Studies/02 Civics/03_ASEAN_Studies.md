# ASEAN Studies — การศึกษาเกี่ยวกับอาเซียน

> *"ASEAN connects Thailand to its neighbors — understanding this regional community is essential for Thai citizens in the 21st century."*

ASEAN Studies teaches students about the Association of Southeast Asian Nations — its history, member states, three pillars, and how it affects Thailand. Students learn about regional cooperation, cultural diversity, and economic integration.

## 3 | ASEAN Three Pillars

| Pillar | Thai | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Political-Security** | การเมืองและความมั่นคง | Peace, stability, rule of law |
| **Economic** | เศรษฐกิจ | Trade, investment, single market |
| **Socio-Cultural** | สังคมและวัฒนธรรม | Education, health, culture, environment |

---

## 4 | ASEAN Economic Community (AEC)

| Aspect | Thai | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Free trade** | การค้าเสรี | Reduced tariffs between members |
| **Free movement of labor** | การเคลื่อนย้ายแรงงาน | Skilled workers can work in other ASEAN countries |
| **Investment** | การลงทุน | Easier cross-border investment |
| **Single market** | ตลาดเดียว | 600+ million consumers |

## 5 | Benefits and Challenges

| Benefits | Challenges |
|---|---|
| Economic growth | Income inequality |
| Cultural exchange | Cultural homogenization |
| Regional security | Territorial disputes |
| Tourism | Environmental pressure |
| Education opportunities | Brain drain |
