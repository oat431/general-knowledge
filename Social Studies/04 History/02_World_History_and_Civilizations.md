# World History and Civilizations — ประวัติศาสตร์โลกและอารยธรรม

> *"Every civilization has contributed something unique to human progress — understanding world history helps us see our place in the larger human story."*

World History covers major civilizations and events that shaped the modern world. From ancient Mesopotamia to the World Wars, students learn how human societies developed, interacted, and transformed over millennia.

## 4 | World Wars

### World War I (สงครามโลกครั้งที่ 1, 2457-2461)

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Cause** | Nationalism, alliances, assassination |
| **Allies** | France, UK, Russia, USA (later) |
| **Central Powers** | Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire |
| **Result** | Treaty of Versailles, League of Nations |

### World War II (สงครามโลกครั้งที่ 2, 2482-2488)

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Cause** | Rise of fascism, Treaty of Versailles failures |
| **Axis** | Germany, Japan, Italy |
| **Allies** | UK, USA, USSR, France, China |
| **Key Events** | Holocaust, atomic bombs, UN formation |
| **Thailand** | Allied with Japan, then joined Allies |

---

## 5 | Cold War (สงครามเย็น, 2492-2534)

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Superpowers** | USA (capitalism) vs USSR (communism) |
| **Key Events** | Berlin Wall, Cuban Missile Crisis, Vietnam War |
| **Thailand** | US ally, anti-communist, 1973/1976 student movements |
| **End** | Fall of Berlin Wall (1989), USSR dissolution (1991) |
