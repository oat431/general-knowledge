# Historical Method — วิธีการทางประวัติศาสตร์

> *"History is not just memorizing dates — it is the systematic study of the past through evidence, interpretation, and critical thinking."*

Historical Method teaches students how historians work — how they find, evaluate, and interpret evidence to construct narratives about the past. Students learn to think critically about sources, understand causation, and recognize that history is always interpreted.

## 2 | What is History?

| Aspect | Thai | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **History** | ประวัติศาสตร์ | The study of past events and their meaning |
| **Chronicle** | พงศาวดาร | Official record of events |
| **Historiography** | ประวัติศาสตร์นิพนธ์ | How history is written and interpreted |

---

## 3 | Types of Historical Evidence

| Type | Thai | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary source** | แหล่งปฐมภูมิ | First-hand account from the time | Letters, diaries, photos, artifacts, inscriptions |
| **Secondary source** | แหล่งทุติยภูมi | Later interpretation of primary sources | Textbooks, biographies, documentaries |
| **Oral tradition** | ประเพณีมุขปาฐะ | Stories passed down by word of mouth | Folk tales, legends, interviews |
| **Archaeological evidence** | หลักฐานทางโบราณคดี | Physical remains from the past | Pottery, tools, buildings, bones |

---

## 4 | Source Evaluation

| Question | Thai | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Who created it?** | ใครสร้าง? | Author's identity and bias |
| **When was it created?** | สร้างเมื่อไหร่? | Time period, contemporary or later? |
| **Why was it created?** | ทำไมถึงสร้าง? | Purpose, propaganda, record-keeping? |
| **What is it about?** | เกี่ยวกับอะไร? | Content and context |
| **How reliable is it?** | เชื่อถือได้แค่ไหน? | Corroboration with other sources |

## 5 | Historical Thinking Skills

| Skill | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Chronology** | ลำดับเวลา | Putting events in order |
| **Causation** | เหตุและผล | Why events happened, what resulted |
| **Comparison** | การเปรียบเทียบ | Similarities and differences |
| **Interpretation** | การตีความ | Multiple perspectives on events |
| **Evidence** | หลักฐาน | Using sources to support claims |
