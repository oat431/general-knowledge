# Important Monarchs — พระมหากษัตริย์ที่สำคัญ

> *"A great king is not measured by the territory he conquers, but by the welfare of the people he serves."*

This note provides detailed profiles of Thailand's most significant monarchs across all periods. Students learn about each king's contributions, historical context, and lasting impact on Thai civilization.

## 2 | The "Great" Kings of Thailand (มหาราช)

Thailand officially recognizes **seven** monarchs with the title **"มหาราช" (the Great)**:

| King | Thai | Period | Title |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ramkhamhaeng** | พ่อขุนรามคำแหงมหาราช | Sukhothai | The Great |
| **Naresuan** | สมเด็จพระนเรศวรมหาราช | Ayutthaya | The Great |
| **Narai** | สมเด็จพระนารายณ์มหาราช | Ayutthaya | The Great |
| **Taksin** | สมเด็จพระเจ้าตากสินมหาราช | Thonburi | The Great |
| **Rama I** | พระบาทสมเด็จพระพุทธยอดฟ้าจุฬาโลกมหาราช | Rattanakosin | The Great |
| **Rama V (Chulalongkorn)** | พระบาทสมเด็จพระจุลจอมเกล้าเจ้าอยู่หัวมหาราช | Rattanakosin | The Great |
| **Rama IX (Bhumibol)** | พระบาทสมเด็จพระบรมชาติกาล | Rattanakosin | The Great |

## 4 | King Naresuan the Great (สมเด็จพระนเรศวรมหาราช)

See [[06_Ayutthaya_Period]] for full details.

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Reign** | พ.ศ. 2133–2148 (1590–1605 CE) |
| **Kingdom** | Ayutthaya |
| **Why "Great"** | Declared independence from Burma, warrior king |

**Top contributions:** Declaration of independence (1584), Elephant Battle at Don Chedi (1592), military campaigns against Burma, reunified territory.

### Naresuan's Childhood

| Event | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Hostage in Burma** | เชลยประกัน | Sent to Hongsawadi at age 9 |
| **Education** | การศึกษา | Learned Burmese, Mon, military strategy |
| **Buddhist ordination** | อุปสมบท | Ordained as monk briefly |
| **Return to Ayutthaya** | เสด็จกลับ | Returned at age 16 |

## 7 | King Rama I the Great (พระบาทสมเด็จพระพุทธยอดฟ้าจุฬาโลก)

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Personal name** | Thong Duang (ทองด้วง) |
| **Reign** | พ.ศ. 2325–2352 (1782–1809 CE) |
| **Kingdom** | Rattanakosin |
| **Why "Great"** | Founded Bangkok, reestablished Thai state |

### Key Achievements

| Achievement | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Founded Bangkok** | สถาปนากรุงรัตนโกสินทร์ | Moved capital from Thonburi to Bangkok |
| **Grand Palace** | พระบรมมหาราชวัง | Built royal palace and Wat Phra Kaew |
| **Law codification** | กฎหมายตราสามดวง | Compiled and updated laws (1805) |
| **Literary revival** | ฟื้นฟูวรรณคดี | Sponsored Ramakien, literature restoration |
| **Buddhist council** | สังคายนาพระไตรปิฎก | Convened to revise Buddhist canon |

## 8 | King Chulalongkorn the Great / Rama V (พระบาทสมเด็จพระจุลจอมเกล้าเจ้าอยู่หัว)

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Born** | September 20, พ.ศ. 2396 (September 20, 1853) |
| **Reign** | พ.ศ. 2411–2453 (1868–1919 CE) — 42 years |
| **Kingdom** | Rattanakosin |
| **Father** | King Rama IV (Mongkut) |
| **Why "Great"** | Modernized Thailand, preserved independence |

### Why Rama V is Revered

| Reason | Description |
|---|---|
| **Preserved independence** | Navigated colonial powers (Britain, France) |
| **Abolished slavery** | Ended 400+ year practice (1874–1911) |
| **Modernized government** | Created modern bureaucratic system |
| **Education** | Established modern schools, sent students abroad |
| **Infrastructure** | Railways, hospitals, telegraph |
| **"Phra Piya Maharat"** | "The Great Beloved King" (พระปิยมหาราช) |

### Abolition of Slavery

| Step | Year | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Act of 2417** | 1874 | Freed slaves born after 1868 at age 21 |
| **Act of 2442** | 1899 | Reduced debt bondage |
| **Act of 2448** | 1905 | Abolished corvée labor (ไพร่ส่วย) |
| **Full abolition** | 2454 (1911) | All remaining slaves freed |

### How Rama V Avoided Colonization

| Strategy | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Diplomatic balancing** | การทูต | Played Britain and France against each other |
| **Territorial concessions** | สละดินแดน | Gave up peripheral territories to satisfy powers |
| **Modernization** | การปฏิรูป | Adopted Western institutions to show "civilized" status |
| **Royal visits** | เสด็จประพาส | Visited Europe (1897, 1907) to build relations |

## 10 | Comparative Assessment (ม.4-6)

### Monarchs by Achievement Type

| Type | Kings | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Founders** | Rama I, Ramkhamhaeng | Established kingdoms, created institutions |
| **Warriors** | Naresuan, Taksin | Military leadership, national survival |
| **Diplomats** | Narai, Rama V | International relations, preserved independence |
| **Reformers** | Rama V, Rama IV | Modernization, social change |
| **Developers** | Rama IX | Rural development, sustainable living |

### Historical Source Quality by King

| King | Sources Available | Source Quality |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramkhamhaeng** | Stone inscription (debated) | Primary but debated |
| **Naresuan** | Royal chronicles, foreign accounts | Good |
| **Narai** | Dutch, French records | Excellent (foreign sources) |
| **Taksin** | Chronicles (written by successors) | Biased against him |
| **Rama V** | Royal letters, documents | Excellent |
| **Rama IX** | Thousands of documents, photos | Excellent |
