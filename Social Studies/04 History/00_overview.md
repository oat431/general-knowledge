# History — ประวัติศาสตร์

> **Strand:** สาระที่ 4 | **Concept Areas:** ~15 | **Duration:** 12 years (ป.1–ม.6)
> **Focus:** Thai history periods, world history, historical method, important figures, local history

## Overview

History builds chronological understanding from personal and local history through Thai national history to world civilizations. Students learn to think like historians — evaluating sources, understanding causation, recognizing interpretation, and connecting past to present.
