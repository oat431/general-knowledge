# Visual Arts — ทัศนศิลป์

> **Strand:** สาระที่ 1 | **Concept Areas:** 8 | **Duration:** 12 years (ป.1–ม.6)
> **Focus:** line (เส้น), shape (รูปร่าง), color (สี), and composition (องค์ประกอบ)

## Overview

Visual Arts (ทัศนศิลป์) is the first strand of the Thai arts curriculum, covering everything children make and see in the visual world: drawing, painting, sculpture, design, printmaking, appreciation, Thai traditional art, and art history. Under Standards ศ 1.1 and ศ 1.2, students move from playful mark-making in primary school to independent creative work and informed criticism in upper secondary. This folder gives Thai parents a road map of what their child learns in each grade band and how to support it at home.
