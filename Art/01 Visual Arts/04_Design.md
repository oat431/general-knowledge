# Design — การออกแบบ

Design (การออกแบบ) is art with a job: arranging visual elements (ทัศนธาตุ) so that messages, products, and spaces work well and look good. Thai students learn the elements in primary school and the principles of design (หลักการออกแบบ) from lower secondary onward.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| ป.1–3 | Simple patterns (ลวดลาย), arranging shapes and colors in repeating designs |
| ป.4–6 | The seven visual elements (ทัศนธาตุ): dot, line, color, shape, form, value, texture |
| ม.1–3 | Principles of design (หลักการออกแบบ): balance, rhythm, proportion, harmony, contrast, emphasis |
| ม.4–6 | Graphic design (การออกแบบกราฟิก), product design (การออกแบบผลิตภัณฑ์), designing for daily life and careers |

## 2 | The Visual Elements (ทัศนธาตุ)

| Element | Thai | Example in daily life |
|---|---|---|
| Dot | จุด | The smallest mark; rows of dots become lines |
| Line | เส้น | Direction and movement in a logo or road marking |
| Color | สี | Mood and attention in packaging |
| Shape | รูปร่าง | Flat 2D areas, such as the outline of a leaf |
| Form | รูปทรง | 3D volume, such as a bottle or a chair |
| Value | น้ำหนัก | Light and dark in a photograph |
| Texture | พื้นผิว | Surface quality, such as rough cloth or smooth plastic |

## 3 | Principles of Design (หลักการออกแบบ)

| Principle | Thai | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Balance | ความสมดุล | Visual weight arranged evenly, symmetrical or asymmetrical |
| Rhythm | จังหวะ | Repeating elements that create movement |
| Proportion | สัดส่วน | Size relationships between parts |
| Harmony | ความกลมกลืน | Elements that fit together comfortably |
| Contrast | ความแตกต่าง | Opposites that create interest |
| Emphasis | ความเด่น | A focal point that draws the eye first |

## 4 | Design in Daily Life

Design is everywhere: food packaging (บรรจุภัณฑ์), posters (โปสเตอร์), book covers, school uniforms, and phone apps. Parents can point out good and bad design on a shopping trip: which package is easiest to read, which poster grabs the eye first, and why.

Thai craft design (การออกแบบผลิตภัณฑ์หัตถกรรม) also connects to local wisdom: patterned Thai silk (ผ้าไหมไทย), celadon pottery (เครื่องเคลือบเซลาดอน), and woven baskets (ตะกร้าสาน). In upper secondary, students plan real projects: designing a logo for a school event, laying out a poster, or sketching a product that solves a small problem at home.
