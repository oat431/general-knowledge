# Drawing — การวาดภาพ

Drawing (การวาดภาพ) is the first art skill every child meets: making marks with lines (เส้น) to show what they see and imagine. It is the foundation of Standard ศ 1.1, and progress runs from scribbled lines in Grade 1 to a personal portfolio of figure drawing (การวาดคน) in Grade 12.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| ป.1–3 | Basic lines (เส้น), shapes (รูปร่าง), free coloring, drawing from imagination and from nature |
| ป.4–6 | Shading (แสงเงา), perspective basics (มุมมอง), observation drawing of real objects |
| ม.1–3 | Figure drawing (การวาดคน), composition (องค์ประกอบ), still life (หุ่นนิ่ง), proportion (สัดส่วน) |
| ม.4–6 | Advanced techniques, personal style, portfolio development for further study and careers |

## 2 | Lines and Shapes: The Building Blocks

Children start with the vocabulary of marks. Every drawing is built from these visual elements (ทัศนธาตุ):

| Element | Thai | What students learn |
|---|---|---|
| Line | เส้น | Straight, curved, zigzag, thick, thin, and expressive lines |
| Shape | รูปร่าง | Flat 2D shapes: circle, square, triangle, and organic shapes |
| Form | รูปทรง | 3D form: cube, sphere, cylinder, and how form sits in space |
| Texture | พื้นผิว | How surfaces feel or look: rough, smooth, soft, hard |
| Value | น้ำหนัก | Light and dark, leading into shading (แสงเงา) |

In primary school, children draw familiar things: family, animals, flowers, and school scenes. Parents can help by keeping paper and pencils available and by asking "what did you draw?" (วาดอะไร) instead of judging the result. Every drawing is a step toward confidence.

## 3 | Shading and Perspective

From Grade 4 onward, drawing becomes observational. Students learn to show depth and light:

| Skill | Thai | Typical exercise |
|---|---|---|
| Shading | แสงเงา | Showing light and shadow with pencil pressure and hatching |
| Hatching | การแรเงา | Shading a sphere with parallel or crossed lines |
| Perspective | มุมมอง | Making distant objects smaller, one-point and two-point perspective |
| One-point perspective | มุมมองแบบจุดเดียว | Drawing a road or railway receding to one point |
| Still life | หุ่นนิ่ง | Arranging fruit and cloth, drawing exactly what is seen |
| Sketching | การร่างภาพ | Quick pencil sketches from a live model or scene |

Observation drawing (การวาดจากของจริง) trains the eye: children compare sizes so parts relate correctly to the whole, which is proportion (สัดส่วน).

## 4 | Figure Drawing and the Senior Portfolio

In lower secondary (ม.1–3), students draw people: head and body proportions, facial features, and action poses. In upper secondary (ม.4–6), they choose a direction: realistic drawing, cartoon and illustration (ภาพประกอบ), or design drawing.

| Level | Focus | Sample output |
|---|---|---|
| ม.1 | Head proportion and facial features | Portrait sketches |
| ม.2 | Full body and action poses | Figure studies |
| ม.3 | Composition (องค์ประกอบ) and background | Complete scene drawings |
| ม.4–6 | Personal style and portfolio | Portfolio (แฟ้มผลงาน) of 10 to 20 finished pieces |

The portfolio is also required for art college entrance, so upper secondary students should keep their best work in one folder and date every piece.
