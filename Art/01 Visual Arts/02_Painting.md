# Painting — การระบายสี

Painting (การระบายสี) is drawing's colorful partner: applying color (สี) with brushes and paint to paper, cloth, or walls. Thai children begin with poster color (สีโปสเตอร์) and watercolor (สีน้ำ), and end with color theory (ทฤษฎีสี) that lets them mix and match any hue with confidence.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| ป.1–3 | Primary colors (สีแม่สี), free painting, painting familiar subjects with poster color |
| ป.4–6 | Mixing secondary and tertiary colors (สีขั้นที่ 2 และ 3), color wheel (วงล้อสี), watercolor basics |
| ม.1–3 | Color theory, warm and cool colors (สีวรรณะร้อนและเย็น), complementary colors (สีคู่ตรงข้าม), mood in painting |
| ม.4–6 | Mixed media, acrylic (สีอะคริลิก) and oil paint (สีน้ำมัน), personal expression, exhibition work |

## 2 | Color Theory

Color theory (ทฤษฎีสี) is the science of how colors are made, mixed, and matched. It is the core knowledge of painting from Grade 4 upward:

| Concept | Thai | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Primary colors | สีแม่สี | Red, yellow, blue: cannot be mixed from other colors |
| Secondary colors | สีขั้นที่ 2 | Orange, green, purple: made by mixing two primaries |
| Tertiary colors | สีขั้นที่ 3 | Six colors made by mixing a primary with a secondary |
| Color wheel | วงล้อสี | A circle arranging colors in rainbow order |
| Complementary colors | สีคู่ตรงข้าม | Opposite pairs on the wheel, such as red and green |
| Warm colors | สีวรรณะร้อน | Red, orange, yellow: feel hot, close, and energetic |
| Cool colors | สีวรรณะเย็น | Blue, green, purple: feel cold, distant, and calm |

A classic classroom activity is mixing the three primary colors to discover the whole wheel. Parents can try this at home with poster color and a paper plate: it is cheap, messy, and exactly what the school expects.

## 3 | Painting Media

| Media | Thai | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Watercolor | สีน้ำ | Transparent washes, landscape and flower painting |
| Poster color | สีโปสเตอร์ | Opaque, bright school projects |
| Tempera | สีเทมเพอรา | Egg-based paint used in traditional art |
| Acrylic | สีอะคริลิก | Fast-drying and versatile, good for canvas |
| Oil paint | สีน้ำมัน | Rich blending, used by professional artists |

Students also learn to care for tools: washing brushes (ล้างแปรง) after use, keeping water jars clean, and mixing colors on a palette (จานผสมสี). Good habits make painting enjoyable rather than stressful.

## 4 | Thai Painting Traditions

Thai traditional painting (จิตรกรรมไทย) uses mineral colors (สีจากแร่) and gold leaf (การปิดทอง). Students learn to appreciate the flat, elegant style of temple murals (จิตรกรรมฝาผนัง), with figures drawn in profile and backgrounds filled with Thai patterns (ลายไทย). Modern Thai painters combine these traditions with Western techniques, and students compare the two approaches: realistic depth versus decorative flatness.
