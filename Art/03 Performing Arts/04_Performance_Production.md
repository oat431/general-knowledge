# Performance Production — การจัดการแสดง

Performance production (การจัดการแสดง) is the teamwork behind every school show: who does what, what equipment is needed, and how the show is rehearsed, presented and reviewed. This note gives parents a map of the roles and jobs their child may take on in school performances (การแสดงในโรงเรียน).

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| ป.1–3 | Taking part in class performances, following simple directions, understanding the performance space (เวที) |
| ป.4–6 | Helping with costumes (เครื่องแต่งกาย) and props (อุปกรณ์ประกอบฉาก), performing in front of audiences |
| ม.1–3 | Taking production roles, planning rehearsals (การซ้อม), basic lighting (แสง) and sound (เสียง) |
| ม.4–6 | Leading full productions, evaluating performances (การประเมินการแสดง), managing a complete show from concept to curtain |

## 2 | Production Roles

A school show needs more than performers (ผู้แสดง). The main roles are:

| Role | Thai | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Producer | ผู้จัด | Budget, schedule, permissions, overall organisation |
| Director | ผู้กำกับ | Interprets the story, guides acting, controls the artistic result |
| Performers | ผู้แสดง | Actors, dancers and singers on stage |
| Stage crew | ฝ่ายฉาก | Building and moving scenery (ฉาก) |
| Props master | ฝ่ายอุปกรณ์ | Preparing and managing props (อุปกรณ์ประกอบฉาก) |
| Costume and makeup | ฝ่ายเครื่องแต่งกายและการแต่งหน้า | Dressing and makeup (การแต่งหน้า) for every role |
| Sound and lighting | ฝ่ายแสงเสียง | Music, microphones and light cues (แสง, เสียง) |

## 3 | Stagecraft Elements

The visible magic of a show is built from five elements:

| Element | Thai | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Stage | เวที | The acting space, with entrances, exits and levels |
| Scenery | ฉาก | Painted or built backgrounds that set the place |
| Lighting | แสง | Focuses attention and creates mood |
| Sound and music | เสียง | Music (ดนตรี) and effects that support the action |
| Costume and makeup | เครื่องแต่งกาย, การแต่งหน้า | Shows character, period and rank at a glance |

## 4 | The Production Process

A performance moves through four phases:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Planning] --> B[Rehearsal]
    B --> C[Performance]
    C --> D[Evaluation]
```

- Planning (การวางแผน): choose the piece, assign roles, set the date.
- Rehearsal (การซ้อม): practise scenes, fix timing, run the full show with all elements.
- Performance (การแสดง): present to the audience with discipline and joy.
- Evaluation (การประเมินผล): review what worked and what to improve next time.

## 5 | Audience Etiquette and Evaluation

Parents can support their child by modelling good audience behaviour (มารยาทของผู้ชม):

- Arrive early, switch off phones, and stay for the whole show.
- Applaud (ปรบมือ) at the right moments and thank the performers afterwards.
- School evaluation (การประเมินการแสดง) looks at voice, movement, teamwork and faithfulness to the script, not at perfection.
