# Khon, Lakhon and Puppetry — โขน ละคร และหุ่น

Khon (โขน), lakhon (ละคร) and Thai puppetry (หุ่นไทย) are the heritage performing arts (มรดกภูมิปัญญาทางวัฒนธรรม) of Thailand, telling epic stories such as the Ramakien (รามเกียรติ์) through masked dance, song and carved figures. This note helps parents introduce these treasured forms to their children at home and at cultural events.

## 2 | Khon (โขน)

Khon is Thailand's masked dance-drama, performed to narration and music:

- It is based on the Ramakien (รามเกียรติ์), the Thai version of the Ramayana epic.
- Performers wear masks (หน้ากาก) according to character type: heroes (ตัวพระ), heroines (ตัวนาง), demons (ตัวยักษ์) and monkeys (ตัวลิง).
- Dance, music, costume and mask-making come together in one art form.
- UNESCO inscribed Khon on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2018.

## 3 | Lakhon (ละคร)

Lakhon is unmasked dance-drama with spoken or sung dialogue, in several historical styles:

| Style | Thai | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Chatri | ละครชาตรี | The oldest form, performed with energetic dance and drums, especially in the South |
| Nok | ละครนอก | Outdoor plays with lively action and folk humour |
| Nai | ละครใน | Court plays, refined and slow, performed inside the palace for royal audiences |

Lakhon stories include the Ramakien and other epics such as Unarut (อุณรุท).

## 4 | Thai Puppetry (หุ่นไทย)

Thailand has four great puppet traditions:

| Form | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Rod puppet | หุ่นกระบอก | Small wooden puppets on rods, moved by one puppeteer who also speaks |
| Royal puppet | หุ่นหลวง | Large jointed puppets, up to human size, worked by several puppeteers |
| Southern shadow play | หนังตะลุง | Carved leather figures cast on a screen, with improvisation and song |
| Large shadow play | หนังใหญ่ | Huge leather figures, often performing the Ramakien with many figures at once |

## 5 | Heritage and Preservation (มรดกภูมิปัญญาทางวัฒนธรรม)

These arts are national treasures, and the curriculum teaches children to value and protect them:

- Schools organise excursions (ทัศนศึกษา) to Khon performances and puppet theatres.
- Local masters (ครูภูมิปัญญา) teach mask painting, puppet carving and performance skills.
- Regular performances, such as the Khon shows at the Sala Chalermkrung theatre, keep the forms alive.
- Children's participation, even as audience members, is itself a form of preservation (การอนุรักษ์).
