# Creative Drama — ละครสร้างสรรค์

Creative drama (ละครสร้างสรรค์) uses play, imagination and role-play (บทบาทสมมติ) to build expression, confidence and cooperation in children, without requiring professional acting skill. This note explains how drama grows from simple storytelling (การเล่านิทาน) in the early grades to full scripted productions (การแสดงละคร) in secondary school.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| ป.1–3 | Role-play (บทบาทสมมติ), imitating people and animals, storytelling (การเล่านิทาน) with movement, improvisation (การแสดงสด) in games |
| ป.4–6 | Short scripted scenes, character building, simple puppetry (การเชิดหุ่น), performing for classmates and parents |
| ม.1–3 | Script writing (การเขียนบทละคร), acting technique (การแสดง), directing basics, staging simple plays |
| ม.4–6 | Full productions, original scripts, directing and stage management, evaluating drama with aesthetic criteria |

## 2 | Core Drama Skills

Children build four overlapping skills from primary school onward:

| Skill | Thai | What It Looks Like |
|---|---|---|
| Role-play | บทบาทสมมติ | Becoming a shopkeeper, a mother or a tiger, and reacting as that character would |
| Storytelling | การเล่านิทาน | Retelling tales (นิทาน) with voice, face and gesture |
| Improvisation | การแสดงสด | Making up scenes on the spot, no script and no rehearsal |
| Creative movement | การเคลื่อนไหวสร้างสรรค์ | Moving as wind, water or fire to express ideas without words |

## 3 | From Story to Stage

The path from play to performance is gradual:

1. Children dramatise known tales (นิทาน) such as folk fables and local legends.
2. They learn to build a simple plot: beginning (เริ่มเรื่อง), problem (ปัญหา) and ending (จบเรื่อง).
3. In upper grades they write their own scripts (การเขียนบทละคร) with dialogue (บทพูด) and stage directions.
4. Finally they rehearse and present a complete play (การแสดงละคร) to an audience.

## 4 | Acting Basics (การแสดง)

Acting in school drama rests on three tools that parents can practise at home:

- Voice (น้ำเสียง): loud and soft, fast and slow, matching the emotion of the line.
- Body (ท่าทาง): posture, gesture and movement that show the character's feelings.
- Face (สีหน้า): expressions that make the emotion visible even from the back of the room.

Students also learn to listen and respond (การฟังและการตอบ), because good acting means reacting to the partner on stage.

## 5 | Drama Values in Thai Schools

Creative drama supports the whole curriculum:

- It builds confidence (ความมั่นใจ) in shy children.
- It teaches teamwork (การทำงานเป็นทีม) and responsibility (ความรับผิดชอบ).
- It lets children explore Thai stories, values and manners (มารยาท) by living them for a few minutes on stage.
