# Electricity and Magnetism — ไฟฟ้าและแม่เหล็ก

> **Category:** Advanced Physics (ม.5, ว302)
> **Covering:** Electrostatics, DC Circuits, Magnetism, EM Induction, EM Waves
> **Foundation for:** Electrical engineering, electronics, telecommunications

## Overview

Electricity and Magnetism (E&M) is the physics of **charges and fields**. From Coulomb's law through Maxwell's equations (conceptual), students learn how electric charges create fields, how circuits work, and how changing magnetic fields produce electricity — the principle behind every power plant on Earth.

## Key Formulas

### Electrostatics & Circuits

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Coulomb's law | F = k·q₁q₂/r² |
| Electric field | E = F/q |
| Capacitance | C = Q/V |
| Ohm's law | V = IR |
| Power | P = IV = I²R = V²/R |
| Series resistance | R_eq = R₁ + R₂ + ... |
| Parallel resistance | 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + ... |
| Kirchhoff's current law | ΣI_in = ΣI_out |
| Kirchhoff's voltage law | ΣV_loop = 0 |

### Magnetism & Induction

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Magnetic force on charge | F = qvB sin θ |
| Magnetic force on wire | F = ILB sin θ |
| Faraday's law | ε = −N·ΔΦ/Δt |
| Transformer | V_p/V_s = N_p/N_s |

### Right-Hand Rules

| Rule | For |
|---|---|
| **RHR #1** | Direction of magnetic force on moving charge |
| **RHR #2** | Magnetic field around current-carrying wire |
| **Fleming's Left Hand** | Force on current in magnetic field (motor effect) |
| **Fleming's Right Hand** | Induced current direction (generator effect) |
