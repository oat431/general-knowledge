# Organic and Applied Chemistry — เคมีอินทรีย์และเคมีประยุกต์

> **Category:** Advanced Chemistry (ม.6, ว313)
> **Covering:** Organic Fundamentals, Hydrocarbons, Functional Groups, Polymers, Biochemistry, Qualitative Analysis, Industrial Chemistry

## Overview

Organic chemistry is the chemistry of **carbon-based compounds** — the molecules of life. From hydrocarbons to proteins, students learn how carbon's unique bonding creates infinite molecular diversity. The applied section covers polymers, industrial processes, and biochemistry — chemistry that touches everyday life.
