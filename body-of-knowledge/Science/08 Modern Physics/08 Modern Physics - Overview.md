# Modern Physics — ฟิสิกส์สมัยใหม่

> **Category:** Advanced Physics (ม.6, ว303)
> **Covering:** Special Relativity, Quantum Physics, Atomic Physics, Nuclear Physics, Particle Physics, Astrophysics

## Overview

Modern Physics is where classical intuition breaks down. Einstein's relativity shows that time slows at high speeds. Quantum mechanics reveals that particles are also waves. Nuclear physics unlocks the atom's energy. This is the ม.6 capstone — the most conceptually challenging and philosophically profound part of high school physics.

## Key Formulas

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Time dilation** | Δt = Δt₀/√(1−v²/c²) |
| **Length contraction** | L = L₀√(1−v²/c²) |
| **Mass-energy** | E = mc² |
| **Photon energy** | E = hf = hc/λ |
| **Photoelectric effect** | K_max = hf − W₀ |
| **de Broglie wavelength** | λ = h/p = h/mv |
| **Radioactive decay** | N = N₀(½)^(t/T₁/₂) |
| **Nuclear binding energy** | E = Δm·c² |
