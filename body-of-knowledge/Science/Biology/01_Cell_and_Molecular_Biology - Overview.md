# Cell and Molecular Biology — ชีววิทยาของเซลล์และชีววิทยาระดับโมเลกุล

> **Category:** Advanced Biology (ม.4, ว321)
> **Covering:** Cell Biology, Membrane Transport, Biomolecules, Cell Energy, Cell Division, Molecular Biology, Genetics

## Overview

Cell and Molecular Biology covers the ม.4 biology curriculum — the fundamental unit of life. From the organelles inside cells to the DNA that programs them, this is where biology becomes a molecular science. Understanding cells is essential for everything that follows: genetics, evolution, physiology, and biotechnology.

## Key Concepts

### Cell Respiration vs Photosynthesis

| Process            | Where        | Input             | Output          | Equation                            |
| ------------------ | ------------ | ----------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Photosynthesis** | Chloroplast  | CO₂ + H₂O + light | C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂    | 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂         |
| **Respiration**    | Mitochondria | C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂      | CO₂ + H₂O + ATP | C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 38ATP |

### DNA → RNA → Protein (Central Dogma)

| Step | Location | Process |
|---|---|---|
| **Replication** | Nucleus | DNA → DNA (copying) |
| **Transcription** | Nucleus | DNA → mRNA (reading the code) |
| **Translation** | Ribosome | mRNA → Protein (building the product) |

### Mendelian Ratios

| Cross | Genotypic Ratio | Phenotypic Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Monohybrid (Aa × Aa) | 1:2:1 | 3:1 |
| Dihybrid (AaBb × AaBb) | 1:2:1:2:4:2:1:2:1 | 9:3:3:1 |
