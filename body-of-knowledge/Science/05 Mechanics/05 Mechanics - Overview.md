# Mechanics — กลศาสตร์

> **Category:** Advanced Physics (ม.4, ว301)
> **Covering:** Measurement, Kinematics, Dynamics, Work/Energy, Momentum, Rotation
> **Foundation for:** All of physics — mechanics is the first and most fundamental branch

## Overview

Mechanics is the study of **motion and its causes**. For Thai วิทย์-คณิต students, this is the first physics course (ม.4, ว301) — and the most important. Newton's laws, conservation of energy and momentum, and rotational motion form the conceptual toolkit that every other physics topic builds upon.

## Key Formulas

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Kinematics** | v = u + at, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as |
| **Newton's 2nd Law** | F = ma (or ΣF = ma) |
| **Friction** | f = μN |
| **Kinetic Energy** | KE = ½mv² |
| **Potential Energy** | PE = mgh (gravitational) |
| **Work-Energy** | W = ΔKE + ΔPE |
| **Momentum** | p = mv, Σpᵢ = Σp_f (conservation) |
| **Torque** | τ = r × F = Iα |
| **Angular momentum** | L = Iω |

## Newton's Three Laws

| Law | Statement | Thai |
|---|---|---|
| **1st (Inertia)** | Object at rest stays at rest; object in motion stays in motion unless acted upon by a net force | กฎของความเฉื่อย |
| **2nd (F=ma)** | Acceleration is proportional to net force and inversely proportional to mass | กฎของแรง |
| **3rd (Action-Reaction)** | For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction | กฎของแรงกิริยา-ปฏิกิริยา |
