# Genetics, Evolution, and Ecology — พันธุศาสตร์ วิวัฒนาการ และนิเวศวิทยา

> **Category:** Advanced Biology (ม.5–ม.6, ว322–ว323)
> **Covering:** Evolution, Diversity, Microbiology, Plants, Animals, Human Body, Ecology, Environmental Science

## Overview

This category spans the "big picture" of biology — from the diversity of life through how species change over time to how organisms interact in ecosystems. Evolution provides the unifying framework; ecology shows how it all fits together in the real world.

## Key Concepts

### Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium

| Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| No mutation | Alleles don't change |
| No gene flow | No migration |
| Random mating | No sexual selection |
| No genetic drift | Large population |
| No natural selection | All genotypes equally fit |

$$p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad p + q = 1$$

### Ecological Pyramids

| Type | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Energy pyramid** | Energy decreases ~90% per trophic level |
| **Biomass pyramid** | Mass of organisms at each level |
| **Numbers pyramid** | Count of organisms at each level |

### Biogeochemical Cycles

| Cycle | Key Process |
|---|---|
| **Water** | Evaporation → condensation → precipitation → runoff |
| **Carbon** | Photosynthesis (fixation) ↔ Respiration (release) |
| **Nitrogen** | N₂ → NH₃ (fixation) → NO₃⁻ (nitrification) → N₂ (denitrification) |
