# Astronomy and Environment — ดาราศาสตร์และสิ่งแวดล้อม

> **Category:** Earth Science (ว341, elective)
> **Covering:** Solar System, Stars/Galaxies/Cosmology, Climate Change, Natural Resources, Hazards

## Overview

Astronomy takes us beyond Earth — to planets, stars, and galaxies. Environmental science brings us back to the urgent challenges facing our planet: climate change, resource depletion, and natural hazards. Together they complete the Earth Science story: where we are in the universe and how to sustain our home.

### Stellar Evolution (H-R Diagram)

| Mass | Life Path | End State |
|---|---|---|
| **Low** (<0.5 M☉) | Main sequence → Red dwarf | White dwarf |
| **Medium** (like Sun) | Main sequence → Red giant → Planetary nebula | White dwarf |
| **High** (>8 M☉) | Main sequence → Red supergiant → Supernova | Neutron star or Black hole |
