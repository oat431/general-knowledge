# Geology and Atmosphere — ธรณีวิทยาและบรรยากาศ

> **Category:** Earth Science (ว341, elective)
> **Covering:** Minerals/Rocks, Plate Tectonics, Earthquakes/Volcanoes, Weathering/Erosion, Earth History, Weather/Climate, Oceanography, Water Cycle

## Overview

Geology and Atmosphere covers the solid Earth and its gaseous envelope — the geosphere and atmosphere. Students learn how rocks form and transform, why earthquakes happen, how weather works, and how climate is changing. This is Earth as a dynamic, interconnected system.

## Key Concepts

### Rock Cycle

| Rock Type | Thai | Formation |
|---|---|---|
| **Igneous** | หินอัคนี | Cooling of magma/lava |
| **Sedimentary** | หินตะกอน | Compaction of sediments |
| **Metamorphic** | หินแปร | Heat + pressure |

### Plate Boundaries

| Type | Motion | Features | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Divergent** | ← → | Mid-ocean ridges, rift valleys | Mid-Atlantic Ridge |
| **Convergent** | → ← | Trenches, mountains, volcanoes | Himalayas, Mariana Trench |
| **Transform** | ↑↓ | Faults, earthquakes | San Andreas Fault |

### Seismic Waves

| Wave | Type | Speed | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P-wave** (Primary) | Compressional | Fastest | Push-pull |
| **S-wave** (Secondary) | Shear | Slower | Up-down / Side-to-side |
| **Surface wave** | Surface | Slowest | Rolling, most destructive |

### Richter vs Mercalli

| Scale | Measures | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Richter** | Energy released (magnitude) | 1–10 (logarithmic) |
| **Mercalli** | Damage and human experience (intensity) | I–XII |
