# Health and Physical Education — สุขศึกษาและพลศึกษา

> **Learning Area:** Health and Physical Education (สุขศึกษาและพลศึกษา) — Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (revised 2560)
> **Total Concept Areas:** ~14 | **Duration:** 12 years (ป.1–ม.6)
> **Source:** Ministry of Education

## Overview

Health and Physical Education combines two complementary strands: **health education** (understanding the body, nutrition, disease prevention, mental health) and **physical education** (movement, sports, fitness, teamwork). Together they build the knowledge, skills, and habits for lifelong wellness.

## Grade Band Progression

| Grade Band | Health Education | Physical Education |
|---|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Body parts, cleanliness, safety basics | Basic movements, simple games, active play |
