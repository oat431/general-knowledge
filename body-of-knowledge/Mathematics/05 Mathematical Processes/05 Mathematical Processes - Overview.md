# Mathematical Processes — กระบวนการทางคณิตศาสตร์

> **Category:** Fundamental Mathematics (ป.1–ม.3)
> **Covering:** Problem-solving, reasoning, communication, connections, representation
> **Duration:** Cross-cutting — taught throughout all 9 years

## Overview

Mathematical Processes are the **"how"** of mathematics — the skills that make mathematical knowledge useful. Unlike other categories that teach *content* (numbers, geometry), this category teaches *process*: how to solve problems, reason mathematically, communicate ideas, connect concepts, and represent thinking. These are woven through every lesson at every grade.

## The Five Process Skills

| Skill | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem Solving** | การแก้ปัญหา | Using strategies to tackle unfamiliar problems |
| **Reasoning** | การให้เหตุผล | Logical thinking — inductive (pattern → rule) and deductive (rule → conclusion) |
| **Communication** | การสื่อสาร | Expressing mathematical ideas clearly in words, symbols, and diagrams |
| **Connections** | การเชื่อมโยง | Linking math topics to each other and to real life |
| **Representation** | การนำเสนอ | Using tables, graphs, equations, diagrams to model situations |

## Polya's Problem-Solving Framework

| Stage | Thai | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Understand** | ทำความเข้าใจปัญหา | Read carefully; identify given/what's asked; restate |
| **2. Plan** | วางแผน | Choose strategy: draw, table, work backward, equation, pattern |
| **3. Execute** | ดำเนินการ | Carry out the plan; show all steps |
| **4. Reflect** | ตรวจสอบ | Does answer make sense? Verify another way |

### Problem-Solving Strategies

| Strategy | When to Use | Thai |
|---|---|---|
| Draw a picture | Geometric/spatial problems | การวาดภาพ |
| Make a table | Pattern finding, organization | การสร้างตาราง |
| Work backward | Known result, unknown start | การย้อนกลับ |
| Guess and check | Limited possibilities | การเดาและตรวจสอบ |
| Write an equation | Algebraic relationships | การเขียนสมการ |
| Look for a pattern | Sequences, predictions | การหารูปแบบ |
| Solve a simpler problem | Complex problems | การแก้ปัญหาที่ง่ายกว่า |
| Act it out | Sequential/concrete | การแสดงบทบาท |

## Types of Reasoning

### Inductive Reasoning (อุปนัย)
**Pattern → Generalization**

> Observe: 1+3=4, 1+3+5=9, 1+3+5+7=16
> Conjecture: Sum of first n odd numbers = n²

### Deductive Reasoning (นิรนัย)
**General rule → Specific conclusion**

> All squares have 4 right angles.
> Shape X is a square.
> Therefore, X has 4 right angles.

## Grade Band Progression

| Skill | ป.1–3 | ป.4–6 | ม.1–3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Solving** | Objects, drawings, acting out | Polya's steps, working backward, making tables | Multi-step problems, mathematical modeling |
| **Reasoning** | Explaining "why" simply | Justifying, checking reasonableness | Deductive proof, contradiction, induction |
| **Communication** | Basic math words | Step-by-step written solutions | Formal notation, critiquing reasoning |
| **Connections** | Math in daily life | Cross-topic connections (fractions↔decimals) | Math↔science, math↔real world systems |
| **Representation** | Objects, drawings | Tables, graphs, simple equations | Multiple representations (table, graph, equation, verbal) |
