# Foundations and Functions — ฐานรากและฟังก์ชัน

> **Category:** Advanced Mathematics (ม.4–ม.6, Sci-Math track)
> **Course:** ค301 (ม.4)
> **Covering:** Sets, Logic, Real Numbers, Algebraic Expressions, Equations, Functions, Exponents/Logarithms, Trigonometry, Sequences & Series

## Overview

This category covers the ม.4 curriculum — the bridge from fundamental mathematics to calculus. Students formalize their understanding of sets and logic, master functions as mathematical objects, and develop fluency with trigonometric, exponential, and logarithmic functions. These are the tools that calculus will operate on.
