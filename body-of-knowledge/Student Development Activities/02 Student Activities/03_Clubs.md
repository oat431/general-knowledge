# Clubs — ชมรม/ชุมนุม

> *"Clubs give students space to follow their interests, practice skills, and learn from peers outside the regular classroom."*

Clubs (ชมรม or ชุมนุม) are interest-based student activities that allow students to explore hobbies, academic interests, sports, arts, and technology in a less formal setting. Each school designs its own club offerings based on student interests, teacher expertise, and available resources.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Simple interest groups, games, creative play |
| **ป.4–6** | Topic-based clubs (art, science, sports, reading), choosing based on interest |
| **ม.1–3** | Diverse club offerings, sustained participation, skill development, peer collaboration |
| **ม.4–6** | Specialized clubs, student-led activities, portfolio building, competition preparation |

## 2 | Common Club Types

| Type | Thai | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Academic | ชมรมวิชาการ | Math, Science, English, Thai, Social Studies |
| Arts | ชมรมศิลปะ | Music, dance, painting, drama, photography |
| Sports | ชมรมกีฬา | Football, basketball, badminton, table tennis, swimming |
| Technology | ชมรมเทคโนโลยี | Computer, robotics, programming, web design |
| Service | ชมรมบำเพ็ญประโยชน์ | Volunteer work, environmental care, community help |
| Culture | ชมรมวัฒนธรรม | Thai dance, traditional music, local crafts |
| Health | ชมรมสุขภาพ | First aid, health promotion, mental health awareness |

## 3 | Key Concepts

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Interest-based learning | การเรียนรู้ตามความสนใจ | Students choose activities that match their interests |
| Skill development | การพัฒนาทักษะ | Practicing abilities beyond the classroom |
| Peer learning | การเรียนรู้จากเพื่อน | Students teaching and learning from each other |
| Leadership | ความเป็นผู้นำ | Club officers, event planning, team management |
| Sustained participation | การมีส่วนร่วมอย่างต่อเนื่อง | Commitment to a club over time |
| Student choice | การเลือกของผู้เรียน | Students select clubs based on interest |
