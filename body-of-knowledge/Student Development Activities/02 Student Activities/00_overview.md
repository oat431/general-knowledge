# Student Activities — กิจกรรมนักเรียน

> **Component:** กิจกรรมพัฒนาผู้เรียน → กิจกรรมนักเรียน
> **Scope:** ป.1–ม.6
> **Purpose:** Teamwork, discipline, leadership, service, interests, and participation

## Overview

Student Activities are organized opportunities for students to learn through participation rather than conventional classroom lessons. The national framework includes activities such as **Scouts and Girl Guides**, Red Cross Youth, clubs, student organizations, and leadership activities. The exact activity offered and its schedule depend on the school's curriculum.

**ลูกเสือและเนตรนารี is not simply a survival subject.** Outdoor and practical skills may be included, but the broader purpose is character development, teamwork, discipline, leadership, citizenship, and service.

## Grade-Band Progression

| Grade band | Main emphasis | Possible activities |
|---|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Rules, cooperation, simple responsibility, safe play | Basic group games, simple Scout activities, school care |
| **ป.4–6** | Practical skills, teamwork, responsibility, outdoor awareness | Knots, navigation, camping basics, service, clubs |
| **ม.1–3** | Leadership, planning, resilience, community participation | Senior Scout activities, clubs, student committees, service projects |

## Implementation Note

The national curriculum defines the category **กิจกรรมนักเรียน**, but each school decides which approved student activities it provides. Therefore, Scouts may be a regular activity in one school while another school emphasizes Red Cross Youth, clubs, or another student organization. The student should not be expected to have the same experience as students in every other school.
