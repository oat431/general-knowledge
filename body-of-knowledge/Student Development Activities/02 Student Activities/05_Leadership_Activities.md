# Leadership Activities — กิจกรรมผู้นำ

> *"Leadership is not about being in charge — it is about planning, communicating, and taking responsibility for shared goals."*

Leadership Activities develop students' ability to plan, communicate, make decisions, collaborate, and take responsibility. These activities may be integrated into Scouts, clubs, student organizations, or stand as dedicated leadership training programs.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Simple responsibilities (class helper, group leader for an activity), following instructions, helping friends |
| **ป.4–6** | Leading small groups, planning simple activities, communicating with peers, taking responsibility for tasks |
| **ม.1–3** | Event planning, team coordination, problem-solving, representing groups, mentoring younger students |
| **ม.4–6** | Strategic planning, project management, mentoring, conflict resolution, preparing for adult leadership roles |

---

## 2 | Key Concepts

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Planning | การวางแผน | Setting goals, identifying steps, allocating resources |
| Communication | การสื่อสาร | Clear expression, active listening, feedback |
| Decision-making | การตัดสินใจ | Gathering information, weighing options, choosing |
| Collaboration | การทำงานร่วมกัน | Working with others toward shared goals |
| Responsibility | ความรับผิดชอบ | Owning tasks and outcomes |
| Mentoring | การให้คำปรึกษาจากเพื่อนพี่ | Older students guiding younger ones |
| Initiative | ความคิดริเริ่ม | Starting projects, proposing improvements |

---

## 3 | Leadership Development Models

| Model | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Role-based leadership | การนำตามบทบาท | Class president, club president, team captain |
| Project-based leadership | การนำโดยโครงงาน | Leading a specific project or event |
| Service leadership | การนำเพื่อบริการ | Leading through community service |
| Peer leadership | การนำจากเพื่อนสู่เพื่อน | Students supporting and guiding peers |
| Mentorship | การเป็นพี่เลี้ยง | Senior students mentoring juniors |

## 5 | Real-World Examples

- **School event planning** — Sports day, Teacher's Day, Loy Krathong, graduation
- **Camp leadership** — Senior scouts leading camp activities
- **Orientation program** — ม.4 students helping ม.1 students adjust to secondary school
- **Community project leadership** — Organizing a recycling drive or temple cleanup
