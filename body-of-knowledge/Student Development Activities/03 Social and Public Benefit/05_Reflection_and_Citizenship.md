# Reflection and Citizenship — การสะท้อนการเรียนรู้และความเป็นพลเมือง

> *"Service without reflection is just work — reflection turns action into learning and citizenship."*

Reflection and Citizenship is the meaning-making component of service learning. Students look back on what they did, what they learned, how they changed, and what it means to be a responsible citizen. This component ensures that public-benefit activities develop civic identity, not just completed tasks.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Simple sharing: "What did we do? How did it feel? Who did we help?" |
| **ป.4–6** | Group discussion, simple journals, connecting service to school values |
| **ม.1–3** | Written reflection, project evaluation, connecting service to civic responsibility |
| **ม.4–6** | Critical reflection, civic identity development, connecting service to social issues and policy |

## 3 | Reflection Methods

| Method | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Group discussion | การอภิปรายกลุ่ม | Sharing experiences and insights verbally |
| Reflection journal | บันทึกการสะท้อนคิด | Written reflection on service experiences |
| Project portfolio | แฟ้มโครงงาน | Documenting project process and outcomes |
| Presentation | การนำเสนอ | Sharing learning with peers or community |
| Evaluation form | แบบประเมิน | Structured assessment of project outcomes |
| Photo/video documentation | การบันทึกภาพ/วิดีทัศน์ | Visual evidence of learning and action |

## 4 | Reflection Questions

| Question | Thai |
|---|---|
