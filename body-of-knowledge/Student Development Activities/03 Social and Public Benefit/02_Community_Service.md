# Community Service — การบำเพ็ญประโยชน์ต่อชุมชน

> *"Community service turns care into action — students learn by helping real people with real needs."*

Community Service involves students in identifying and responding to needs in their local community. It goes beyond school-bound responsibility and connects students with neighbors, temples, local organizations, and public spaces.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Helping at school events, simple kindness, visiting nearby community places |
| **ป.4–6** | Temple cleanup, helping elderly neighbors, community clean-up, school fair support |
| **ม.1–3** | Community needs survey, planned service projects, partnership with local organizations |
| **ม.4–6** | Sustained community partnerships, project management, mentoring younger students in service |

## 2 | Key Concepts

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Community needs | ความต้องการของชุมชน | Identifying what the community actually needs |
| Service learning | การเรียนรู้จากการบริการ | Connecting service with reflection and learning |
| Community partnership | การร่วมมือกับชุมชน | Working with local organizations and leaders |
| Cultural respect | การเคารพวัฒนธรรม | Respecting local customs and traditions during service |
| Reflection | การสะท้อนคิด | Learning from the service experience |
| Sustainability | ความยั่งยืน | Designing projects that last beyond one event |

## 3 | Common Activities

| Activity | Thai | Typical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Temple cleanup | ทำความสะอาดวัด | Respecting and caring for religious/community spaces |
| Elderly support | ช่วยเหลือผู้สูงอายุ | Visits, companionship, practical help |
| Community clean-up | ทำความสะอาดชุมชน | Improving public spaces |
| School fair | งานวันเด็ก / งานรื่นเริง | Organizing activities for community children |
| Blood donation awareness | รณรงค์บริจาคเลือด | Supporting Red Cross activities |
| Local festival support | ช่วยงานเทศกาล | Supporting community cultural events |

## 5 | Real-World Examples

- **Temple cleaning during Buddhist holidays** — Students help clean temple grounds
- **Elderly home visits** — Students visit local nursing homes or elderly neighbors
- **Community cleanup** — Monthly cleanup of local streets, canals, or parks
- **Children's Day activities** — ม.4–6 students organize games for ป.1–3 students
