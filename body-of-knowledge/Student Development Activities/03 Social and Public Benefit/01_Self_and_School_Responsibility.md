# Self and School Responsibility — ความรับผิดชอบต่อตนเองและโรงเรียน

> *"Responsibility begins with caring for yourself and your shared spaces."*

Self and School Responsibility is the foundational level of social and public benefit activities. Students learn to care for themselves, their belongings, their classroom, and their school environment. It builds the habits of reliability, care, and shared responsibility that underpin all later service.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Personal belongings, classroom cleanliness, following routines, caring for shared items |
| **ป.4–6** | Classroom duties, school environment care, reliability in assigned tasks, environmental awareness |
| **ม.1–3** | School improvement projects, mentoring younger students, sustained responsibility for areas of school life |
| **ม.4–6** | Leading school improvement, modeling responsibility for younger students, strategic school projects |

## 5 | Real-World Examples

- **Daily classroom cleaning** — Students take turns sweeping, wiping desks, organizing
- **School garden** — Planting and maintaining flowers or vegetables
- **Recycling bins** — Sorting paper, plastic, and general waste
- **Library helpers** — ม.1–3 students helping ป.1–3 find books
