# Environmental Service — การอนุรักษ์สิ่งแวดล้อม

> *"Environmental service teaches students that caring for nature is caring for their own future."*

Environmental Service involves students in conservation, waste reduction, tree planting, water protection, and biodiversity care. It connects classroom science learning with real-world environmental action in the school and community.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Simple recycling, not littering, caring for plants, basic water awareness |
| **ป.4–6** | Waste sorting, tree planting, school garden, energy saving, water conservation |
| **ม.1–3** | Environmental survey, conservation project, community education campaign |
| **ม.4–6** | Sustainability research, environmental policy advocacy, long-term conservation projects |

## 3 | Common Activities

| Activity | Thai | Typical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Tree planting | ปลูกต้นไม้ | Reforestation, school greening |
| Recycling program | โครงการรีไซเคิล | Waste sorting and recycling |
| Canal/river cleanup | ทำความสะอาดคลอง/แม่น้ำ | Protecting water sources |
| School garden | สวนโรงเรียน | Growing vegetables, herbs, flowers |

| Environmental education | การให้ความรู้ด้านสิ่งแวดล้อม | Teaching community about conservation |

## 5 | Real-World Examples

- **Tree planting on National Arbor Day** — Annual school tree-planting events
- **Beach cleanup** — Coastal schools organize beach clean-up days
- **Composting** — School food-waste composting for garden use
- **Plastic-free campaign** — Reducing single-use plastic in school cafeteria
