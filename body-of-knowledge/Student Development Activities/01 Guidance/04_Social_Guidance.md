# Social Guidance — การแนะแนวสังคม

> *"Social guidance helps students build healthy relationships, communicate effectively, and participate responsibly in their community."*

Social guidance develops students' interpersonal skills, social responsibility, and ability to live peacefully with others. It covers friendship, communication, cooperation, conflict resolution, and responsible participation in school and community life.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Making friends, sharing, taking turns, following rules, respectful communication |
| **ป.4–6** | Teamwork, active listening, understanding differences, basic conflict resolution, digital etiquette |
| **ม.1–3** | Peer relationships, managing conflicts, responsible social media use, understanding social roles |
| **ม.4–6** | Professional communication, leadership in groups, civic participation, managing complex relationships |

| Concept | Thai | Description |

| Conflict resolution | การแก้ไขความขัดแย้ง | Negotiation, mediation, peaceful solutions |
| Empathy | ความเห็นอกเห็นใจ | Understanding others' feelings |

## 3 | Common Activities

| Activity | Thai | Typical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Group discussion | การอภิปรายกลุ่ม | Practicing communication and listening |
| Role-play | การแสดงบทบาท | Practicing social situations |
| Team-building games | เกมสร้างทีม | Cooperation and trust |
| Conflict mediation practice | การฝึกไกล่เกลี่ย | Learning to resolve disputes peacefully |
| Community project | โครงงานชุมชน | Applying social skills in real settings |
| Digital citizenship workshop | กิจกรรมพลเมืองดิจิทัล | Online safety, digital etiquette, anti-cyberbullying |

## 5 | Real-World Examples

- **Group projects** — Learning to share work, communicate, and resolve disagreements
- **Social media** — Managing online identity, avoiding cyberbullying, digital footprint awareness
- **Classroom conflicts** — Mediating disputes between classmates
- **Community volunteer work** — Applying social skills in real community settings
