# Personal Guidance — การแนะแนวส่วนตัว

> *"Personal guidance helps students understand their emotions, build self-esteem, and make responsible decisions."*

Personal guidance supports the emotional and psychological development of students. It helps them understand their feelings, build self-confidence, manage stress, make ethical decisions, and develop resilience. This domain is closely connected to mental health and well-being.

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Naming feelings, understanding right and wrong, building self-confidence, managing simple emotions |
| **ป.4–6** | Self-esteem, understanding others' feelings, managing anger and disappointment, basic decision-making |
| **ม.1–3** | Identity development, emotional regulation, stress management, responsible decision-making, self-acceptance |
| **ม.4–6** | Mental health awareness, resilience building, independent decision-making, managing life transitions and pressures |

## 3 | Common Activities

| Activity | Thai | Typical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Self-reflection journal | บันทึกการสะท้อนคิด | Writing about feelings and experiences |
| Individual counseling | การให้คำปรึกษารายบุคคล | Private support for personal concerns |
| Group counseling | การให้คำปรึกษาเป็นกลุ่ม | Peer support and shared learning |
| Values clarification | การจำแนกค่านิยม | Exploring what matters most |
| Mindfulness practice | การฝึกสติ | Simple breathing and awareness exercises |
| Goal-setting workshop | กิจกรรมตั้งเป้าหมาย | Personal goals and action planning |

## 5 | Real-World Examples

- **Exam stress** — Learning to manage pressure before O-NET or university entrance exams
- **Bullying** — Building confidence to report and cope with bullying situations
- **Family problems** — Counseling support when home life affects learning
- **Social media pressure** — Managing comparison, cyberbullying, and digital identity
