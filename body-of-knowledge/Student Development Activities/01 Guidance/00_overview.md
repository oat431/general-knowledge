# Guidance and Counseling Activities — กิจกรรมแนะแนว

> **Component:** กิจกรรมพัฒนาผู้เรียน → กิจกรรมแนะแนว
> **Scope:** ป.1–ม.6
> **Purpose:** Educational, career, personal, and social development

## Overview

Guidance is a learner-development activity, not another academic learning area. It helps students understand themselves, manage learning, explore education and careers, develop life skills, and build healthy relationships. The exact timetable and delivery model are determined by each school's curriculum.

The official OBEC guidance framework describes three broad developmental stages:

| Stage | Thai | Main emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| **Career Awareness** | การตระหนักรู้ด้านอาชีพ | ป.1–ป.6: knowing oneself, learning, work, and the variety of careers |
| **Career Exploration** | การสำรวจอาชีพ | ม.1–ม.3: exploring interests, abilities, pathways, and work experiences |
| **Career Preparation and Assimilation** | การเตรียมความพร้อมและปรับตัวสู่อาชีพ | ม.4–ม.6: higher-education planning, career preparation, and adapting to real situations |

## Grade-Band Progression

| Grade band | Educational | Career | Personal and social |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Adjusting to school, learning routines, asking for help | Recognizing community jobs and personal interests | Naming feelings, friendship, cooperation, safety |
| **ป.4–6** | Study strategies, goals, strengths and learning preferences | Career awareness, work values, basic career information | Self-confidence, communication, problem-solving, digital safety |
| **ม.1–3** | Choosing an upper-secondary or vocational pathway | Career exploration, aptitude and interest discovery, work experience | Identity, stress management, relationships, responsible decisions |
| **ม.4–6** | Higher-education planning, applications, transition planning | Career preparation, employability, portfolio, workplace adjustment | Well-being, resilience, relationships, independent decision-making |

## Common Guidance Activities

| Activity | Thai | Typical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Self-exploration | การสำรวจตนเอง | Interests, abilities, values, personality, strengths |
| Study planning | การวางแผนการเรียน | Goals, schedules, learning strategies |
| Career information | ข้อมูลอาชีพ | Occupations, pathways, qualifications, work conditions |
| Career interest assessment | แบบวัดความสนใจอาชีพ | A starting point for reflection, not a fixed label |
| Counseling conversation | การให้คำปรึกษา | Listening, clarifying concerns, planning support |
| Higher-education guidance | แนะแนวการศึกษาต่อ | Programs, admissions, vocational and academic routes |
| Life-skills activity | กิจกรรมทักษะชีวิต | Coping, communication, decisions, safety |
| Peer support | เพื่อนที่ปรึกษา | Students supporting one another with adult guidance |

## Implementation Note

Schools may assign guidance to a dedicated guidance teacher, homeroom teachers, a student-support team, or a combination. The number of guidance teachers is a school staffing matter, not a national curriculum requirement. Activities may be weekly, block-scheduled, individual, group-based, or integrated with school projects.
