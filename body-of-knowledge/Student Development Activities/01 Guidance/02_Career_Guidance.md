# Career Guidance — การแนะแนวอาชีพ

> *"Career guidance is not about choosing a job early — it is about discovering who you are and what you can contribute."*

Career guidance helps students understand themselves, explore the world of work, and prepare for future careers. It follows three OBEC developmental stages: Career Awareness (ป.1–6), Career Exploration (ม.1–3), and Career Preparation & Assimilation (ม.4–6).

---

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Recognizing community jobs (teacher, doctor, farmer, shopkeeper), understanding that work helps people |
| **ป.4–6** | Career awareness: variety of occupations, work values, what parents and community members do |
| **ม.1–3** | Career exploration: interests, aptitudes, personality, work-experience activities, considering pathways |
| **ม.4–6** | Career preparation: employability skills, portfolio, workplace visits, adapting to professional environments |

---

## 2 | OBEC Three Career Development Stages

| Stage | Thai | Level | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Career Awareness** | การตระหนักรู้ด้านอาชีพ | ป.1–6 | Knowing oneself, learning, work, and career variety |
| **Career Exploration** | การสำรวจอาชีพ | ม.1–3 | Exploring interests, abilities, pathways, work experiences |
| **Career Preparation & Assimilation** | การเตรียมความพร้อมและปรับตัวสู่อาชีพ | ม.4–6 | Higher-education planning, career preparation, workplace adjustment |

## 3 | Key Concepts

| Concept | Thai | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Self-understanding | การรู้จักตนเอง | Interests, abilities, values, personality |
| Career awareness | ความตระหนักรู้ด้านอาชีพ | Knowing the variety of careers that exist |
| Career exploration | การสำรวจอาชีพ | Investigating specific careers and pathways |
| Career preparation | การเตรียมความพร้อมทางอาชีพ | Skills, qualifications, portfolio development |
| Career assimilation | การปรับตัวสู่อาชีพ | Adapting to workplace culture and demands |
| Work values | ค่านิยมในการทำงาน | What matters to the person in work (security, creativity, service, income) |
| Employability | ความพร้อมในการประกอบอาชีพ | Skills that make a person hirable and adaptable |

---

## 4 | Common Activities

| Activity | Thai | Typical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Career-interest inventory | แบบวัดแววอาชีพ | Starting point for self-reflection (not a fixed label) |
| Career day | วันอาชีพ | Guest speakers from various professions |
| Workplace visit | การดูงาน | Observing real workplaces |
| Work experience | การฝึกประสบการณ์อาชีพ | Short-term placement in a workplace |
| Portfolio | แฟ้มสะสมผลงาน | Documenting skills and achievements |
| Career counseling | การให้คำปรึกษาอาชีพ | Individual guidance on career decisions |

## 6 | Real-World Examples

- **Career Day at school** — Parents and community members describe their jobs
- **SUMMER CAMP อาชีพ** — OBEC program for work-experience during school break
- **แบบวัดแววอาชีพ** — OBEC-provided interest inventory as a self-reflection starting point
- **University open days** — ม.5–6 students visit campuses to explore programs
