# Career and Technology — การงานอาชีพ

> **Learning Area:** Career and Technology (การงานอาชีพ) — Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (revised 2560)
> **Total Concept Areas:** ~25 | **Duration:** 12 years (ป.1–ม.6)
> **Source:** Ministry of Education

## Overview

Career and Technology (การงานอาชีพ) is the most practical learning area — where school meets real life. Students learn household management, agriculture, crafts, career exploration, and digital technology. The Sufficiency Economy Philosophy (ปรัชญาเศรษฐกิจพอเพียง) of King Rama IX underpins this entire learning area.
