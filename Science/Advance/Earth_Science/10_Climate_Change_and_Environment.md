# Climate Change and Environment — การเปลี่ยนแปลงสภาพภูมิอากาศและสิ่งแวดล้อม

> *"We are the first generation to feel the impact of climate change and the last generation that can do something about it."* — Barack Obama

Climate change (การเปลี่ยนแปลงสภาพภูมิอากาศ) refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. While Earth's climate has changed naturally throughout its history, the current warming trend is unprecedented in its speed and is driven primarily by human activities (กิจกรรมของมนุษย์) — especially the burning of fossil fuels (เชื้อเพลิงฟอสซิล) which releases greenhouse gases (ก๊าซเรือนกระจก) into the atmosphere.

The **greenhouse effect** (ปรากฏการณ์เรือนกระจก) is a natural process that keeps Earth warm enough for life — without it, Earth's average temperature would be $-18^\circ\text{C}$ instead of $+15^\circ\text{C}$. However, human-enhanced greenhouse warming (การเพิ่มของปรากฏการณ์เรือนกระจก) has raised atmospheric $\ce{CO2}$ from $280$ ppm to over $420$ ppm since the Industrial Revolution, causing global temperatures to rise by approximately $1.1^\circ\text{C}$. Understanding the carbon cycle (วัฏจักรคาร์บอน), energy resources, and sustainability (ความยั่งยืน) is essential for addressing this defining challenge of our era.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 The Greenhouse Effect (ปรากฏการณ์เรือนกระจก)

**Natural greenhouse effect:**

1. Solar radiation (รังสีจากดวงอาทิตย์) reaches Earth — mostly visible and UV light
2. Earth's surface absorbs radiation and warms up
3. Surface emits **infrared radiation** (รังสีอินฟราเรด) back toward space
4. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-radiate some infrared → traps heat
5. Result: Earth's average temperature is $+15^\circ\text{C}$ (not $-18^\circ\text{C}$)

**Enhanced greenhouse effect:** Human activities increase GHG concentrations → more heat is trapped → global warming (ภาวะโลกร้อน).

### 3.2 Greenhouse Gases (ก๊าซเรือนกระจก)

| Gas | Formula | Pre-industrial | Current (2024) | GWP (100 yr) | Main Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbon dioxide** | $\ce{CO2}$ | $280$ ppm | $\sim 422$ ppm | $1$ | Fossil fuel combustion |
| **Methane** | $\ce{CH4}$ | $0.7$ ppm | $\sim 1.9$ ppm | $28$ | Livestock, rice paddies, landfills |
| **Nitrous oxide** | $\ce{N2O}$ | $0.27$ ppm | $\sim 0.33$ ppm | $265$ | Fertilizers, industrial processes |
| **Water vapor** | $\ce{H2O}$ | Variable | Increasing | — | Natural feedback |
| **CFCs** | Various | $0$ | Trace | $4{,}000$–$14{,}000$ | Refrigerants (banned) |

**Global Warming Potential (GWP)** compares warming impact relative to $\ce{CO2}$.

### 3.3 Evidence of Global Warming (หลักฐานภาวะโลกร้อน)

1. **Temperature rise**: Global average temperature increased $\sim 1.1^\circ\text{C}$ since 1880
2. **Sea level rise**: $\sim 21$ cm since 1900 ($\sim 3.3$ mm/year currently)
3. **Ice melt**: Arctic sea ice decreasing $\sim 13\%$ per decade; glaciers retreating worldwide
4. **Ocean warming**: Oceans absorb $\sim 90\%$ of excess heat
5. **Extreme weather**: More frequent and intense hurricanes, droughts, heatwaves

**Keeling Curve**: Continuous measurement of atmospheric $\ce{CO2}$ at Mauna Loa Observatory since 1958 shows steady increase with seasonal oscillations.

### 3.4 The Carbon Cycle (วัฏจักรคาร์บอน)

| Process | Direction | Carbon Flux (GtC/yr) |
|---|---|---|
| Photosynthesis (การสังเคราะห์แสง) | Atmosphere → biosphere | $-120$ |
| Respiration (การหายใจ) | Biosphere → atmosphere | $+120$ |
| Ocean absorption | Atmosphere → ocean | $-90$ |
| Ocean release | Ocean → atmosphere | $+90$ |
| Fossil fuel burning (การเผาไหม้) | Geosphere → atmosphere | $+10$ |
| Deforestation (การตัดไม้ทำลายป่า) | Biosphere → atmosphere | $+1.5$ |

**The imbalance:** Human activities add $\sim 11.5$ GtC/year, but natural sinks absorb only $\sim 5$ GtC/year → $\sim 5$ GtC accumulates in the atmosphere annually.

### 3.5 Carbon Footprint (ลายเท้าคาร์บอน)

**Carbon footprint** = total greenhouse gas emissions caused by an individual, event, organization, or product, expressed as $\ce{CO2}$ equivalent ($\ce{CO2e}$).

**Ways to reduce carbon footprint:**
- Energy efficiency (use LED, efficient appliances)
- Renewable energy (พลังงานหมุนเวียน)
- Public transportation, cycling, walking
- Reduce meat consumption
- Reduce, reuse, recycle (ลด ใช้ซ้ำ รีไซเคิล)
- Plant trees (ปลูกต้นไม้)

### 3.6 Energy Resources (แหล่งพลังงาน)

| Type | Source | Advantage | Disadvantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coal** (ถ่านหิน) | Fossil fuel | Abundant, cheap | High $\ce{CO2}$, pollution |
| **Oil/Petroleum** (ปิโตรเลียม) | Fossil fuel | Versatile, energy-dense | Finite, geopolitical |
| **Natural gas** (ก๊าซธรรมชาติ) | Fossil fuel | Cleanest fossil fuel | Still emits $\ce{CO2}$ |
| **Solar** (พลังงานแสงอาทิตย์) | Renewable | Clean, abundant | Intermittent, requires storage |
| **Wind** (พลังงานลม) | Renewable | Clean, cost-effective | Intermittent, visual/noise |
| **Hydro** (พลังงานน้ำ) | Renewable | Reliable, storage-capable | Ecosystem disruption |
| **Biomass** (ชีวมวล) | Renewable | Carbon-neutral (ideally) | Land use, food competition |
| **Geothermal** (พลังงานความร้อนใต้พิภพ) | Renewable | Constant, reliable | Location-limited |
| **Nuclear** (นิวเคลียร์) | Non-renewable | Low $\ce{CO2}$, high output | Waste, safety concerns |

| Issue | Impact | Region |
|---|---|---|
| **Sea level rise** | Coastal erosion, Bangkok subsidence | Gulf of Thailand coast |
| **Changing rainfall** | Flood/drought extremes | Nationwide |
| **Coral bleaching** | Reef ecosystem loss | Andaman Sea, Gulf |

### 3.8 The IPCC and Climate Policy

The **IPCC** (คณะกรรมการระหว่างรัฐบาลว่าด้วยการเปลี่ยนแปลงสภาพภูมิอากาศ) is the UN body for assessing climate science. Key findings from AR6 (2021):

- Human influence has **warmed the climate at a rate unprecedented** in at least $2{,}000$ years
- Warming of $1.5^\circ\text{C}$ will be reached by early 2030s under all scenarios
- **Net-zero $\ce{CO2}$ emissions** by 2050 required to limit warming to $1.5^\circ\text{C}$
- Every additional $0.5^\circ\text{C}$ increases climate extremes significantly

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Greenhouse Effect Mechanism

> Explain why the greenhouse effect is both essential for life and currently a problem.

**Solution:**
- **Essential**: Without the natural greenhouse effect, Earth's average temperature would be $-18^\circ\text{C}$ — too cold for life. Greenhouse gases keep Earth at $+15^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Problem**: Human activities have **enhanced** the greenhouse effect by increasing GHG concentrations, causing **additional** warming beyond the natural level — leading to climate disruption.

### Type 2: Calculating Carbon Footprint

> A family uses $5{,}000$ kWh of electricity per year. If the emission factor is $0.5$ kg $\ce{CO2}$/kWh, calculate their annual carbon footprint from electricity.

**Solution:**

$$\text{Emissions} = 5{,}000 \text{ kWh} \times 0.5 \text{ kg CO}_2\text{/kWh} = 2{,}500 \text{ kg CO}_2 = 2.5 \text{ tonnes CO}_2\text{/year}$$

### Type 3: Comparing GWP of Greenhouse Gases

> $1$ kg of $\ce{CH4}$ is released. How much $\ce{CO2}$-equivalent does this represent? (GWP of $\ce{CH4}$ = $28$)

**Solution:**

$$\ce{CO2e} = 1 \text{ kg} \times 28 = 28 \text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$$

This means $1$ kg of methane has the same warming impact as $28$ kg of $\ce{CO2}$ over $100$ years.

### Type 4: Evaluating Energy Source Trade-offs

> A country is deciding between coal and solar power. Compare their advantages and disadvantages.

**Solution:**

| Factor | Coal | Solar |
|---|---|---|
| $\ce{CO2}$ emissions | High ($\sim 1.0$ kg/kWh) | Near zero |
| Cost (per kWh) | Low | Decreasing, competitive |
| Reliability | High (on-demand) | Intermittent (needs storage) |
| Resource availability | Finite | Infinite (renewable) |
| Environmental impact | Mining, air pollution | Land use, panel recycling |

**Conclusion:** Solar is better for long-term sustainability despite intermittency challenges.
