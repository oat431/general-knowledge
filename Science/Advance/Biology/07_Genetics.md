# Genetics — พันธุศาสตร์

> *"Like begets like."* — Ancient proverb, formalized by Gregor Mendel

Genetics (พันธุศาสตร์) is the study of heredity (การถ่ายทอดลักษณะ) and variation (ความแปรปรวน) in living organisms. Gregor Mendel's (เกรกอร์ เมนเดล) experiments with pea plants in the 1860s established the fundamental laws of inheritance through the concept of discrete hereditary factors (now called genes). His work, rediscovered in 1900 by de Vries, Correns, and von Tschermak, laid the foundation for modern genetics.

Modern genetics extends Mendel's work to include non-Mendelian inheritance patterns such as incomplete dominance (การถ่ายทอดลักษณะแบบเด่นไม่สมบูรณ์), codominance (การถ่ายทอดลักษณะแบบเด่นร่วม), sex-linked traits (ลักษณะที่เกี่ยวข้องกับเพศ), and chromosomal abnormalities. Understanding genetics is essential for medicine, agriculture, forensics, and evolutionary biology.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Mendel's Laws of Inheritance

Gregor Mendel worked with *Pisum sativum* (pea plants) and tracked 7 traits over multiple generations.

**Law of Dominance (กฎของความเด่น):** In a heterozygote (Aa), only the dominant allele is expressed in the phenotype. The recessive allele is masked but not eliminated.

**Law of Segregation (กฎของการแยกตัว):** During gamete formation, the two alleles for each gene separate (segregate) so that each gamete carries only one allele. This occurs during meiosis I when homologous chromosomes separate.

**Law of Independent Assortment (กฎของการจัดเรียงตัวอิสระ):** Alleles of different genes assort independently during gamete formation (provided they are on different chromosomes or far apart on the same chromosome). This occurs during metaphase I when homologous pairs align randomly.

### 3.2 Monohybrid Cross (การทดลองลูกผสมเดี่ยว)

Cross examining one trait. Mendel crossed pure-breeding tall (TT) x pure-breeding short (tt):

|  | T | T |
|---|---|---|
| **t** | Tt | Tt |
| **t** | Tt | Tt |

- F1 generation: All Tt (tall) — demonstrates dominance.
- F1 x F1 cross (Tt x Tt):

|  | T | t |
|---|---|---|
| **T** | TT | Tt |
| **t** | Tt | tt |

- F2 genotypic ratio: 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt
- F2 phenotypic ratio: **3 tall : 1 short (3:1)**
- This demonstrates the Law of Segregation.

### 3.3 Dihybrid Cross (การทดลองลูกผสมคู่)

Cross examining two traits simultaneously. Mendel crossed round yellow seeds (RRYY) x wrinkled green seeds (rryy):

- F1: All RrYy (round yellow)
- F1 x F1 (RrYy x RrYy): 4x4 Punnett square yields:
- F2 phenotypic ratio: **9:3:3:1**
  - 9 round yellow (R_Y_)
  - 3 round green (R_yy)
  - 3 wrinkled yellow (rrY_)
  - 1 wrinkled green (rryy)

This demonstrates the Law of Independent Assortment.

### 3.4 Test Cross (การทดสอบลูกผสม)

To determine the genotype of a dominant phenotype (e.g., tall plant), cross it with a homozygous recessive (tt):
- If all offspring are tall -> the unknown is **TT** (homozygous dominant).
- If about 50% are short -> the unknown is **Tt** (heterozygous).

### 3.5 Non-Mendelian Inheritance

**Incomplete Dominance (การถ่ายทอดลักษณะแบบเด่นไม่สมบูรณ์):** The heterozygote shows an intermediate phenotype. Example: Red snapdragons (RR) x White snapdragons (R'R') -> Pink (RR') in F1.

|  | R | R' |
|---|---|---|
| **R** | RR | RR' |
| **R'** | RR' | R'R' |

- F2 ratio: 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white (**1:2:1** phenotypic ratio, same as genotypic).

**Codominance (การถ่ายทอดลักษณะแบบเด่นร่วม):** Both alleles are fully expressed in the heterozygote. Example: In cattle, red (RR) x white (R'R') -> roan (RR') — both red and white hairs present.

**Multiple Alleles:** More than two alleles exist in a population (though each individual has only two). Example: ABO blood group (หมู่เลือดเอบีโอ) has three alleles: $I^A$, $I^B$, and $i$.
- $I^A I^A$ or $I^A i$ -> Type A
- $I^B I^B$ or $I^B i$ -> Type B
- $I^A I^B$ -> Type AB (codominance)
- $ii$ -> Type O

**Polygenic Inheritance (การถ่ายทอดลักษณะแบบพอลิยีน):** Multiple genes contribute to one phenotype, producing a continuous distribution. Examples: skin color, height, eye color.

### 3.6 Sex-Linked Traits (ลักษณะที่เกี่ยวข้องกับเพศ)

Genes located on the X chromosome show distinctive inheritance patterns because males (XY) have only one X chromosome — they are **hemizygous**. A recessive allele on the X chromosome will be expressed in males but masked by a dominant allele in females.

**Example: Color blindness (ตาบอดสี)** — X-linked recessive ($X^c$):
- $X^C X^C$: Normal female
- $X^C X^c$: Carrier female (normal phenotype)
- $X^c X^c$: Color-blind female (rare)
- $X^C Y$: Normal male
- $X^c Y$: Color-blind male

**Cross: Carrier female x Normal male ($X^C X^c$ x $X^C Y$):**

|  | $X^C$ | Y |
|---|---|---|
| $X^C$ | $X^C X^C$ | $X^C Y$ |
| $X^c$ | $X^C X^c$ | $X^c Y$ |

- 50% normal sons, 50% color-blind sons; all daughters phenotypically normal (50% carriers).

### 3.7 Pedigree Analysis (การวิเคราะห์เพดดิกรี)

Pedigrees use standardized symbols:
- circle = female, square = male
- Filled = affected; half-filled = carrier
- Horizontal line = mating; vertical line = offspring

Key patterns:
- **Autosomal dominant:** Affected individuals appear in every generation; affected individuals have at least one affected parent.
- **Autosomal recessive:** Can skip generations; affected individuals can have unaffected parents (carriers); both sexes equally affected.
- **X-linked recessive:** More males affected; carrier mothers can pass to sons; affected fathers pass carrier status to all daughters.

### 3.8 Chromosomal Abnormalities (ความผิดปกติของโครโมโซม)

**Aneuploidy (แอนยูพลอยดี):** Abnormal chromosome number due to nondisjunction (การไม่แยกตัว) during meiosis.

| Condition | Karyotype | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Down syndrome | Trisomy 21 (47, +21) | Intellectual disability, characteristic facial features |
| Turner syndrome | 45, X | Female; short stature, infertile |
| Klinefelter syndrome | 47, XXY | Male; tall, infertile, may have breast development |
| Edwards syndrome | Trisomy 18 (47, +18) | Severe; usually fatal in first year |
| Patau syndrome | Trisomy 13 (47, +13) | Severe; usually fatal in first year |

**Karyotype (คาริโอไทปี):** Photograph of chromosomes arranged by size, banding pattern, and centromere position. Used to diagnose chromosomal abnormalities. Prepared from cells arrested in metaphase.

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Monohybrid Cross with Dominance
> In pea plants, purple flowers (P) are dominant over white (p). Cross Pp x Pp. What fraction of offspring will have white flowers?

**Solution:**

|  | P | p |
|---|---|---|
| **P** | PP | Pp |
| **p** | Pp | pp |

- Genotypic ratio: 1 PP : 2 Pp : 1 pp
- White flowers (pp): **1/4 or 25%**

### Type 2: Dihybrid Cross Probability
> In guinea pigs, black (B) is dominant over brown (b), and short hair (S) is dominant over long (s). Cross BbSs x BbSs. What is the probability of a brown, short-haired offspring?

**Solution:** By independent assortment:
- P(bb) = 1/4
- P(S_) = 3/4
- P(bbS_) = 1/4 x 3/4 = **3/16 or 18.75%**

### Type 3: Sex-Linked Inheritance
> A carrier mother ($X^H X^h$) and a hemophiliac father ($X^h Y$) have children. What fraction of sons will be hemophiliac?

**Solution:**

|  | $X^h$ | Y |
|---|---|---|
| $X^H$ | $X^H X^h$ | $X^H Y$ |
| $X^h$ | $X^h X^h$ | $X^h Y$ |

- Sons: $X^H Y$ (normal) and $X^h Y$ (hemophiliac)
- **50% of sons** will be hemophiliac.

### Type 4: ABO Blood Type Cross
> A man with type A blood (genotype $I^A i$) and a woman with type B blood (genotype $I^B i$) have children. What are the possible blood types?

**Solution:**

|  | $I^A$ | $i$ |
|---|---|---|
| $I^B$ | $I^A I^B$ (AB) | $I^B i$ (B) |
| $i$ | $I^A i$ (A) | $ii$ (O) |

Possible blood types: **A, B, AB, O** — each with 25% probability. This is the only cross that can produce all four blood types.
