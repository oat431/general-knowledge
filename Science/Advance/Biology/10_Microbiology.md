# Microbiology — จุลชีววิทยา

> *"The smallest organisms are the most numerous on Earth."*

Microbiology (จุลชีววิทยา) is the study of microscopic organisms (จุลชีพ) including bacteria (แบคทีเรีย), viruses (ไวรัส), fungi (เห็ดรา), protists (โพรทิสตา), and archaea (อาร์เคีย). These organisms play critical roles in ecosystems as decomposers (ผู้ย่อยสลาย), nitrogen fixers, pathogens (เชื้อก่อโรค), and in biotechnology. Despite their tiny size, microbes drive global nutrient cycles, cause diseases, and are essential for human health (e.g., gut microbiome).

The study of microorganisms bridges medicine, ecology, and industry. Understanding microbial structure, reproduction, and metabolism is crucial for combating infectious diseases (โรคติดเชื้อ), developing antibiotics (ยาปฏิชีวนะ), and harnessing microbes for food production and bioremediation (การบำบัดทางชีวภาพ).

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Bacterial Structure

Bacteria (แบคทีเรีย) are prokaryotic — no membrane-bound nucleus or organelles. Key structures: **cell wall** (ผนังเซลล์, made of peptidoglycan), **cell membrane** (เยื่อหุ้มเซลล์), **nucleoid** (นิวคลีอยด์, circular DNA region), **plasmid** (พลาสมิด, small extra-chromosomal DNA), **ribosomes** (70S), **flagella** (แฟลกเจลลัม, for movement), **pili** (พิลิ, for attachment/conjugation), and **capsule** (แคปซูล, for protection).

**Gram staining** differentiates bacteria by cell wall composition. **Gram-positive** (แกรมบวก) bacteria have a thick peptidoglycan layer and stain purple. **Gram-negative** (แกรมลบ) bacteria have a thin peptidoglycan layer plus an outer membrane and stain pink/red.

### 3.2 Bacterial Reproduction

**Binary fission** (การแบ่งตัวแบบทวิภาค) — asexual reproduction producing two identical daughter cells; can occur every 20 minutes under ideal conditions. **Genetic variation** mechanisms include: **transformation** (การแปลงสภาพ, uptake of free DNA), **transduction** (การถ่ายทอดโดย噬菌体, transfer via bacteriophage), **conjugation** (การจับคู่ถ่ายทอด, direct transfer via pili), and **transposons** (ทรานสโพซอน, jumping genes).

### 3.3 Viral Structure and Replication

Viruses (ไวรัส) are **acellular** — not truly living. They consist of a **nucleic acid core** (DNA or RNA), a **protein capsid** (แคปซิด), and sometimes a **lipid envelope** (enveloped viruses like HIV, influenza, SARS-CoV-2). Viruses are obligate intracellular parasites — they can only replicate inside a host cell (เซลล์เจ้าของ).

The **lytic cycle** (วงจรไลติก) involves viral attachment, injection of nucleic acid, hijacking host machinery for replication, assembly of new virions, and **lysis** (การทำลาย) of the host cell. The **lysogenic cycle** (วงจรไลโซจินิก) involves viral DNA integrating into the host chromosome as a **provirus** (โพรไวรัส), remaining dormant until triggered to enter the lytic cycle.

**HIV** (ไวรัสภูมิคุ้มกันบกพร่อง) targets CD4+ T-cells, leading to AIDS. **SARS-CoV-2** causes COVID-19, an enveloped RNA virus using spike proteins to bind ACE2 receptors.

### 3.4 Fungi

Fungi (เห็ดรา) are eukaryotic with chitin cell walls. Body structure: **hyphae** (ไฮฟา, thread-like filaments) form a **mycelium** (ไมซีเลียม). Some form **mushrooms** (เห็ด, fruiting bodies for spore dispersal). Reproduction: both **asexual** (spores, fragmentation) and **sexual** (fusion of hyphae → zygospore, ascospore, or basidiospore). Fungi are decomposers, form mycorrhizae with plants, and include pathogens like *Candida* and *Aspergillus*.

### 3.5 Beneficial vs. Pathogenic Microorganisms

**Beneficial roles**: decomposition (การย่อยสลาย), nitrogen fixation (การตรึงไนโตรเจน), food production (yogurt, cheese, bread, beer), biotechnology (recombinant DNA, enzyme production), gut microbiome (จุลินทรีย์ในลำไส้). **Pathogenic roles**: infectious diseases (tuberculosis, malaria, cholera, COVID-19), food spoilage. **Antibiotic resistance** (การดื้อยาปฏิชีวนะ) is a growing threat driven by overuse of antibiotics and natural selection in bacterial populations.

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Gram Stain Identification
> A bacterium stains purple with Gram stain. Describe its cell wall and predict its susceptibility to penicillin.

**Solution:**
1. Purple stain → **Gram-positive** (แกรมบวก)
2. Thick peptidoglycan layer in cell wall
3. Generally **more susceptible** to penicillin, which inhibits peptidoglycan synthesis

### Type 2: Viral Replication Cycle
> A virus integrates its DNA into the host chromosome and remains dormant for years. Which cycle is this? What can trigger it to become active?

**Solution:** This is the **lysogenic cycle** (วงจรไลโซจินิก). The integrated viral DNA (provirus) can be triggered to enter the lytic cycle by factors such as UV radiation, chemical mutagens, stress, or immune suppression in the host.

### Type 3: Disease Classification
> Classify the following as bacterial, viral, or fungal: tuberculosis, influenza, athlete's foot.

**Solution:**
- **Tuberculosis** (วัณโรค): bacterial (*Mycobacterium tuberculosis*)
- **Influenza** (ไข้หวัดใหญ่): viral (Influenza virus)
- **Athlete's foot** (โรคเชื้อราที่เท้า): fungal (*Trichophyton*)
