# Cell Biology — ชีววิทยาของเซลล์

> *"The cell is the basic unit of life."* — Theodor Schwann & Matthias Schleiden

Cell biology (ชีววิทยาเซลล์) explores the fundamental unit of all living organisms. All organisms are composed of one or more cells (เซลล์), which arise from pre-existing cells and contain hereditary information passed during cell division. The cell theory (ทฤษฎีเซลล์) established by Schleiden, Schwann, and Virchow forms the foundation of modern biology.

Prokaryotic cells (เซลล์โพรคาริโอต) such as bacteria (แบคทีเรีย) lack membrane-bound organelles, while eukaryotic cells (เซลล์ยูคาริโอต) found in plants, animals, fungi, and protists possess a true nucleus (นิวเคลียส) and numerous specialized organelles (ออร์แกเนลล์). Understanding cell structure is essential for studying metabolism, genetics, and disease.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Cell Theory (ทฤษฎีเซลล์)

Three fundamental principles:
1. All living organisms are composed of one or more cells.
2. The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.
3. All cells arise from pre-existing cells (*Omnis cellula e cellula*).

### 3.2 Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic Cells

| Feature | Prokaryote | Eukaryote |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | No true nucleus (nucleoid region) | Membrane-bound nucleus |
| DNA | Circular, naked | Linear, histone-bound chromosomes |
| Organelles | No membrane-bound organelles | Membrane-bound organelles |
| Size | Typically 1-10 µm | Typically 10-100 µm |
| Examples | Bacteria, Archaea | Animals, plants, fungi, protists |

### 3.3 Major Organelles and Their Functions

**Nucleus (นิวเคลียส):** The largest organelle, bounded by a double membrane called the nuclear envelope (เยื่อหุ้มนิวเคลียส). Contains chromatin (โครมาติน) — DNA complexed with histone proteins. The nucleolus (นิวคลีโอลัส) within the nucleus is the site of ribosomal RNA synthesis. Nuclear pores regulate transport of molecules between nucleus and cytoplasm.

**Mitochondria (ไมโทคอนเดรีย):** Double-membrane organelles responsible for aerobic cellular respiration (การหายใจระดับเซลล์). The inner membrane is highly folded into cristae (คริสเท), increasing surface area for the electron transport chain. Has its own DNA and ribosomes, supporting the endosymbiotic theory (ทฤษฎีเอนโดซิมไบโอติก).

**Chloroplast (คลอโรพลาสต์):** Found only in photosynthetic eukaryotes. Contains thylakoid membranes (เยื่อไทลาคอยด์) organized into grana (กรานา) where light reactions occur. The stroma (สโตรมา) is the site of the Calvin cycle. Also contains its own DNA.

**Endoplasmic Reticulum (เอ็นโดพลาสมิกเรติคิวลัม):** Rough ER (rER) is studded with ribosomes and synthesizes proteins for secretion or membrane insertion. Smooth ER (sER) synthesizes lipids, detoxifies drugs, and stores calcium ions.

**Golgi Apparatus (กลไกิแอปพาราตัส):** Stacks of flattened membrane sacs (cisternae) that modify, sort, and package proteins and lipids received from the ER. Produces lysosomes and secretory vesicles.

**Ribosomes (ไรโบโซม):** Composed of rRNA and protein; site of protein synthesis. Free ribosomes float in cytoplasm; bound ribosomes attach to rough ER.

**Lysosomes (ไลโซโซม):** Membrane-bound vesicles containing hydrolytic enzymes (pH about 5) for intracellular digestion, autophagy, and apoptosis.

**Vacuole (แวคิวโอล):** In plant cells, a large central vacuole maintains turgor pressure (แรงดันทอร์กอร์) and stores nutrients. Animal cells have smaller, more numerous vacuoles.

**Cell Wall (ผนังเซลล์):** Rigid structure outside the cell membrane in plants (cellulose), fungi (chitin), and bacteria (peptidoglycan). Provides structural support and protection.

**Cytoskeleton (ไซโทสเคลเลตอน):** Network of protein filaments including microtubules (ไมโครทิวบูล), microfilaments (ไมโครฟิลาเมนต์), and intermediate filaments. Maintains cell shape, enables movement, and organizes organelles.

### 3.4 Endosymbiotic Theory (ทฤษฎีเอนโดซิมไบโอติก)

Proposed by Lynn Margulis, this theory explains that mitochondria and chloroplasts were once free-living prokaryotes engulfed by an ancestral eukaryotic cell. Evidence includes:
- Both have their own circular DNA
- Both have double membranes
- Both have ribosomes similar to prokaryotic 70S ribosomes
- Both replicate by binary fission

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Identifying Cell Types
> A cell under a microscope has a nucleus, mitochondria, and a cell wall but no chloroplasts. Is it a plant or animal cell? What type could it be?

**Solution:** The presence of a cell wall (ผนังเซลล์) rules out animal cells. Without chloroplasts (คลอโรพลาสต์), it cannot be a photosynthetic plant cell. This is likely a **fungal cell** (เซลล์เชื้อรา) — which has a cell wall (chitin), nucleus, mitochondria, but no chloroplasts.

### Type 2: Organelle Function Matching
> Which organelle would be most abundant in a cell that secretes large amounts of protein (e.g., pancreatic cell)?

**Solution:** A secretory cell requires extensive **rough ER** (for protein synthesis), **Golgi apparatus** (for packaging), and **mitochondria** (for energy). The rough ER and Golgi would be most prominent.
