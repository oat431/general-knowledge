# Atomic Structure — โครงสร้างอะตอม

> *"The atom is the smallest unit of ordinary matter that forms a chemical element. Everything around us is made of atoms."* — Richard Feynman

Atomic structure (โครงสร้างอะตอม) is the fundamental building block of chemistry. It explains the components inside the atom (อะตอม): protons (โปรตอน), neutrons (นิวตรอน), and electrons (อิเล็กตรอน), as well as the arrangement of electrons in different energy levels (ระดับพลังงาน). This is the foundation for predicting the properties of elements (ธาตุ) and their chemical behavior (พฤติกรรมทางเคมี).

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Subatomic Particles

An atom (อะตอม) consists of three main subatomic particles (อนุภาคมูลฐาน):

| Particle | Charge (C) | Mass (kg) | Mass (u) | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proton ($p^+$) | $+1.602 \times 10^{-19}$ | $1.673 \times 10^{-27}$ | 1.0073 | Nucleus (นิวเคลียส) |
| Neutron ($n^0$) | $0$ | $1.675 \times 10^{-27}$ | 1.0087 | Nucleus (นิวเคลียส) |
| Electron ($e^-$) | $-1.602 \times 10^{-19}$ | $9.109 \times 10^{-31}$ | 0.00055 | Around nucleus (รอบนิวเคลียส) |

The relationship between the atomic number (เลขอะตอม) and the mass number (เลขมวล):

$$A = Z + N$$

### 3.2 Isotopes

Isotopes (ไอโซโทป) are atoms of the same element that have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons, for example:

- $\ce{^1_1H}$ (protium) — 0 neutrons
- $\ce{^2_1H}$ (deuterium) — 1 neutron
- $\ce{^3_1H}$ (tritium) — 2 neutrons

Average atomic mass (มวลอะตอมเฉลี่ย):

$$\bar{A} = \sum (\text{fractional abundance} \times \text{isotope mass})$$

### 3.3 Quantum Numbers

Each electron in an atom is described by four quantum numbers (เลขควอนตัม 4 ชุด):

| Quantum | Symbol | Values | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Principal | $n$ | $1, 2, 3, ...$ | Main energy level (ระดับพลังงานหลัก), orbital size |
| Azimuthal | $l$ | $0$ ถึง $n-1$ | Shape (s=0, p=1, d=2, f=3) |
| Magnetic | $m_l$ | $-l$ ถึง $+l$ | Orientation in space |
| Spin | $m_s$ | $+\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}$ | Intrinsic spin (การหมุนรอบตัวเอง) |

### 3.4 Orbital Shapes

- **s-orbital** ($l=0$): Spherical (ทรงกลม)
- **p-orbital** ($l=1$): Dumbbell shape with 3 orientations ($p_x, p_y, p_z$) (รูปดัมเบลล์ 3 ทิศทาง)
- **d-orbital** ($l=2$): 5 orbitals with complex shapes (รูปทรงซับซ้อน)
- **f-orbital** ($l=3$): 7 orbitals, found in lanthanides/actinides (ธาตุแลนทาไนด์/แอกทิไนด์)

### 3.5 Electron Configuration

**Principles of electron configuration (หลักการจัดเรียงอิเล็กตรอน):**

1. **Aufbau principle**: Electrons fill lower-energy orbitals first, following the order $1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p, 5s, 4d, 5p, 6s, 4f, 5d, 6p, 7s, 5f, 6d, 7p$

2. **Pauli exclusion principle**: Each orbital can hold a maximum of 2 electrons with opposite spins (สปินตรงข้าม)

3. **Hund's rule**: Electrons occupy equal-energy orbitals singly (one at a time) before pairing, to minimize repulsion (กระจายตัวก่อนจับคู่เพื่อลดแรงผลัก)

**Example (ตัวอย่าง):**

$$\ce{Na}: 1s^2\, 2s^2\, 2p^6\, 3s^1 \quad \text{(11 e)}$$

$$\ce{Fe}: 1s^2\, 2s^2\, 2p^6\, 3s^2\, 3p^6\, 4s^2\, 3d^6$$

### 3.6 Orbital Diagrams

Use the arrow symbols $\uparrow$ and $\downarrow$ to represent electrons:

- $\ce{N}$ (7 e⁻): $[\ce{He}]\,2s^2\,2p^3$
  - $2s$: $\uparrow\downarrow$
  - $2p$: $\uparrow\, \uparrow\, \uparrow$ (Hund's rule — distributed singly (กระจายตัว))

### 3.7 Aufbau Diagram (Energy Filling Order)

Order of electron filling (ลำดับการเติมอิเล็กตรอน) (easy mnemonic: diagonal arrows from top to bottom (ทแยงบนลงล่าง)):

$$1s \to 2s \to 2p \to 3s \to 3p \to 4s \to 3d \to 4p \to 5s \to 4d \to 5p \to 6s \to 4f \to 5d \to 6p$$

### 3.8 Valence Electrons

The outermost electrons (อิเล็กตรอนวงนอกสุด) determine the chemical properties of an element:

- Metals (โลหะ): 1–3 valence electrons
- Nonmetals (อโลหะ): 4–7 valence electrons
- Noble gases (แก๊สมีตระกูล): 8 valence electrons (except He = 2)

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Finding Atomic Number and Mass Number

> Element $\ce{X}$ has 17 protons and 18 neutrons. Find the atomic number, mass number, and identify the element.

**Solution:**

- Atomic number $Z = 17$ → Chlorine ($\ce{Cl}$)
- Mass number $A = Z + N = 17 + 18 = 35$
- Nuclear symbol: $\ce{^35_17Cl}$

### Type 2: Writing Electron Configuration

> Write the electron configuration of $\ce{Ca}$ (Z=20).

**Solution:**

$$\ce{Ca}: 1s^2\, 2s^2\, 2p^6\, 3s^2\, 3p^6\, 4s^2$$

Or use noble gas shorthand: $[\ce{Ar}]\, 4s^2$

### Type 3: Counting Unpaired Electrons

> How many unpaired electrons (อิเล็กตรอนที่ไม่มีคู่) does $\ce{Fe}$ have?

**Solution:**

$\ce{Fe}$ (Z=26): $[\ce{Ar}]\, 4s^2\, 3d^6$

- $4s^2$: $\uparrow\downarrow$ (paired)
- $3d^6$: $\uparrow\downarrow\, \uparrow\downarrow\, \uparrow\, \uparrow\, \uparrow$ → **4 unpaired electrons (ตัวที่ไม่จับคู่)**

### Type 4: Calculating Average Atomic Mass

> $\ce{Cl}$ has 2 isotopes: $\ce{^35Cl}$ (75.77%) and $\ce{^37Cl}$ (24.23%). Find the average atomic mass.

**Solution:**

$$\bar{A} = (0.7577 \times 35) + (0.2423 \times 37) = 35.48 \,\text{u}$$
