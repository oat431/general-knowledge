# Organic Compounds by Functional Group — สารประกอบอินทรีย์ตามหมู่ฟังก์ชัน

> *"Functional groups are the heart of organic chemistry — they determine a molecule's reactivity, properties, and nomenclature."* — Jonathan Clayden

A **functional group (หมู่ฟังก์ชัน)** is a group of atoms that determines the chemical and physical properties of an organic compound. Classifying compounds by functional group makes it much easier to predict their behavior. This note covers the seven main functional groups: alcohols (แอลกอฮอล์), aldehydes (แอลดีไฮด์), ketones (คีโตน), carboxylic acids (กรดคาร์บอกซิลิก), esters (เอสเทอร์), amines (เอมีน), and amides (เอไมด์).

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Alcohols (แอลกอฮอล์) — $\ce{R-OH}$

**General formula:** $\ce{C_nH_{2n+1}OH}$ or $\ce{R-OH}$.

**Types by position of the OH group:**
- **1° (ปฐมภูมิ, primary):** $\ce{R-CH2-OH}$ (OH on a C bonded to 1 other C).
- **2° (ทุติยภูมิ, secondary):** $\ce{R2CH-OH}$ (OH on a C bonded to 2 other C).
- **3° (ตติยภูมิ, tertiary):** $\ce{R3C-OH}$ (OH on a C bonded to 3 other C).

**IUPAC:** alkan- + **ol** (e.g. ethanol = $\ce{CH3CH2OH}$).

**Properties:**
- High bp (H-bonding).
- Miscible with water at low carbon count.
- Form H-bonds between molecules.

**Key reactions:**
1. **Oxidation:**
   - 1° alcohol → aldehyde → carboxylic acid
   - 2° alcohol → ketone
   - 3° alcohol → not easily oxidized

$$\ce{CH3CH2OH ->[Cu, 325°C] CH3CHO ->[Cu, 325°C] CH3COOH}$$

$$\ce{CH3CH(OH)CH3 ->[Cu, 325°C] CH3COCH3}$$

2. **Dehydration (E1):** $\ce{R-OH ->[H2SO4, 170°C] Alkene + H2O}$
3. **Esterification:** $\ce{R-OH + R'COOH -> R'COOR + H2O}$ (catalyzed by H⁺)

### 3.2 Aldehydes (แอลดีไฮด์) — $\ce{R-CHO}$

**Formula:** $\ce{R-CHO}$, IUPAC name ends in **-al**.

**Properties:**
- The $\ce{-CHO}$ group is at the end of the chain.
- bp falls between those of alcohols and hydrocarbons.
- Some have a characteristic odor.

**Key reactions:**
1. **Oxidation** (Tollens' / Fehling's test):
   - Tollens' ($\ce{Ag(NH3)2OH}$) → silver mirror.
   - Fehling's → brick-red $\ce{Cu2O}$.

$$\ce{R-CHO + 2Ag(NH3)2OH -> R-COONH4 + 2Ag + 3NH3 + H2O}$$

2. **Reduction** → 1° alcohol:

$$\ce{R-CHO + H2 ->[Ni] R-CH2-OH}$$

3. **Addition of HCN** → cyanohydrin.

### 3.3 Ketones (คีโตน) — $\ce{R-CO-R'}$

**Formula:** $\ce{R-CO-R'}$, IUPAC name ends in **-one**.

**Properties:**
- The C=O group is internal (not at the end of the chain).
- Not easily oxidized (Tollens', Fehling's: negative).
- Used as solvents (acetone).

**Key reactions:**
1. **Reduction** → 2° alcohol:

$$\ce{R-CO-R' + H2 ->[Ni] R-CH(OH)-R'}$$

2. **Addition** of $\ce{HCN}$, $\ce{NH2OH}$ (oxime), $\ce{2,4-DNP}$.
3. **Iodoform test:** ketones that contain $\ce{-COCH3}$ (e.g. acetone) give a positive result.

### 3.4 Carboxylic Acids (กรดคาร์บอกซิลิก) — $\ce{R-COOH}$

**Formula:** $\ce{R-COOH}$, IUPAC name ends in **-oic acid**.

**Properties:**
- Very high bp (H-bonding as dimers).
- Weak acids ($K_a \approx 10^{-5}$).

$$\ce{R-COOH <=> R-COO^- + H^+}$$

**Key reactions:**
1. **With bases:** $\ce{R-COOH + NaOH -> R-COONa + H2O}$
2. **With metals:** $\ce{2R-COOH + Zn -> (R-COO)2Zn + H2}$
3. **With alcohols:** form an ester (see 3.5).
4. **With $\ce{NaHCO3}$:** releases $\ce{CO2}$.

### 3.5 Esters (เอสเทอร์) — $\ce{R-COOR'}$

**Formula:** $\ce{R-COO-R'}$, IUPAC name ends in **-oate**.

**Properties:**
- Pleasant, fruity smell.
- Lower bp than the parent acid.
- Insoluble in water.

**Key reactions:**
1. **Acid hydrolysis:** $\ce{R-COOR' + H2O <=> R-COOH + R'-OH}$
2. **Saponification (base hydrolysis):** $\ce{R-COOR' + NaOH -> R-COONa + R'-OH}$

### 3.6 Amines (เอมีน) — $\ce{R-NH2}$

**Types:**
- **1°:** $\ce{R-NH2}$ (1 alkyl group)
- **2°:** $\ce{R2-NH}$ (2 alkyl groups)
- **3°:** $\ce{R3-N}$ (3 alkyl groups)

**Properties:**
- Basic (the N has a lone pair).

$$\ce{R-NH2 + H2O <=> R-NH3^+ + OH^-}$$

- bp falls between those of alcohols and hydrocarbons.
- 1° amines smell fishy.

**Key reactions:**
1. **With acids:** $\ce{R-NH2 + HCl -> R-NH3^+ Cl^-}$

### 3.7 Amides (เอไมด์) — $\ce{R-CONH2}$

**Formula:** $\ce{R-CO-NH2}$, IUPAC name ends in **-amide**.

**Properties:**
- Very weak bases.
- Found in proteins (peptide bonds, พันธะเปปไทด์).
- Strong H-bonds (high bp).

**Reactions:** hydrolysis → acid + amine.

$$\ce{R-CONH2 + H2O -> R-COOH + R-NH2}$$

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Identifying a Functional Group

> Which of the following is an alcohol: $\ce{CH3CHO, CH3CH2OH, CH3OCH3, CH3COOH}$?

**Solution:** only $\ce{CH3CH2OH}$ (ethanol) has an -OH group attached to a saturated C.

### Type 2: IUPAC Naming

> What is the name of $\ce{CH3-CH(OH)-CH3}$?

**Solution:** propan-2-ol (propan-2-ol) or isopropanol.

### Type 3: Predicting the Product of a Reaction

> $\ce{CH3COOH + CH3OH ->[H+] ?}$

**Solution:** $\ce{CH3COOCH3 + H2O}$ (methyl acetate).

### Type 4: Distinguishing Aldehydes from Ketones

> Substance A gives a silver mirror with Tollens' reagent — is it an aldehyde or a ketone?

**Solution:** **Aldehyde** (ketones do not react with Tollens' reagent).
