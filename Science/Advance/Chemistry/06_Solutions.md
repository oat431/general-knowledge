# Solutions — สารละลาย

> *"In solution, chemistry comes alive. Solutes meet solvents, and the dance of molecules begins."* — Anonymous

A solution (สารละลาย) is a homogeneous mixture of two or more substances, consisting of a solute (ตัวถูกละลาย) and a solvent (ตัวทำละลาย). Understanding concentration, dilution, and colligative properties explains many chemical and everyday phenomena, such as freezing, boiling, and osmosis.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Components of a Solution (องค์ประกอบของสารละลาย)

**Solution (สารละลาย):** A homogeneous (เนื้อเดียว) mixture of two or more substances.

- **Solute (ตัวถูกละลาย):** The substance that is dissolved, present in smaller amount (มีปริมาณน้อยกว่า)
- **Solvent (ตัวทำละลาย):** The substance that dissolves the other, present in larger amount (มีปริมาณมากกว่า)

**Example (ตัวอย่าง):** Salt water = $\ce{NaCl}$ (solute) + $\ce{H2O}$ (solvent)

### 3.2 Solubility (ความสามารถในการละลาย)

The maximum amount of solute that can dissolve in a solvent at a given temperature.

**Factors affecting solubility (ปัจจัยที่มีผล):**
- Nature of solute/solvent ("Like dissolves like") (ธรรมชาติของ solute/solvent)
- Temperature — most solid solutes dissolve better at higher T; gases dissolve less at higher T (อุณหภูมิ)
- Pressure — important for gases: Henry's Law (ความดัน สำคัญสำหรับแก๊ส)

**Henry's Law:**

$$C = k_H \cdot P$$

The concentration of a gas in solution is directly proportional to its partial pressure (ความเข้มข้นของแก๊สในสารละลายแปรผันตรงกับความดันย่อย).

### 3.3 Molarity (M) (ความเข้มข้นเชิงโมล)

$$M = \frac{n \text{ (mol solute)}}{V \text{ (L solution)}}$$

Unit: mol/L or M (e.g., 1.5 M)

**Advantage (ข้อดี):** Easy to use in the laboratory.
**Disadvantage (ข้อเสีย):** Changes with temperature (because V changes).

### 3.4 Molality (m) (ความเข้มข้นเชิงโมลัล)

$$m = \frac{n \text{ (mol solute)}}{\text{mass of solvent (kg)}}$$

Unit: mol/kg

**Advantage (ข้อดี):** Temperature-independent → used in colligative properties.

### 3.5 Percent Concentration (ร้อยละความเข้มข้น)

**Three main forms (3 แบบหลัก):**

| Type | Formula | Used for (ใช้กับ) |
|---|---|---|
| $\% w/w$ | $\frac{m_{\text{solute}}}{m_{\text{solution}}} \times 100$ | Solid in solid (ของแข็งในของแข็ง) |
| $\% v/v$ | $\frac{V_{\text{solute}}}{V_{\text{solution}}} \times 100$ | Liquid in liquid (ของเหลวในของเหลว) |
| $\% w/v$ | $\frac{m_{\text{solute}} \text{(g)}}{V_{\text{solution}} \text{(mL)}} \times 100$ | Solid in liquid (ของแข็งในของเหลว) |

### 3.6 ppm and ppb (ส่วนในล้านส่วน)

Used for very low concentrations.

$$\text{ppm} = \frac{m_{\text{solute}}}{m_{\text{solution}}} \times 10^6$$

$$\text{ppb} = \frac{m_{\text{solute}}}{m_{\text{solution}}} \times 10^9$$

**Example (ตัวอย่าง):** $\ce{Pb}$ content in water = 2 ppm → 2 mg/L

### 3.7 Dilution (การเจือจาง)

Adding solvent → moles of solute stay the same, but V increases.

$$\boxed{M_1V_1 = M_2V_2}$$

where $M_1, V_1$ are before dilution and $M_2, V_2$ are after dilution.

### 3.8 Colligative Properties (สมบัติคอลลิเกทีฟ)

Properties that depend on the **number of solute particles** (จำนวนอนุภาค solute), not their identity.

**Four main types (4 ประเภทหลัก):**

#### 3.8.1 Vapor Pressure Lowering (การลดลงของความดันไอ)

$$\Delta P = P^0 \cdot x_{\text{solute}}$$

where $x_{\text{solute}}$ = mole fraction of solute (Raoult's Law).

#### 3.8.2 Boiling Point Elevation (การเพิ่มของจุดเดือด)

$$\Delta T_b = K_b \cdot m$$

where $K_b$ = ebullioscopic constant (depends on solvent), $m$ = molality.

**Values of $K_b$:**
- Water: $0.512 \,°\text{C·kg/mol}$
- Benzene: $2.53$
- Ethanol: $1.22$

#### 3.8.3 Freezing Point Depression (การลดลงของจุดเยือกแข็ง)

$$\Delta T_f = K_f \cdot m$$

**Values of $K_f$:**
- Water: $1.86 \,°\text{C·kg/mol}$
- Benzene: $5.12$
- Camphor: $40$

#### 3.8.4 Osmotic Pressure ($\pi$) (ความดันออสโมติก)

$$\pi = MRT$$

where $M$ = molarity, $R = 0.0821 \,\text{L·atm/(mol·K)}$, $T$ = temperature (K).

**van't Hoff Factor ($i$):** If the solute dissociates (e.g. $\ce{NaCl -> Na^+ + Cl^-}$), use $i$.

$$\Delta T_f = i \cdot K_f \cdot m$$

| Solute | $i$ (ideal) | $i$ (observed) |
|---|---|---|
| Non-electrolyte (e.g. sugar, น้ำตาล) | 1 | 1 |
| $\ce{NaCl}$ | 2 | ~1.9 |
| $\ce{CaCl2}$ | 3 | ~2.7 |
| $\ce{AlCl3}$ | 4 | ~3.8 |

### 3.9 Osmosis & Osmotic Pressure

**Osmosis:** Water moves through a semipermeable membrane from low to high solute concentration (น้ำผ่านเยื่อเลือกผ่านจากความเข้มข้นต่ำไปสูง).

**Three types of tonicity (3 ประเภทของ tonicity):**
- **Isotonic:** Equal concentrations, no net flow
- **Hypotonic:** Lower solute outside → water flows in (cell lysis) (เซลล์แตก)
- **Hypertonic:** Higher solute outside → water flows out (cell shrivels) (เซลล์เหี่ยว)

### 3.10 Electrolytes vs Non-electrolytes

| Type | Conductivity (การนำไฟฟ้า) | Example | van't Hoff $i$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong electrolyte | High (ดี) | $\ce{NaCl, HCl, NaOH}$ | Number of ions (จำนวนไอออน) |
| Weak electrolyte | Low (น้อย) | $\ce{CH3COOH, NH3}$ | $1 < i <$ number of ions |
| Non-electrolyte | None (ไม่นำ) | $\ce{C12H22O11, urea}$ | 1 |

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Calculating Molarity

> 8.0 g of $\ce{NaOH}$ in 500 mL of water. Find the molarity.

**Solution:**

$$M_{\ce{NaOH}} = 40.00 \,\text{g/mol}$$

$$n = 8.0 / 40.00 = 0.20 \,\text{mol}$$

$$M = 0.20 / 0.500 = 0.40 \,\text{M}$$

### Type 2: Dilution

> Dilute 6.0 M $\ce{HCl}$ to 0.50 M in a final volume of 250 mL. How many mL of the stock $\ce{HCl}$ are needed?

**Solution:**

$$M_1V_1 = M_2V_2 \Rightarrow 6.0 \times V_1 = 0.50 \times 250$$

$$V_1 = \frac{0.50 \times 250}{6.0} = 20.8 \,\text{mL}$$

### Type 3: Boiling Point Elevation

> 34.2 g of sugar ($\ce{C12H22O11}$) in 500 g of water. Find the new boiling point ($K_b$ of water = 0.512).

**Solution:**

$$M_{\ce{C12H22O11}} = 342.3 \,\text{g/mol}$$

$$n = 34.2 / 342.3 = 0.100 \,\text{mol}$$

$$m = 0.100 / 0.500 = 0.200 \,\text{mol/kg}$$

$$\Delta T_b = 0.512 \times 0.200 = 0.1024 \,°\text{C}$$

$$T_b = 100 + 0.1024 = 100.10 \,°\text{C}$$

### Type 4: Freezing Point Depression

> 11.1 g of $\ce{CaCl2}$ in 250 g of water. Find the freezing point ($K_f$ of water = 1.86, $i = 2.7$).

**Solution:**

$$M_{\ce{CaCl2}} = 111.0, \quad n = 0.100 \,\text{mol}$$

$$m = 0.100 / 0.250 = 0.400 \,\text{mol/kg}$$

$$\Delta T_f = 2.7 \times 1.86 \times 0.400 = 2.01 \,°\text{C}$$

$$T_f = 0 - 2.01 = -2.01 \,°\text{C}$$

### Type 5: Osmotic Pressure

> What is $\pi$ for a 0.10 M solution at 25°C?

**Solution:**

$$\pi = MRT = 0.10 \times 0.0821 \times 298 = 2.45 \,\text{atm}$$

### Type 6: Empirical/Molecular Formula from % Composition

> A compound is 52.2% C, 13.0% H, 34.8% O and has molar mass 46 g/mol. Find its molecular formula.

**Solution:** (same as stoichiometry)
- mol C = 52.2/12 = 4.35, H = 13.0/1 = 13.0, O = 34.8/16 = 2.175
- Divide by 2.175 → C:H:O = 2:6:1
- Empirical = $\ce{C2H6O}$ (mass 46)
- $n = 46/46 = 1$ → **Molecular = $\ce{C2H6O}$** (ethanol)
