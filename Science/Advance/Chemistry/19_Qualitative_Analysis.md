# Qualitative Analysis — การวิเคราะห์เชิงคุณภาพ

> *"In qualitative analysis, we identify substances by their characteristic reactions — the chemical fingerprints nature left behind."* — Classic Analytical Chemistry

**Qualitative analysis (การวิเคราะห์เชิงคุณภาพ)** is the identification of the kind of species (ions/compounds) present in a sample, using specific reactions that give characteristic colors, precipitates, or gases. This note covers flame tests, precipitation reactions, solubility rules, and systematic cation analysis.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Flame Test (การทดสอบเปลวไฟ)

A clean **platinum wire (ลวดแพลทินัม)** is dipped in the solution, then held in a Bunsen flame; the resulting color is observed.

**Flame colors of metals (สีเปลวไฟของโลหะ):**

|| Ion | Flame color |
||---|---|
|| $\ce{Li^+}$ | crimson red (แดง) |
|| $\ce{Na^+}$ | intense yellow (เหลือง) |
|| $\ce{K^+}$ | violet (ม่วง), view through cobalt glass |
|| $\ce{Ca^{2+}}$ | brick red (ส้มแดง) |
|| $\ce{Sr^{2+}}$ | crimson red (แดง) |
|| $\ce{Ba^{2+}}$ | apple green (เขียวอ่อน) |
|| $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ | turquoise (เขียว/ฟ้า) |

**Principle:** heat excites electrons to higher energy levels → they fall back and release the energy as light of a characteristic color.

### 3.2 Solubility Rules (กฎการละลาย)

**Soluble compounds (สารที่ละลายน้ำได้):**
- Almost all **Group 1** salts ($\ce{Na^+, K^+, Li^+}$) and **$\ce{NH4^+}$** salts.
- Almost all **$\ce{NO3^-}$** (nitrate) salts.
- Almost all **$\ce{CH3COO^-}$** (acetate) salts.
- Almost all **$\ce{Cl^-, Br^-, I^-}$** except $\ce{Ag^+, Pb^{2+}, Hg2^{2+}}$.
- Almost all **$\ce{SO4^{2-}}$** except $\ce{Ba^{2+}, Pb^{2+}, Ca^{2+}, Sr^{2+}}$.

**Insoluble compounds (สารที่ไม่ละลายน้ำ):**
- Almost all **$\ce{CO3^{2-}, PO4^{3-}, S^{2-}, OH^-}$** except Group 1 and $\ce{NH4^+}$.
- Almost all **$\ce{O^{2-}}$** except Group 1 and $\ce{Ca^{2+}}$ (sparingly soluble).

### 3.3 Systematic Cation Analysis (การวิเคราะห์ Cation เป็นกลุ่ม)

**Group reagents used to separate cations:**

|| Group | Cations | Group reagent | Precipitate |
||---|---|---|---|
|| **1** | $\ce{Ag^+, Pb^{2+}, Hg2^{2+}}$ | dilute $\ce{HCl}$ | chlorides |
|| **2** | $\ce{Cu^{2+}, Pb^{2+}, Bi^{3+}, Cd^{2+}, Hg^{2+}, As^{3+}}$ | $\ce{H2S}$ in dilute $\ce{HCl}$ | sulfides |
|| **3** | $\ce{Fe^{3+}, Al^{3+}, Cr^{3+}}$ | $\ce{NH4OH}$ in $\ce{NH4Cl}$ | hydroxides |
|| **4** | $\ce{Co^{2+}, Ni^{2+}, Mn^{2+}, Zn^{2+}}$ | $\ce{H2S}$ in $\ce{NH4OH}$ | sulfides |
|| **5** | $\ce{Ba^{2+}, Sr^{2+}, Ca^{2+}, Mg^{2+}}$ | $\ce{(NH4)2CO3}$ | carbonates |
|| **6** (remaining) | $\ce{Na^+, K^+, NH4^+}$ | no precipitate | — |

### 3.4 Anion Tests (การทดสอบ Anion)

|| Anion | Reagent | Positive result |
||---|---|---|
|| $\ce{CO3^{2-}}$ | dilute acid | bubbles of $\ce{CO2}$ |
|| $\ce{SO4^{2-}}$ | $\ce{BaCl2}$ + $\ce{HCl}$ | white precipitate of $\ce{BaSO4}$ |
|| $\ce{Cl^-}$ | $\ce{AgNO3}$ + $\ce{HNO3}$ | white precipitate of $\ce{AgCl}$ |
|| $\ce{Br^-}$ | $\ce{AgNO3}$ + $\ce{HNO3}$ | pale yellow precipitate of $\ce{AgBr}$ |
|| $\ce{I^-}$ | $\ce{AgNO3}$ + $\ce{HNO3}$ | yellow precipitate of $\ce{AgI}$ |
|| $\ce{NO3^-}$ | brown ring test | brown ring |
|| $\ce{S^{2-}}$ | lead acetate paper | turns black |

### 3.5 Example Precipitation Reactions

$$\ce{AgNO3 + NaCl -> AgCl (s) + NaNO3}$$

$$\ce{BaCl2 + Na2SO4 -> BaSO4 (s) + 2NaCl}$$

$$\ce{Pb(NO3)2 + 2KI -> PbI2 (s) + 2KNO3}$$

$$\ce{CuSO4 + NaOH -> Cu(OH)2 (s) + Na2SO4}$$

### 3.6 Biomolecule Tests (การทดสอบชีวโมเลกุล)

|| Substance | Reagent | Positive result |
||---|---|---|
|| **Starch** | Iodine ($\ce{I2}$) | deep blue (น้ำเงินเข้ม) |
|| **Reducing sugar** (e.g. glucose) | Benedict's / Fehling's | orange-red precipitate $\ce{Cu2O}$ |
|| **Protein** | Biuret reagent | purple (ม่วง) |
|| **Lipid** | Sudan III | red (แดง) |

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Predicting a Precipitate

> Mixing $\ce{AgNO3}$ with $\ce{NaCl}$ — what happens?

**Solution:** $\ce{Ag+ + Cl- -> AgCl (s)}$ (white precipitate).

### Type 2: Separating Group-1 Cations

> A solution contains $\ce{Ag^+, Cu^{2+}, Fe^{3+}}$. Which reagent removes the Group-1 cation?

**Solution:** Add **dilute $\ce{HCl}$** → $\ce{Ag+}$ precipitates as $\ce{AgCl}$ (filter off).

### Type 3: Flame Test

> A substance gives an intense yellow color in a flame. Which ion is present?

**Solution:** $\ce{Na^+}$ (intense yellow).

### Type 4: Anion Test

> Substance A + dilute $\ce{HNO3}$ → colorless gas that turns limewater cloudy. What is A?

**Solution:** $\ce{CO3^{2-}}$ (releases $\ce{CO2}$ → $\ce{Ca(OH)2 + CO2 -> CaCO3 + H2O}$).

### Type 5: Reducing-Sugar Test

> Solution X + Benedict's reagent → orange-red precipitate. What is X?

**Solution:** A **reducing sugar** (e.g. glucose, fructose, maltose — but not sucrose).
