# Industrial and Applied Chemistry — เคมีอุตสาหกรรมและประยุกต์

> *"The chemical industry transforms raw materials into the products that modern life depends on — from fertilizers to fuels to pharmaceuticals."* — John H. Perry

**Industrial chemistry (เคมีอุตสาหกรรม)** applies chemical knowledge to the commercial production of important chemicals such as ammonia, sulfuric acid, nitric acid, metals, and fuels. This note also covers environmental chemistry (เคมีสิ่งแวดล้อม) — pollution, the greenhouse effect, the ozone layer, and wastewater treatment.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Haber Process (กระบวนการฮาเบอร์) — Production of $\ce{NH3}$

$$\ce{N2 (g) + 3H2 (g) <=> 2NH3 (g)} \quad \Delta H = -92\ \text{kJ/mol}$$

**Optimum conditions (สภาวะที่เหมาะสม):**
- Temperature: **400–500 °C** (compromise between equilibrium and kinetics).
- Pressure: **200–300 atm**.
- Catalyst: **Fe (iron)** promoted with $\ce{K2O, Al2O3, CaO}$.
- Conversion per pass ≈ 15–20% → unreacted gases are recycled.

**Le Chatelier's principle:** low temperature and high pressure favor $\ce{NH3}$, but the rate is too slow → a catalyst is used.

### 3.2 Contact Process (กระบวนการคอนแทค) — Production of $\ce{H2SO4}$

**Three steps:**

1. Burn sulfur → $\ce{SO2}$:

$$\ce{S + O2 -> SO2}$$

2. $\ce{SO2 -> SO3}$ (catalyst $\ce{V2O5}$, 400–450 °C):

$$\ce{2SO2 + O2 <=> 2SO3} \quad \Delta H = -198\ \text{kJ/mol}$$

3. Absorb $\ce{SO3}$ in 98% $\ce{H2SO4}$ to form oleum, then dilute with water:

$$\ce{SO3 + H2O -> H2SO4} \quad \text{(in practice, oleum is used)}$$

### 3.3 Ostwald Process (กระบวนการออสต์วาลด์) — Production of $\ce{HNO3}$

**Three steps:**

1. Oxidation of $\ce{NH3}$ (Pt–Rh catalyst, 900 °C):

$$\ce{4NH3 + 5O2 -> 4NO + 6H2O}$$

2. $\ce{NO -> NO2}$:

$$\ce{2NO + O2 -> 2NO2}$$

3. Absorption in water:

$$\ce{3NO2 + H2O -> 2HNO3 + NO}$$

### 3.4 Metallurgy (โลหกรรม)

#### (a) Iron (เหล็ก)
**Blast furnace (เตา Blast Furnace):**

$$\ce{Fe2O3 + 3CO -> 2Fe + 3CO2} \quad \text{(reduction)}$$

Process: charge ore $\ce{Fe2O3}$ + coke ($\ce{C}$) + limestone ($\ce{CaCO3}$) → reduce with $\ce{CO}$ → pig iron.

#### (b) Aluminum (อะลูมิเนียม) — Hall–Héroult
**Electrolysis** of molten $\ce{Al2O3}$ dissolved in cryolite ($\ce{Na3AlF6}$).

$$\ce{2Al2O3 ->[electrolysis] 4Al + 3O2}$$

Cathode: $\ce{Al^{3+} + 3e- -> Al}$, Anode: $\ce{2O^{2-} -> O2 + 4e-}$

#### (c) Copper (ทองแดง)
- **Froth flotation** → **Roasting** → **Smelting** → **Electrorefining**.

### 3.5 Petrochemicals (ปิโตรเคมี)

**Fractional distillation of crude oil (การกลั่นน้ำมันดิบ):**

|| Fraction | bp range (°C) | Use |
||---|---|---|
|| Petroleum gas (ก๊าซปิโตรเลียม) | < 40 | fuel, plastic feedstock |
|| Gasoline / naphtha (น้ำมันเบนซิน) | 40–200 | car fuel |
|| Kerosene (น้ำมันก๊าด) | 200–300 | jet fuel |
|| Diesel (น้ำมันดีเซล) | 250–350 | truck fuel |
|| Lubricating oil (น้ำมันหล่อลื่น) | 300–400 | lubrication |
|| Asphalt / bitumen (ยางมะตอย/บิทูเมน) | > 400 | road paving |

**Cracking:** breaks large molecules into smaller ones (catalytic cracking or steam cracking).

### 3.6 Environmental Chemistry (เคมีสิ่งแวดล้อม)

#### (a) Air Pollution (มลพิษทางอากาศ)
- $\ce{CO2}$ → greenhouse effect, climate change.
- $\ce{SO2, NOx}$ → **acid rain** (ฝนกรด).
- $\ce{CFC}$ (chlorofluorocarbons) → ozone depletion.
- PM 2.5, ground-level ozone.

#### (b) Greenhouse Effect (ปรากฏการณ์เรือนกระจก)
$\ce{CO2, CH4, N2O, H2O}$ absorb IR radiation → global warming.

**Carbon footprint:** measure of $\ce{CO2}$ emissions; aim to reduce it.

#### (c) Ozone Layer (ชั้นโอโซน)
$\ce{O3}$ in the stratosphere absorbs UV radiation.
**CFC + UV → Cl radical → destroys $\ce{O3}$:**

$$\ce{Cl + O3 -> ClO + O2}$$
$$\ce{ClO + O -> Cl + O2}$$

#### (d) Wastewater Treatment (การบำบัดน้ำเสีย)

**Three stages:**
1. **Primary:** settle out and filter solids.
2. **Secondary:** biological breakdown by microorganisms.
3. **Tertiary:** chlorination, ozone, UV, or reverse osmosis.

**BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand):** an indicator of water pollution.

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Haber-Process Yield

> $\ce{N2}$ 14 g + $\ce{H2}$ 6 g → what is the maximum mass of $\ce{NH3}$?

**Solution:**
- mol $\ce{N2}$ = 0.5, mol $\ce{H2}$ = 3
- Ratio 1:3 → $\ce{N2}$ runs out when 1.5 mol of $\ce{H2}$ is consumed, leaving 1.5 mol
- mol $\ce{NH3}$ = 1 → m = **17 g**

### Type 2: Choosing Process Conditions

> The Contact process uses $\ce{V2O5}$ as a catalyst at what temperature?

**Solution:** 400–450 °C (balance between yield and kinetics).

### Type 3: Yield from Distillation

> 1000 kg of crude oil is distilled; the gasoline fraction is 25%. How many kg of gasoline?

**Solution:** 1000 × 0.25 = **250 kg**.

### Type 4: Pollution Analysis

> Acid rain has pH 4.0. What is $[\ce{H^+}]$?

**Solution:** $[\ce{H^+}] = 10^{-4} = 10^{-4}$ M (normal rain has pH ≈ 5.6).
