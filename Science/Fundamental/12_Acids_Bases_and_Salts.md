# Acids, Bases, and Salts — กรด เบส เกลือ

> *"Chemistry is the study of matter, but I prefer to see it as the study of change."* — Walter White

This note covers acids, bases, pH, neutralization, and salts. Students learn to identify, test, and understand acid-base chemistry from ม.1 through ม.3.

## 3 | Acids (กรด)

### 3.1 Properties of Acids

| Property | Description |
|---|---|
| Taste | Sour (เปรี้ยว) — never taste in lab! |
| pH | Less than 7 |
| Litmus | Turns blue litmus red |
| React with metals | Produce hydrogen gas |
| React with bases | Produce salt + water |
| Conduct electricity | When dissolved in water |

### 3.2 Common Acids

| Formula | Name | Thai | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCl | Hydrochloric acid | กรดไฮโดรคลอริก | Stomach acid |
| H₂SO₄ | Sulfuric acid | กรดซัลฟิวริก | Car batteries |
| HNO₃ | Nitric acid | กรดไนตริก | Fertilizers |
| CH₃COOH | Acetic acid | กรดอะซิติก | Vinegar (น้ำส้มสายชู) |
| H₂CO₃ | Carbonic acid | กรดคาร์บอนิก | Carbonated drinks |
| C₆H₈O₇ | Citric acid | กรดซิตริก | Citrus fruits |
| C₄H₆O₆ | Tartaric acid | กรดทาร์ทาริก | Tamarind (มะขาม) |

## 4 | Bases (เบส/ด่าง)

### 4.1 Properties of Bases

| Property | Description |
|---|---|
| Taste | Bitter (ขม) |
| Feel | Slippery/soapy (ลื่น) |
| pH | Greater than 7 |
| Litmus | Turns red litmus blue |
| React with acids | Produce salt + water |
| Conduct electricity | When dissolved in water |

### 4.2 Common Bases

| Formula | Name | Thai | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| NaOH | Sodium hydroxide | โซเดียมไฮดรอกไซด์ | Soap making |
| KOH | Potassium hydroxide | โพแทสเซียมไฮดรอกไซด์ | Soap making |
| Ca(OH)₂ | Calcium hydroxide | แคลเซียมไฮดรอกไซด์ | Construction (ปูนขาว) |
| NaHCO₃ | Sodium bicarbonate | โซเดียมไบคาร์บอเนต | Baking soda |
| Mg(OH)₂ | Magnesium hydroxide | แมกนีเซียมไฮดรอกไซด์ | Antacid |

## 5 | pH Scale

### 5.1 The Scale

```
0 ──── 7 ──── 14
酸 ←── Neutral ──→ 碱
กรด    กลาง     เบส/ด่าง
```

| pH Range | Classification | Thai | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Strong acid | กรดแก่ | Battery acid (H₂SO₄) |
| 4-6 | Weak acid | กรดอ่อน | Vinegar, lemon juice |
| 7 | Neutral | กลาง | Pure water |
| 8-10 | Weak base | เบสอ่อน | Soap, baking soda |
| 11-14 | Strong base | เบสแก่ | Bleach, drain cleaner |

### 5.2 pH Formula

$$\text{pH} = -\log[\text{H}^+]$$

### 5.3 Indicators

| Indicator | Acid | Neutral | Base |
|---|---|---|---|
| Litmus | Red | Purple | Blue |
| Phenolphthalein | Colorless | Colorless | Pink |
| Methyl orange | Red | Orange | Yellow |
| Universal indicator | Red → Orange → Yellow | Green | Blue → Violet |

## 6 | Neutralization (ปฏิกิริยาเป็นกลาง)

### 6.1 General Equation

$$\text{Acid} + \text{Base} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Water}$$

### 6.2 Examples

| Reaction | Equation |
|---|---|
| HCl + NaOH | HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O |
| H₂SO₄ + 2NaOH | H₂SO₄ + 2NaOH → Na₂SO₄ + 2H₂O |
| HCl + KOH | HCl + KOH → KCl + H₂O |

### 6.3 Ionic Equation

$$\text{H}^+ + \text{OH}^- \rightarrow \text{H}_2\text{O}$$

## 7 | Salts (เกลือ)

### 7.1 Common Salts

| Formula | Name | Thai | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| NaCl | Sodium chloride | เกลือแกง | HCl + NaOH |
| Na₂SO₄ | Sodium sulfate | โซเดียมซัลเฟต | H₂SO₄ + NaOH |
| CaCO₃ | Calcium carbonate | แคลเซียมคาร์บอเนต | Limestone, chalk |
| NaNO₃ | Sodium nitrate | โซเดียมไนเตรต | HNO₃ + NaOH |

### 7.2 Naming Salts

The salt name comes from:
- **Metal/positive ion** from the base
- **Non-metal/negative ion** from the acid

Example: HCl + NaOH → NaCl (sodium chloride)

## 8 | Applications

### 8.1 Daily Life

| Application | Acid/Base | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Cooking | Acid | Lemon juice, vinegar |
| Cleaning | Base | Soap, baking soda |
| Medicine | Base | Antacids for heartburn |
| Agriculture | Base | Liming acidic soil |
| Food preservation | Acid | Pickling with vinegar |

### 8.2 Environmental Issues

| Issue | Cause | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Acid rain | SO₂, NOₓ + water | Damages buildings, kills fish |
| Ocean acidification | CO₂ dissolving | Harms coral reefs |
| Soil pH | Natural or human | Affects plant growth |
