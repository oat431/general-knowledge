# Forces and Motion — แรงและการเคลื่อนที่

> *"An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion."* — Newton's First Law

This note covers Newton's laws of motion, types of forces, speed, velocity, acceleration, and momentum. Students progress from pushing and pulling objects to calculating forces and analyzing motion.

## 3 | Newton's Laws of Motion

### 3.1 First Law (กฎข้อที่ 1) — Inertia

> An object at rest stays at rest; an object in motion stays in motion at constant velocity, unless acted upon by a net external force.

**Thai:** วัตถุจะคงสภาพเดิม (หยุดนิ่งหรือเคลื่อนที่) เว้นแต่จะมีแรงลัพธ์มากระทำ

**Examples:**
- Seatbelt prevents body from continuing forward when car stops
- Coin on card stays in place when card is pulled quickly

### 3.2 Second Law (กฎข้อที่ 2) — F = ma

$$F = ma$$

Where:
- $F$ = net force (Newtons, N)
- $m$ = mass (kilograms, kg)
- $a$ = acceleration (m/s²)

**Example:** A 10 kg object accelerating at 2 m/s²
$$F = 10 \times 2 = 20 \text{ N}$$

### 3.3 Third Law (กฎข้อที่ 3) — Action-Reaction

> For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

$$F_{\text{action}} = -F_{\text{reaction}}$$

**Examples:**
- Rocket pushes gas down; gas pushes rocket up
- Walking: foot pushes ground back; ground pushes foot forward

## 4 | Types of Forces

| Force | Thai | Description | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gravity | แรงโน้มถ่วง | Attraction between masses | $W = mg$ |
| Friction | แรงเสียดทาน | Opposes motion | Depends on surfaces |
| Normal | แรงปฏิกิริยาตั้งฉาก | Support from surface | Perpendicular to surface |
| Tension | แรงตึง | Pull through rope/string | Along rope direction |
| Applied | แรงภายนอก | External push/pull | Applied by person |
| Elastic | แรงยืดหยุ่น | Spring force | $F = kx$ (Hooke's law) |
| Magnetic | แรงแม่เหล็ก | Attraction/repulsion | Between magnets |
| Buoyancy | แรงลอยตัว | Upward force in fluid | Archimedes' principle |

## 5 | Key Formulas

### 5.1 Speed and Velocity

$$\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{d}{t}$$

$$\text{Velocity} = \frac{\text{Displacement}}{\text{Time}}$$

### 5.2 Acceleration

$$a = \frac{v - u}{t}$$

Where:
- $v$ = final velocity (m/s)
- $u$ = initial velocity (m/s)
- $t$ = time (s)

### 5.3 Newton's Second Law

$$F = ma$$

### 5.4 Weight

$$W = mg$$

Where $g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2 \approx 10 \text{ m/s}^2$

### 5.5 Momentum

$$p = mv$$

### 5.6 Conservation of Momentum

$$m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 = m_1v_1' + m_2v_2'$$

### 5.7 Hooke's Law (แรงยืดหยุ่น)

$$F = kx$$

Where $k$ = spring constant (N/m), $x$ = extension (m)

## 6 | Graphs of Motion

### 6.1 Distance-Time Graph

```
Distance
    ↑     /  ← Constant speed
    |    /
    |   /
    |  /
    | /
    |/_____________ Time
```

### 6.2 Velocity-Time Graph

```
Velocity
    ↑     /  ← Constant acceleration
    |    /
    |   /
    |  /
    | /
    |/_____________ Time
```

**Slope of distance-time graph** = Speed
**Slope of velocity-time graph** = Acceleration
**Area under velocity-time graph** = Distance
