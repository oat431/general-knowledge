# Red Cross Youth — ยุวกาชาด

> *"ยุวกาชาดเป็นกิจกรรมที่พัฒนาเด็กและเยาวชนให้เป็นมนุษย์ที่สมบูรณ์ เป็นคนดี มีความพร้อมในการปฏิบัติการด้านมนุษยธรรมเพื่อพลโลก" — Red Cross Youth vision*

Red Cross Youth (ยุวกาชาด) is the youth wing of the Thai Red Cross Society (สภากาชาดไทย), organized in schools as a form of Student Activities. It develops humanitarian values, health and life-protection skills, fellowship, and public service. Its curriculum operates nationwide under Ministry of Education Order 777/2554, in force since the 2555 academic year.

---

## 1 | Structure: Four Levels Across 12 Years

| Level | Thai | Grades | Characteristic |
|---|---|---|---|
| ยุวกาชาดระดับ 1 | Level 1 | ป.1–3 | Foundation: Red Cross awareness, simple health, friendship |
| ยุวกาชาดระดับ 2 | Level 2 | ป.4–6 | Health skills, first-aid basics, community service |
| ยุวกาชาดระดับ 3 | Level 3 | ม.1–3 | Life protection, humanitarian operations, leadership |
| ยุวกาชาดระดับ 4 | Level 4 | ม.4–6 | Advanced humanitarian service, community leadership |

Each level spans three school years, with main activities (กิจกรรมหลัก) required for all members and special activities (กิจกรรมพิเศษ) chosen by interest.

Source: [Red Cross Youth curriculum document](https://www.phichai.ac.th/home/yu12.pdf)

## 4 | The Red Cross Ideals (หลักการกาชาด)

| Principle | Thai | Student meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Humanity | มนุษยธรรม | Protect life and health; respect every person |
| Impartiality | ความไม่เลือกปฏิบัติ | Help by need alone — no discrimination by class, politics, religion |
| Neutrality | ความเป็นกลาง | Don't take sides in hostilities |
| Independence | ความเป็นอิสระ | The Movement decides its own action |
| Voluntary service | การรับใช้โดยสมัครใจ | Help is not for gain |
| Unity | ความเป็นหนึ่งเดียว | One Red Cross in each country, open to all |
| Universality | ความเป็นสากล | Equal status, shared duty worldwide |

*(The Movement's seven fundamental principles; ยุวกาชาด teaching emphasizes humanity, impartiality, and voluntary service at student level.)*

## 5 | Main Activities (กิจกรรมหลัก) — 4 Groups

| Group | Thai | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Red Cross and Youth Red Cross | กิจกรรมกาชาดและยุวกาชาด | Movement history, ideals, international Red Cross (ICRC/IFRC), สภากาชาดไทย |
| 2. Health | กิจกรรมสุขภาพ | Personal and public health; life protection — first aid, home safety, accident prevention |
| 3. Fellowship and Understanding | กิจกรรมสัมพันธภาพและความเข้าใจอันดี | Unity, discipline, friendship, cooperation |
| 4. Public Benefit | กิจกรรมบำเพ็ญประโยชน์ | Service to community; nature and environmental conservation |

### Special Activities (กิจกรรมพิเศษ)

- **57 special activities** from which students choose by interest and ability
- On passing the criteria, members earn the right to wear the **special activity badge (เครื่องหมายกิจกรรมพิเศษ)**
- May be scheduled in class time, after school, integrated with other learning areas, or as camps

## 6 | Assessment and Progression

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Participation | Join main activities ≥ 80% of sessions (or the school's own threshold) |
| Objectives | Pass the key objectives of each activity (theory and practice) |
| Method | Continuous observation, questioning, and testing throughout the term/year |
| Result | Level promotion (ระดับ 1→4) and course completion |

## 7 | A Worked Scenario

> **Situation:** At a school sports day, a runner collapses in the heat. Two ยุวกาชาดระดับ 3 members staffing the first-aid tent respond.

**The response as trained:**

| Step | Action | Source skill |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Scene safety | Check the track is clear before approaching | Life protection |
| 2. Assess | Responsive? Airway? Breathing? | First-aid basics |
| 3. Identify | Pale, sweating, cramps, confusion → likely heat exhaustion | Health activities |
| 4. Act | Shade, cool the body, small sips of water; monitor; if vomiting or unconscious → emergency call and nurse | First-aid protocol |
| 5. Hand over and record | Report to the school nurse; log the incident | Responsibility |
| 6. Reflect | At the next meeting: what worked, what to stock better in the tent | สะท้อนการเรียนรู้ |

## 9 | Real-World Examples

- **พิธีเข้าประจำหมู่ยุวกาชาด** — Enrollment ceremony opening each year
- **งานชุมนุมยุวกาชาด** — Annual gathering with competitions and skill stations
- **Blood-donation support** — Assisting สภากาชาดไทย blood drives (Level 4 typically)
- **Dengue and hygiene campaigns** — Students survey mosquito breeding sites and teach the community
- **World Red Cross Day (May 8)** — School ceremonies and public service
- **Sources for activities** — ICRC/IFRC materials, สภากาชาดไทย, and real community situations, as the curriculum itself recommends
