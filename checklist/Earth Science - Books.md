# Earth Science — Book Checklist

> **Subject:** Earth Science (วิทยาศาสตร์โลก) — ม.4–ม.5 (ว341, elective)
> **Related Overview:** [[Earth Science - Overview]]

## 🔴 Essential

### IPST Textbooks (สสวท.)
| Book | Publisher | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| วิทยาศาสตร์โลก | สสวท. | Geology, Meteorology, Astronomy, Environmental Science — [Free PDF](https://www.ipst.ac.th) |

## 🟡 Recommended

### International Textbooks
| Book | Author | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Earth Science | Tarbuck & Lutgens | Most popular earth science textbook — clear explanations |
| Physical Geology | Plummer, Carlson & Hammersley | For deeper geology |
| Meteorology Today | Ahrens | For deeper atmospheric science |

## 🟢 Optional

| Book | Author | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Cosmos | Pasachoff & Filippenko | For deeper astronomy |
| Oceanography | Trujillo & Thurman | For deeper oceanography |
| Environmental Science | Miller & Spoolman | For deeper environmental topics |

## Note

> Earth Science is an elective in the วิทย์-คณิต track (ว341). Fewer dedicated Thai exam prep books exist. IPST textbook is the primary reference. For university entrance, earth science content is usually covered within Physics and Chemistry exams.
