# Book Checklist — Overview

> **Purpose:** A curated list of textbooks, study guides, and exam prep books for each subject in the Math-Sci curriculum. Organized by subject with priority tiers for future summarizing.
>
> **Priority Legend:**
> - 🔴 **Essential** — Must-have for the subject
> - 🟡 **Recommended** — Very useful supplement
> - 🟢 **Optional** — Good for deeper exploration

## How to Use This Checklist

1. Pick a subject below
2. Start with 🔴 Essential books
3. Add 🟡 Recommended as you progress
4. Use 🟢 Optional for deeper dives or specific topics
5. Summarize each book into your vault notes

## International Textbook Translations

Many international textbooks have Thai translations available at SE-ED, B2S, and Naiin:

| Subject | Top Pick | Also Good |
|---|---|---|
| **Physics** | Giancoli (most popular) | Halliday Resnick (rigorous) |
| **Chemistry** | Zumdahl (clear) | Chang (widely used) |
| **Biology** | Campbell (gold standard) | Raven (supplementary) |
| **Mathematics** | Stewart Calculus | Thomas Calculus |

## Where to Buy

- **SE-ED Book Center** — Largest selection of study books
- **B2S** — Good selection, often has promotions
- **Naiin** — Also carries exam prep books
- **IPST Website** — Free PDF textbooks at [ipst.ac.th](https://www.ipst.ac.th)
- **Online** — Lazada, Shopee (often cheaper)
