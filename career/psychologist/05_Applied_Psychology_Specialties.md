# 05 — Applied Psychology Specialties

> *"Psychology isn't just about mental illness — it's about making every domain of human life better: work, school, sport, law, and health."*

Not all psychologists treat mental disorders. Many apply psychological science to specific contexts: improving workplace productivity, helping students learn, supporting athletes, assisting legal processes, and promoting health. These specialties often do **not** require a clinical license — but are increasingly regulated.

---

## 1 | Industrial-Organizational Psychology (จิตวิทยาอุตสาหกรรมและองค์การ)

> **"I-O Psychology = The science of people at work."**

### 1.1 The I-O Framework

| Domain | Key Topics |
|---|---|
| **Job Analysis** | Breaking down jobs into tasks, KSAOs (Knowledge, Skills, Abilities, Other characteristics) |
| **Selection & Recruitment** | Structured interviews, assessment centers, biodata, work samples — validity hierarchy |
| **Performance Appraisal** | 360-degree feedback, BARS (Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scales), rater bias training |
| **Training & Development** | Needs assessment, Kirkpatrick's 4 levels (Reaction → Learning → Behavior → Results) |
| **Motivation** | Maslow, Herzberg (Hygiene vs. Motivators), Self-Determination Theory (autonomy, competence, relatedness), Goal-Setting Theory (SMART) |
| **Job Satisfaction** | Job Characteristics Model (skill variety, task identity, task significance, autonomy, feedback) |
| **Leadership** | Transformational vs. Transactional, LMX (Leader-Member Exchange), servant leadership |
| **Organizational Culture** | Schein's 3 levels: Artifacts → Espoused Values → Basic Assumptions |
| **Occupational Health** | Burnout (Maslach: emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, reduced accomplishment), work-life balance |

### 1.2 I-O in Thailand

| Context | Details |
|---|---|
| **Common roles** | HR Manager, OD Consultant, Talent Management, Training Specialist, People Analytics |
| **Key employers** | Large corporations (PTT, SCG, CP Group), consulting firms, government |
| **Salary** | Starting ฿25K–฿50K; senior consultant/manager ฿80K–฿200K+ |
| **Key skills** | Statistics (SPSS/R), survey design, data analysis, business acumen |

## 2 | Educational Psychology (จิตวิทยาการศึกษา)

> **"How do people learn? How can we help them learn better?"**

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Learning theories** | Behaviorism (Skinner — reinforcement), Cognitivism (Piaget, Vygotsky — mental processes), Constructivism (learners build knowledge), Social Learning (Bandura — modeling) |
| **Motivation** | Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic; Self-Determination Theory; Growth Mindset vs. Fixed Mindset (Dweck) |
| **Classroom management** | Positive reinforcement, clear expectations, relationship-building |
| **Special education** | Learning disabilities (dyslexia, dyscalculia), ADHD, giftedness — IEPs, accommodations |
| **Assessment** | Formative vs. Summative, standardized testing (O-NET in Thailand), authentic assessment |
| **School psychology** | Counseling, behavioral interventions, crisis response, collaboration with teachers/parents |

### 2.1 Educational Psychology in Thailand

| Role | Setting |
|---|---|
| **School Psychologist / Counselor** | International schools, large government schools, universities |
| **Special Education Specialist** | Special education centers, inclusive schools |
| **Educational Researcher** | Universities, Ministry of Education, ONEC (Office of National Education Commission) |
| **Learning Designer** | EdTech companies, curriculum development |

## 3 | Health Psychology (จิตวิทยาสุขภาพ)

> **"The mind and body are not separate. Health psychology studies how they interact."**

### 3.1 The Biopsychosocial Model of Health

| Domain | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Bio** | Genetics, immune function, disease pathology |
| **Psycho** | Stress, coping, health beliefs, personality |
| **Social** | Social support, culture, healthcare access, socioeconomic status |

### 3.2 Key Topics

| Topic | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Stress & coping** | Lazarus & Folkman: Primary appraisal (is it a threat?) → Secondary appraisal (can I cope?); Allostatic load |
| **Health behavior change** | Health Belief Model, Theory of Planned Behavior, Transtheoretical Model |
| **Chronic illness** | Diabetes management, cardiac rehab, cancer — adherence, quality of life, meaning-making |
| **Pain management** | Gate Control Theory, CBT for chronic pain, mindfulness-based pain management |
| **Sleep** | CBT-I (CBT for Insomnia) — stimulus control, sleep restriction, cognitive restructuring |
| **Death & dying** | Palliative care, advance directives, family communication |

## 4 | Forensic Psychology (จิตวิทยานิติวิทยาศาสตร์)

> **"Psychology applied to the legal system — where clinical meets criminal."**

| Area | Activities |
|---|---|
| **Criminal profiling** | Analyzing crime scenes to infer offender characteristics (more art than science — use cautiously) |
| **Competency evaluation** | Is the defendant competent to stand trial? (เข้าใจข้อกล่าวหา + สามารถต่อสู้คดีได้) |
| **Insanity defense** | Did the defendant know right from wrong at the time of the offense? |
| **Risk assessment** | Violence risk, sexual recidivism risk (HCR-20, Static-99) |
| **Child custody** | Best interest of the child evaluations |
| **Eyewitness testimony** | Memory is reconstructive — lineup procedures, expert testimony on memory fallibility |
| **Correctional psychology** | Inmate mental health, rehabilitation programs, suicide prevention in prisons |

## 5 | Other Emerging Specialties

### 5.1 Sports Psychology (จิตวิทยาการกีฬา)

| Topic | Application |
|---|---|
| **Mental skills training** | Goal-setting, imagery/visualization, self-talk, arousal regulation |
| **Performance under pressure** | Choking vs. clutch performance, pre-performance routines |
| **Team dynamics** | Cohesion, communication, leadership |
| **Injury recovery** | Psychological response to injury, adherence to rehab |
| **Athlete well-being** | Burnout, identity foreclosure, transition out of sport |

### 5.2 Community Psychology (จิตวิทยาชุมชน)

| Topic | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Prevention > Treatment** | Primary prevention programs — stop problems before they start |
| **Empowerment** | Giving communities tools to solve their own problems |
| **Ecological model** | Individual ← Family ← Community ← Society — intervene at multiple levels |
| **Social justice** | Addressing systemic inequality as a mental health issue |
| **Participatory research** | Community members are partners, not just subjects |

### 5.3 Neuropsychology (ประสาทจิตวิทยา)

| Topic | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Brain-behavior relationships** | Which cognitive functions map to which brain regions? |
| **Neuropsychological assessment** | Comprehensive batteries (Halstead-Reitan, Luria-Nebraska) for brain injury, dementia, stroke |
| **Rehabilitation** | Cognitive rehab after TBI, compensatory strategies for dementia |
| **Neuroimaging** | fMRI, PET, EEG — understanding the living brain |

### 5.4 Positive Psychology (จิตวิทยาเชิงบวก)

> **Founded by Martin Seligman (1998):** "Psychology is not just the study of weakness and damage; it is also the study of strength and virtue."

| PERMA Model of Well-Being |
|---|
| **P**ositive Emotion — Joy, gratitude, serenity |
| **E**ngagement — Flow, using strengths |
| **R**elationships — Connection, belonging |
| **M**eaning — Purpose, serving something bigger |
| **A**ccomplishment — Mastery, achievement |
