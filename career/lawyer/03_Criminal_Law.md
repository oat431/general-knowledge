# 03 — Criminal Law (กฎหมายอาญา)

> *"It is better that ten guilty persons escape than that one innocent suffer."* — William Blackstone

Criminal law — codified in Thailand's **ประมวลกฎหมายอาญา (ป.อ.)** — defines offenses against the state and society, sets punishments, and establishes the principles of criminal responsibility. This is where lawyers fight for liberty.

---

## 1 | The Penal Code (ประมวลกฎหมายอาญา)

### 1.1 Structure

| Part | Thai | Sections | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General Provisions** | ภาค 1 บทบัญญัติทั่วไป | §§ 1–106 | Definitions, jurisdiction, punishments, criminal responsibility, defenses, attempt, parties |
| **Specific Offenses** | ภาค 2 ความผิด | §§ 107–366/4 | 12 Titles of specific crimes |
| **Misdemeanors** | ภาค 3 ลหุโทษ | §§ 367–398 | Minor offenses (fine ≤ ฿10,000, imprisonment ≤ 1 month) |

### 1.2 Fundamental Principles

| Principle | Latin / English | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **No crime, no punishment without law** | *Nullum crimen, nulla poena sine lege* | § 2 — Conduct is criminal only if law says so at the time |
| **No retrospective criminal law** | *Ex post facto* | Law cannot criminalize past conduct |
| **Presumption of innocence** | — | Accused is innocent until proven guilty |
| **Burden of proof on prosecution** | — | พิสูจน์จนสิ้นสงสัย (beyond reasonable doubt) |
| **In dubio pro reo** | "When in doubt, for the accused" | Benefit of doubt goes to defendant |

## 2 | Elements of Crime (องค์ประกอบความผิด)

Every crime has two essential elements:

### 2.1 External Element (องค์ประกอบภายนอก — Actus Reus)

| Component | Thai |
|---|---|
| **Act** (การกระทำ) | Commission (doing) OR omission (failing to do a legal duty) |
| **Result** (ผล) | The harm caused (not all crimes require a result) |
| **Causation** (ความสัมพันธ์ระหว่างการกระทำและผล) | Defendant's act caused the result |

### 2.2 Internal Element (องค์ประกอบภายใน — Mens Rea)

| Mental State | Thai | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Intention** | เจตนา | Knowing the nature of the act and desiring the result (§ 59) |
| **Intentional by result** | เจตนาโดยผลเล็งเห็นได้ | Not desiring the result but foreseeing it as certain |
| **Negligence** | ประมาท | Acting without due care (only where law specifies) |

> **General rule (§ 59):** Intentional acts only, unless the law specifically punishes negligence.

### 2.3 Strict Liability Offenses

| Offense | No mens rea required for... |
|---|---|
| Some regulatory offenses | e.g., Food Act, Drug Act violations |
| Vicarious liability | Employer liability for employee acts in some statutes |

## 3 | Criminal Responsibility & Defenses

### 3.1 Who Can Be Criminally Liable?

| Category | Thai | Rule (§) |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural person** | บุคคลธรรมดา | Anyone ≥ age of criminal responsibility |
| **Juristic person** | นิติบุคคล | Only where specifically provided by law |
| **Age of criminal responsibility** | — | Age: < 10 = no liability; 10–15 = court discretion (no punishment, welfare measures); 15–18 = reduced punishment; ≥ 18 = full liability |

### 3.2 Defenses (ข้อแก้ตัว / ข้อยกเว้น)

| Defense | Thai | Section | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Insanity / Mental defect** | วิกลจริต / จิตบกพร่อง | § 65 | No punishment (may be detained for treatment) |
| **Intoxication (involuntary)** | มึนเมาโดยไม่รู้ | § 66 | Exempts |
| **Intoxication (voluntary)** | มึนเมาโดยสมัครใจ | § 66 | NOT a defense (unless it negates intent) |
| **Necessity** | จำเป็น | § 67 | Exempts — act to avoid imminent danger to life/body |
| **Self-defense / Defense of others** | ป้องกัน | § 68 | Exempts — proportionate response to unlawful danger |
| **Lawful order** | คำสั่งที่ชอบด้วยกฎหมาย | § 70 | Exempts |
| **Diminished responsibility** | — | § 72 | Punishment may be reduced |

### 3.3 Inchoate Offenses

| Type | Thai | Section |
|---|---|---|
| **Attempt** | พยายามกระทำความผิด | § 80 — punished at 2/3 of completed offense |
| **Preparation** | ตระเตรียมการ | Generally NOT punished unless specified |
| **Conspiracy** | — | Specific statutes (e.g., narcotics, organized crime) |

### 3.4 Parties to Crime

| Party | Thai | Section |
|---|---|---|
| **Principal** | ตัวการ | § 83 — the person who commits the act |
| **Aider / Abettor** | ผู้สนับสนุน | § 86 — helps before or during the offense (punished at 2/3) |
| **Instigator** | ผู้ใช้ | § 84 — induces another to commit crime (punished as principal) |

## 4 | Specific Offenses (Selected Major Categories)

### 4.1 Offenses Against Life & Body

| Offense | Thai | Section | Max Penalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Murder** | ฆ่าผู้อื่น | § 288 | Death (life imprisonment in practice since de facto moratorium) |
| **Manslaughter** (intentional injury causing death) | ทำร้ายจนเป็นเหตุให้ถึงแก่ความตาย | § 290 | 3–15 years |
| **Negligent killing** | กระทำโดยประมาทเป็นเหตุให้ผู้อื่นถึงแก่ความตาย | § 291 | ≤ 10 years + fine |
| **Bodily harm** | ทำร้ายร่างกาย | §§ 295–300 | Varies by severity |

### 4.2 Offenses Against Property

| Offense | Thai | Section | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Theft** | ลักทรัพย์ | § 334 | Dishonestly taking another's property |
| **Aggravated theft** | ลักทรัพย์ในเคหสถาน / โดยใช้ยานพาหนะ ฯลฯ | §§ 335–336 | Nighttime, dwelling, vehicle use |
| **Snatching / Robbery** | วิ่งราวทรัพย์ | § 336 bis | Snatching (no violence) |
| **Robbery** | ชิงทรัพย์ | § 339 | Theft + violence/threat |
| **Gang robbery** | ปล้นทรัพย์ | § 340 | Robbery by 3+ persons |
| **Extortion** | กรรโชก | § 337 | Threat to compel property surrender |
| **Fraud / Cheating** | ฉ้อโกง | § 341 | Deceit to obtain property |
| **Embezzlement** | ยักยอก | § 352 | Misappropriating property in one's possession |
| **Receiving stolen property** | รับของโจร | § 357 | Knowingly receiving stolen goods |
| **Criminal damage** | ทำให้เสียทรัพย์ | § 358 | Damaging another's property |

### 4.3 Other Major Offense Categories

| Category | Sections | Key Offenses |
|---|---|---|
| **Title I** — Security | §§ 107–135 | Treason, sedition, offenses against royalty (lèse-majesté § 112) |
| **Title II** — Public Administration | §§ 136–144 | Bribery, malfeasance, resisting arrest |
| **Title III** — Judicial Process | §§ 145–172 | Perjury, contempt of court |
| **Title IV** — Religion | §§ 173–181 | Offenses against religious places/objects |
| **Title VI** — Public Order | §§ 182–195 | Affray, public nuisance |
| **Title VIII** — Public Morals | §§ 209–216 | Criminal association, secret society |
| **Title IX** — Sexual Offenses | §§ 276–287 | Rape, sexual assault, child sexual abuse |
| **Title XI** — Liberty & Reputation | §§ 309–333 | False imprisonment, defamation, extortion |
| **Computer Crime Act** | พ.ร.บ. คอมพิวเตอร์ | Separately codified |

## 5 | Punishments (โทษทางอาญา)

| Punishment | Thai | Section | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Death** | ประหารชีวิต | § 18(1) | Lethal injection; de facto moratorium since 2018 |
| **Imprisonment** | จำคุก | § 18(2) | Jail term |
| **Detention** | กักขัง | § 18(3) | Alternative to imprisonment for minor offenses |
| **Fine** | ปรับ | § 18(4) | Monetary penalty |
| **Property forfeiture** | ริบทรัพย์สิน | § 18(5) | Forfeiture of instrumentalities/proceeds |

**Sentencing principles:** Aggravating factors, mitigating factors, recidivism, suspension of sentence (รอการลงโทษ).
