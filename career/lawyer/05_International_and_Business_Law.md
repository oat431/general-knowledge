# 05 — International & Business Law

> *"Commerce and law are the twin pillars of modern civilization."*

This domain covers the law governing cross-border relations and modern business — where most high-paying legal jobs are. From international trade to corporate mergers to protecting brand names, these are the growth areas of Thai legal practice.

---

## Part A: International Law (กฎหมายระหว่างประเทศ)

### 1 | Sources of International Law

| Source | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Treaties / Conventions** | Written agreements between states | UN Charter, WTO agreements, ASEAN Charter, Thailand-Australia FTA |
| **Customary International Law** | Consistent state practice accepted as law | Diplomatic immunity, law of the sea |
| **General Principles** | Principles recognized by civilized nations | Good faith, pacta sunt servanda |
| **Judicial Decisions & Scholarship** | Subsidiary sources | ICJ decisions, ILC reports |

### 2 | International Law in Thai Domestic Law

| Principle | Thai Practice |
|---|---|
| **Dualism** | International law does NOT automatically become domestic law |
| **Ratification needed** | Treaty ratified by Parliament → implementing legislation → domestic effect |
| **Priority** | Constitution prevails; ordinary law applies alongside treaty obligations |

### 3 | Key Areas of International Law

| Area | Key Topics | Relevance to Thailand |
|---|---|---|
| **Public International Law** | State sovereignty, jurisdiction, state responsibility, use of force | Border disputes (Preah Vihear), Law of the Sea (Gulf of Thailand) |
| **International Humanitarian Law** | Geneva Conventions, war crimes | Thailand is a party |
| **International Human Rights Law** | ICCPR, ICESCR, CEDAW, CRC, CRPD | Thailand ratified most core treaties (with reservations) |
| **International Criminal Law** | Genocide, crimes against humanity, ICC | Thailand is NOT an ICC State Party |
| **International Environmental Law** | Climate change (Paris Agreement), biodiversity, transboundary pollution | Mekong River governance, PM₂.₅ |
| **Law of the Sea (UNCLOS)** | Territorial sea, EEZ, continental shelf | Gulf of Thailand, Andaman Sea |
| **ASEAN Law** | ASEAN Charter, AEC (ASEAN Economic Community), dispute settlement | Thailand as ASEAN founding member |

## Part B: Business & Corporate Law

### 4 | Business Entities in Thailand

| Entity | Thai | Key Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Sole Proprietorship** | กิจการเจ้าของคนเดียว | Simple; unlimited liability; no separate legal personality |
| **Ordinary Partnership** | ห้างหุ้นส่วนสามัญ | All partners have unlimited liability |
| **Registered Ordinary Partnership** | ห้างหุ้นส่วนสามัญจดทะเบียน | Separate legal personality; still unlimited liability |
| **Limited Partnership** | ห้างหุ้นส่วนจำกัด | At least 1 general partner (unlimited) + limited partners (limited to contribution) |
| **Private Limited Company** | บริษัทจำกัด | 🔑 Most common — shareholders limited to shares; minimum 2 promoters |
| **Public Limited Company** | บริษัทมหาชนจำกัด | Listed on SET; minimum 15 promoters; more regulation |
| **Branch Office** | สำนักงานสาขา | Foreign company branch; restricted activities |
| **Representative Office** | สำนักงานผู้แทน | Limited to non-revenue activities |

### 5 | Key Corporate Law Topics

| Topic | Key Rules |
|---|---|
| **Company formation** | Memorandum of Association + Articles of Association + statutory meeting + registration |
| **Shareholders** | Rights: vote, dividend, inspect books; derivative action |
| **Directors** | Fiduciary duties: duty of care, duty of loyalty; liability for wrongful acts |
| **Board meetings** | Quorum, resolutions, minutes |
| **Capital** | Par value, issued vs. paid-up, increase/reduction of capital |
| **Mergers & Acquisitions** | Amalgamation under ป.พ.พ.; share purchase vs. asset purchase |
| **Dissolution & Liquidation** | Voluntary vs. court-ordered winding up |

### 6 | Foreign Business Act (พ.ร.บ. การประกอบธุรกิจของคนต่างด้าว พ.ศ. 2542)

| List | Description |
|---|---|
| **List 1** | Businesses NOT permitted to foreigners (e.g., land trading, media, farming) |
| **List 2** | Businesses permitted if Thailand has treaty obligations or with Cabinet approval |
| **List 3** | Businesses requiring Director-General approval (most services, construction, etc.) |
| **Exemptions** | BOI promotion; IEAT industrial estate; US Treaty of Amity (for Americans) |

### 7 | Intellectual Property Law

| Right | Thai | Key Law | Term |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trademark** | เครื่องหมายการค้า | Trademark Act B.E. 2534 | 10 years (renewable indefinitely) |
| **Patent** | สิทธิบัตร | Patent Act B.E. 2522 | 20 years (invention), 10 years (design) |
| **Petty Patent** | อนุสิทธิบัตร | Same Act | 6 years (renewable 2 × 2 years) |
| **Copyright** | ลิขสิทธิ์ | Copyright Act B.E. 2537 | Life + 50 years |
| **Trade Secret** | ความลับทางการค้า | Trade Secrets Act B.E. 2545 | As long as kept secret |
| **Geographical Indication** | สิ่งบ่งชี้ทางภูมิศาสตร์ | GI Protection Act B.E. 2546 | Indefinite (e.g., ข้าวหอมมะลิทุ่งกุลาร้องไห้) |
| **Plant Variety** | คุ้มครองพันธุ์พืช | Plant Variety Protection Act | Varies |

### 8 | Tax Law (Selected)

| Tax | Thai | Rate | Law |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal Income Tax** | ภาษีเงินได้บุคคลธรรมดา | 0–35% (progressive) | Revenue Code |
| **Corporate Income Tax** | ภาษีเงินได้นิติบุคคล | 20% (standard) | Revenue Code |
| **Value Added Tax (VAT)** | ภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม | 7% (current temporary rate) | Revenue Code |
| **Specific Business Tax** | ภาษีธุรกิจเฉพาะ | Varies (e.g., banking 3%) | Revenue Code |
| **Stamp Duty** | อากรแสตมป์ | Varies by instrument | Revenue Code |
| **Customs Duty** | ภาษีศุลกากร | Varies | Customs Act |
| **Excise Tax** | ภาษีสรรพสามิต | Varies (alcohol, tobacco, petroleum, etc.) | Excise Act |

### 9 | Labor Law (Selected)

| Topic | Key Law |
|---|---|
| **Employment contract** | Labour Protection Act B.E. 2541 |
| **Minimum wage** | Set by provincial wage committees |
| **Working hours** | ≤ 8 hrs/day, ≤ 48 hrs/week |
| **Overtime** | 1.5–3× normal rate |
| **Leave** | Sick leave (as needed, paid up to 30 days), annual leave (≥ 6 days after 1 year), maternity (98 days) |
| **Termination** | Notice or pay in lieu; severance pay (30–400 days depending on length of service) |
| **Unfair termination** | Reinstatement or damages |
| **Social Security** | Social Security Act — covers sickness, maternity, disability, death, child allowance, old age, unemployment |
| **Labor unions** | Labour Relations Act B.E. 2518 |
| **Workmen's Compensation** | Workmen's Compensation Act |
