# 05 — Food Processing & Safety

> *"Thailand doesn't just grow food — we process it, package it, and send it to dinner tables in 190 countries."*

Food processing is where raw agricultural products become consumer goods — and where the most value is added. Thailand's agro-industry is world-class, especially in seafood, poultry, sugar, and fruit processing. For every ฿1 of rice exported as grain, there's ฿10 of processed food potential.

---

## 1 | Food Processing (การแปรรูปอาหาร)

### 1.1 Processing by Level

| Level | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Minimal processing — clean, sort, grade, pack | Fresh fruits, chilled chicken, milled rice |
| **Secondary** | Transform raw material into ingredient or product | Canned tuna, frozen shrimp, sugar, flour, vegetable oil |
| **Tertiary** | Ready-to-eat / value-added | Ready meals, snacks, instant noodles, sauces, beverages |
| **Quaternary** | Extracted compounds / nutraceuticals | Rice bran oil, collagen peptides, functional foods |

### 1.2 Major Thai Food Processing Industries

| Industry | Products | Key Players |
|---|---|---|
| **Seafood** | Canned tuna, frozen shrimp, surimi, fish sauce | Thai Union, CPF, Surapon, Narong |
| **Poultry** | Fresh/frozen/cooked chicken | CPF, Betagro, GFPT, Cargill |
| **Rice** | Milled, parboiled, rice flour, rice noodles, rice bran oil | CP, Thai Wah, Capital Rice |
| **Sugar** | Raw, refined, liquid sugar | Mitr Phol, Thai Roong Ruang, KSL |
| **Fruits & Vegetables** | Canned pineapple, sweet corn, fruit juice, dried fruit | Dole Thailand, Malee, Tipco, Doi Kham |
| **Dairy** | UHT milk, yoghurt, ice cream, condensed milk | Dutch Mill, CP-Meiji, Foremost, Dairy Home |
| **Beverages** | Beer, spirits, energy drinks, ready-to-drink tea | ThaiBev, Boon Rawd, Osotspa, Carabao |
| **Snacks & Confectionery** | Instant noodles, chips, biscuits | Thai President Foods (Mama), Berli Jucker |
| **Functional Foods** | Collagen, plant protein, probiotics | growing startup sector |

### 1.3 Food Processing Technologies

| Technology | Application |
|---|---|
| **Thermal processing** | Canning, pasteurization, UHT, retort — kill pathogens, extend shelf life |
| **Freezing** | IQF (Individual Quick Frozen), blast freezing — preserve quality |
| **Drying** | Spray drying (milk powder, coffee), freeze drying (premium fruit), solar drying (traditional) |
| **Extrusion** | Snacks, breakfast cereals, pet food, texturized vegetable protein |
| **Fermentation** | Fish sauce, soy sauce, yogurt, kimchi, natto |
| **Extraction** | Oil pressing, juice extraction, bioactive compounds |
| **Packaging** | Modified atmosphere (MAP), vacuum, active packaging (oxygen scavengers), smart labels |
| **Cold chain** | Temperature-controlled from farm to consumer — critical for fresh/chilled exports |

## 2 | Food Safety (ความปลอดภัยทางอาหาร)

> **Thailand's entire export reputation depends on food safety. One contamination scandal can close markets for years.**

### 2.1 Key Hazards

| Hazard Type | Examples | Control |
|---|---|---|
| **Biological** | Salmonella, E. coli, Listeria, Vibrio, norovirus | Heat kill step, cold chain, hygiene |
| **Chemical** | Pesticide residue, heavy metals, antibiotics, mycotoxins | GAP compliance, MRL testing, supplier audit |
| **Physical** | Glass, metal, stones, plastic | Metal detectors, X-ray, sieves, filters |
| **Allergens** | Crustaceans, fish, egg, milk, peanut, soy, tree nuts, wheat, sulphites | Segregation, labeling, cleaning validation |

### 2.2 Food Safety Management Systems

| System | Focus | Required For |
|---|---|---|
| **GAP** (Good Agricultural Practice) | Farm level — safe production | All export-oriented farms |
| **GMP** (Good Manufacturing Practice) | Factory — hygiene, sanitation, process control | All food factories (legal requirement) |
| **HACCP** (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points) | Identify and control specific hazards | Export markets (mandatory for seafood, meat to EU/US) |
| **ISO 22000** | Integrated food safety management | International recognition |
| **FSSC 22000** | GFSI-benchmarked | Global retailers (Walmart, Tesco, Costco) |
| **BRC / IFS** | Retailer standards | UK/European retailers |
| **Halal Certification** | Islamic dietary law compliance | Muslim markets (Middle East, Malaysia, Indonesia) |
| **FDA Thailand** | National food safety regulation | All products sold in Thailand |

### 2.3 HACCP — The 7 Principles

| Principle | Thai |
|---|---|
| **1. Conduct hazard analysis** | วิเคราะห์อันตราย |
| **2. Determine CCPs** (Critical Control Points) | กำหนดจุดวิกฤตที่ต้องควบคุม |
| **3. Establish critical limits** | กำหนดค่าวิกฤต |
| **4. Establish monitoring procedures** | กำหนดระบบตรวจติดตาม |
| **5. Establish corrective actions** | กำหนดวิธีแก้ไข |
| **6. Establish verification procedures** | กำหนดวิธีทวนสอบ |
| **7. Establish record-keeping** | กำหนดระบบเอกสารและบันทึก |

## 3 | Food Innovation & Product Development

| Trend | Opportunity |
|---|---|
| **Plant-based protein** | Plant-based meat, dairy alternatives — Thailand's agri base is perfect (soy, pea, rice) |
| **Functional foods** | Collagen drinks, probiotics, fiber-enriched, vitamin-fortified |
| **Clean label** | No artificial additives — natural preservatives, natural colors |
| **Insect protein** | Cricket flour — Thailand already has edible insect culture |
| **Upcycled foods** | Use byproducts — okara (soy pulp), spent grain, fruit peels |
| **Personalized nutrition** | DNA-based diet, microbiome testing |
| **CBD / Hemp foods** | Legalized in Thailand (2021) — growing industry |
| **Pet food** | Premium pet food from Thai poultry/seafood byproducts |
