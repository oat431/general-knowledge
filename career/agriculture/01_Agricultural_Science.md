# 01 — Agricultural Science

> *"To be a successful farmer, you must first know the soil. The soil is the soul of agriculture."*

Agricultural science forms the biological and physical foundation for modern farming. Whether you grow rice, raise shrimp, or manage an orchard, understanding the science underneath separates the professional from the amateur. These are **Year 1–2** subjects in most agricultural programs.

---

## 1 | Soil Science (ปฐพีวิทยา)

### 1.1 Soil Properties

| Property | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | Texture (sand, silt, clay), structure, porosity, water holding capacity, bulk density |
| **Chemical** | pH, CEC (Cation Exchange Capacity), organic matter, macro-nutrients (N-P-K), micro-nutrients (Ca, Mg, S, Fe, Zn, Mn, B, Cu, Mo) |
| **Biological** | Soil microbiome, mycorrhizae, nitrogen-fixing bacteria, earthworms, decomposition |

### 1.2 Thai Soil Types & Regions

| Region | Dominant Soil | Best Crops |
|---|---|---|
| **Central Plains** | Alluvial clay (ดินเหนียวตะกอนน้ำพา) | Rice — Thailand's rice bowl |
| **Northeast (Isan)** | Sandy loam (ดินร่วนปนทราย), low fertility | Cassava, sugar cane, jasmine rice (ทุ่งกุลาร้องไห้) |
| **North** | Mountain soils, terraced | Longan, lychee, coffee, temperate vegetables |
| **South** | Acid sulfate soils, lateritic | Rubber, oil palm, tropical fruits |
| **East** | Sandy clay loam | Durian, mangosteen, rambutan |

### 1.3 Soil Management

| Practice | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Fertilizer application** | N-P-K based on soil test; precision application (variable rate) |
| **Liming** | Raise pH in acid soils (common in Thailand) |
| **Organic matter** | Compost, green manure, cover crops (ปอเทือง) |
| **Conservation tillage** | Reduce erosion, preserve soil structure |
| **Crop rotation** | Break pest cycles, replenish nutrients |

## 2 | Plant Science (พืชศาสตร์)

### 2.1 Plant Physiology

| Process | Agricultural Relevance |
|---|---|
| **Photosynthesis** | C3 (rice, wheat) vs. C4 (corn, sugar cane) — C4 more efficient in heat |
| **Transpiration** | Water management, drought tolerance |
| **Nutrient uptake** | Foliar vs. root feeding; deficiency symptoms (เหลือง = N, ม่วง = P, ขอบใบไหม้ = K) |
| **Growth regulators** | Hormones (auxin, gibberellin, cytokinin) — flowering, fruit set, ripening |

### 2.2 Major Thai Crops & Their Science

| Crop | Scientific Name | Key Growing Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Rice (ข้าว)** | *Oryza sativa* | Flooded paddy; photoperiod-sensitive varieties; ~120 days |
| **Rubber (ยางพารา)** | *Hevea brasiliensis* | Tropical climate; 6-7 years to first tapping; 25+ year productive life |
| **Sugar Cane (อ้อย)** | *Saccharum officinarum* | C4 plant; high water use; 10-12 months to harvest; ratoon crops |
| **Cassava (มันสำปะหลัง)** | *Manihot esculenta* | Drought-tolerant; 8-12 months; cyanide concerns in processing |
| **Oil Palm (ปาล์มน้ำมัน)** | *Elaeis guineensis* | High rainfall; 3 years to first harvest; 25+ year productive life |
| **Durian (ทุเรียน)** | *Durio zibethinus* | Well-drained soil; 5-7 years to fruit; sensitive to water stress |
| **Orchid (กล้วยไม้)** | *Dendrobium* spp. | Thailand = #1 orchid exporter; tissue culture propagation |

### 2.3 Plant Breeding & Genetics

| Method | Application |
|---|---|
| **Traditional breeding** | Selection, hybridization — most Thai varieties |
| **Marker-assisted selection** | Speed up breeding with DNA markers |
| **Tissue culture** | Disease-free planting material, mass propagation (orchids, bananas) |
| **GMO / Gene editing** | Limited in Thailand — strict regulations; some research (papaya, tomato) |
| **Seed technology** | Seed priming, coating, storage |

### 2.4 Pest & Disease Management (IPM — Integrated Pest Management)

| Component | Methods |
|---|---|
| **Cultural** | Crop rotation, resistant varieties, planting dates, sanitation |
| **Biological** | Natural enemies (parasitoids, predators), biopesticides (Bt, neem, fungi) |
| **Chemical** | Pesticides as LAST resort; selective, targeted, minimal |
| **Monitoring** | Scouting, pheromone traps, weather-based disease forecasting |

**Thai context:** Thailand is a heavy pesticide user — the push toward organic and IPM creates demand for trained professionals.

## 3 | Animal Science (สัตวศาสตร์)

### 3.1 Major Thai Livestock

| Animal | Industry Scale | Key Products |
|---|---|---|
| **Broiler Chicken** | 🔥 Massive — top 5 global exporter | Fresh/frozen chicken, processed (CP Group) |
| **Layer Chicken** | Domestic market | Eggs (~11 billion eggs/year) |
| **Swine** | Large domestic market | Pork (Thailand's #1 consumed meat) |
| **Dairy Cattle** | Growing | Fresh milk, dairy products (school milk program) |
| **Beef Cattle** | Smaller, growing | Beef, live export |

### 3.2 Core Animal Science Topics

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Nutrition** | Rations, feed formulation, protein/energy balance, feed additives |
| **Breeding** | Artificial insemination (AI), genetic improvement, crossbreeding |
| **Health** | Vaccination, biosecurity, disease surveillance (avian influenza, ASF, FMD) |
| **Housing** | Climate control (Thailand heat!), ventilation, waste management |
| **Welfare** | Five Freedoms; growing consumer demand for welfare standards |

## 4 | Aquaculture (เพาะเลี้ยงสัตว์น้ำ)

> Thailand is a **global aquaculture powerhouse** — especially in shrimp and fish.

### 4.1 Major Aquaculture Species

| Species | Production | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **White Shrimp (กุ้งขาว)** | ~300,000 tons/yr | *Litopenaeus vannamei* — major export |
| **Tilapia (ปลานิล)** | ~200,000 tons/yr | Domestic staple; introduced by King Rama IX |
| **Catfish (ปลาดุก)** | Domestic | Easy to farm; common small-scale |
| **Sea Bass (ปลากะพง)** | Growing | High value; cage culture in coastal areas |
| **Grouper (ปลาเก๋า)** | High value | Live fish trade to Hong Kong/China |

### 4.2 Aquaculture Science

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Water quality** | DO, pH, ammonia, nitrite, alkalinity, temperature |
| **Nutrition** | Pelleted feed, FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio), protein sources |
| **Disease** | EMS/AHPND in shrimp, viral diseases, probiotics for prevention |
| **Breeding** | SPF (Specific Pathogen Free) broodstock, genetic selection |
| **Systems** | Pond, cage, RAS (Recirculating Aquaculture System), biofloc, aquaponics |
