# 05 — Chemical & Environmental Engineering

> *"Chemical engineers turn raw materials into products. Environmental engineers ensure we don't destroy the planet doing it."*

These two fields share foundations in chemistry, thermodynamics, and process design — but apply them in different directions. Thailand's petrochemical industry is one of the world's largest (Map Ta Phut), while environmental engineering is growing fast with sustainability mandates.

---

## Part A: Chemical Engineering (วิศวกรรมเคมี)

### 1 | Core Areas

#### 1.1 Chemical Process Principles

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Material Balances** | Conservation of mass — input = output + accumulation (the ChemE bread-and-butter) |
| **Energy Balances** | Conservation of energy — heat in = heat out + work + accumulation |
| **Phase Equilibria** | Vapor-liquid (distillation), liquid-liquid (extraction), solid-liquid (crystallization) |
| **Chemical Reaction Engineering** | Kinetics, reactor design (CSTR, PFR, batch), catalysis |

#### 1.2 Unit Operations

| Operation | Purpose | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| **Distillation** | Separate by boiling point | Distillation columns, trays, packing |
| **Absorption / Stripping** | Gas-liquid mass transfer | Packed towers, spray columns |
| **Extraction** | Liquid-liquid separation | Mixer-settlers, extraction columns |
| **Filtration** | Solid-liquid separation | Filter presses, rotary drum filters |
| **Drying** | Remove moisture | Spray dryers, rotary dryers, fluidized bed |
| **Heat Exchange** | Transfer heat | Shell-and-tube, plate, fin-fan exchangers |
| **Crystallization** | Form solid crystals from solution | Crystallizers |
| **Membrane Separation** | RO, UF, NF, gas separation membranes | Membrane modules |

#### 1.3 Thermodynamics & Transport Phenomena

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Chemical Thermodynamics** | Gibbs free energy, chemical potential, fugacity, activity coefficients |
| **Fluid Mechanics** | Pumps, compressors, piping design, pressure drop, sizing |
| **Heat Transfer** | Conduction, convection, radiation; heat exchanger design (LMTD, NTU) |
| **Mass Transfer** | Diffusion (Fick's law), convective mass transfer, interfacial mass transfer |

#### 1.4 Process Design & Control

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Process Flow Diagrams (PFD)** | Block flow → PFD → P&ID (Piping & Instrumentation Diagram) |
| **Equipment Sizing** | Reactors, columns, heat exchangers, pumps, vessels |
| **Process Control** | Feedback/feedforward, cascade control, DCS (Distributed Control System) |
| **Process Safety** | HAZOP, LOPA, SIL, relief systems, inherently safer design |
| **Process Simulation** | Aspen Plus, HYSYS, PRO/II — industry-standard simulators |

#### 1.5 Key Industries (Thailand)

| Industry | Products | Key Employers |
|---|---|---|
| **Petrochemical** | Ethylene, propylene, plastics (PE, PP, PVC), aromatics | PTT Global Chemical, SCG Chemicals, IRPC, Thai Oil |
| **Refining** | Gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, LPG | Thai Oil, Bangchak, Esso, PTT |
| **Polymers & Plastics** | Packaging, automotive parts, construction materials | Indorama, HMC Polymers |
| **Food Processing** | Sugar, starch, edible oils, beverages | CP Group, Mitr Phol, Thai Union, ThaiBev |
| **Pharmaceuticals** | Generic drugs, APIs | Government Pharmaceutical Org, Siam Bioscience |
| **Biochemical / Biofuels** | Ethanol, biodiesel, bioplastics | Mitr Phol, Global Green Chemicals |

**Starting salary:** ฿25,000–฿40,000 (higher in oil & gas)

## Part B: Environmental Engineering (วิศวกรรมสิ่งแวดล้อม)

> **⚠️ Environmental engineering is a CONTROLLED field for EIA-related work.**

### 2 | Core Areas

#### 2.1 Water & Wastewater

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Water Quality** | Parameters: BOD, COD, TSS, DO, pH, nutrients (N, P), pathogens |
| **Water Treatment** | Coagulation-flocculation, sedimentation, filtration (rapid sand, membrane), disinfection (chlorine, UV, ozone) |
| **Wastewater Treatment** | Primary (settling) → Secondary (activated sludge, trickling filter, oxidation pond) → Tertiary (nutrient removal, filtration) → Sludge handling |
| **Industrial Wastewater** | Specific treatments for heavy metals, oil/grease, toxic organics |

#### 2.2 Air Pollution

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Air Pollutants** | PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, SO₂, NOₓ, CO, O₃, VOCs, HAPs |
| **Emission Control** | Scrubbers, electrostatic precipitators (ESP), baghouse filters, catalytic converters |
| **Dispersion Modeling** | AERMOD, CALPUFF — predict pollution plumes |
| **Air Quality Standards** | Thailand's Ambient Air Quality Standards; WHO guidelines |

**Thai context:** PM₂.₅ is a CRITICAL public health issue, especially in Bangkok and the North (agricultural burning). Environmental engineers are at the center of the solution.

#### 2.3 Solid & Hazardous Waste

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Municipal Solid Waste** | Collection, transfer stations, sanitary landfill, incineration (Waste-to-Energy) |
| **Hazardous Waste** | Classification, storage, treatment, secure landfill |
| **Recycling & Circular Economy** | Material recovery, extended producer responsibility (EPR) |
| **E-Waste** | Growing problem — informal recycling sector, formalization |

#### 2.4 Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **EIA Process** | Screening → Scoping → Baseline study → Impact prediction → Mitigation → Public participation → Monitoring |
| **Thai EIA Law** | Required for specified project types (power plants, dams, industrial estates, high-rises) |
| **EHIA (Environmental & Health Impact Assessment)** | Required for projects with potentially severe impacts |

#### 2.5 Sustainability & Climate

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **GHG Accounting** | Scope 1, 2, 3 emissions; carbon footprint; CDM (Clean Development Mechanism) |
| **Climate Adaptation** | Flood protection, coastal erosion, water security |
| **ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance)** | Corporate reporting, sustainable finance |
