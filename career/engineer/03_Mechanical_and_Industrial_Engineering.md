# 03 — Mechanical & Industrial Engineering

> *"Mechanical engineers make things move. Industrial engineers make things work — efficiently."*

These two disciplines power Thailand's manufacturing and automotive industries. Mechanical engineering is one of the broadest fields; industrial engineering focuses on optimization. Together they're the backbone of Thailand as the "Detroit of Asia."

---

## Part A: Mechanical Engineering (วิศวกรรมเครื่องกล)

### 1 | Core Areas

#### 1.1 Thermodynamics & Energy

| Topic | Key Concepts | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Laws of Thermodynamics** | Conservation of energy, entropy, irreversibility | All energy systems |
| **Power Cycles** | Rankine (steam), Brayton (gas turbine), Otto (gasoline), Diesel, Combined cycle | Power plants, engines |
| **Refrigeration & Heat Pumps** | Vapor-compression cycle, COP, refrigerants | Air conditioning, cooling |
| **Heat Transfer** | Conduction, convection, radiation, heat exchangers | Thermal design |
| **Combustion** | Stoichiometry, emissions, flame types | Engines, boilers, furnaces |

**Thai context:** Thailand's hot climate makes HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning) a massive industry. Every building needs it — and mechanical engineers design it.

#### 1.2 Machine Design

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Mechanisms** | Linkages, cams, gears, bearings, belts, chains |
| **Machine Elements** | Shafts, keys, springs, fasteners, couplings, clutches, brakes |
| **Stress Analysis** | Static failure theories (von Mises, Tresca), fatigue (S-N curves, Goodman), fracture mechanics |
| **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** | Stress, thermal, vibration — computer simulation before building |
| **Design for Manufacturing (DFM)** | Design parts that can actually be made efficiently |

#### 1.3 Manufacturing Processes

| Process | Description | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| **Casting** | Pour molten metal into mold | Iron, aluminum, steel |
| **Forming** | Forging, rolling, extrusion, sheet metal stamping | Metals |
| **Machining** | Turning, milling, drilling, grinding — subtractive | Metals, plastics |
| **Joining** | Welding (MIG, TIG, arc), brazing, adhesive bonding | Metals, plastics |
| **Additive (3D Printing)** | FDM, SLA, SLS, DMLS — rapid prototyping + production | Plastics, metals |

#### 1.4 Dynamics & Control

| Topic | Key Concepts | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinematics** | Position, velocity, acceleration analysis of mechanisms | Robot arms, engines |
| **Kinetics** | Forces causing motion; Newton-Euler, Lagrange methods | Machine dynamics |
| **Vibration** | Free, forced, damped; natural frequency, resonance, isolation | Engine balancing, building isolation |
| **Control Systems** | PID control, feedback, stability, PLC programming | Factory automation, robotics |

#### 1.5 HVAC & Building Services (Controlled Field)

| System | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Air Conditioning** | Cooling load calculation, psychrometrics, duct design, chiller selection |
| **Ventilation** | Fresh air requirements (ASHRAE 62.1), exhaust systems |
| **Fire Protection** | Sprinkler systems, fire pumps, smoke control |
| **Plumbing** | Water supply, drainage, hot water systems |
| **Elevators & Escalators** | Traction/hydraulic elevators; traffic analysis |

> **HVAC, elevators, and fire protection = CONTROLLED engineering fields.** Mechanical engineers in these areas MUST be licensed (สามัญวิศวกร).

## Part B: Industrial Engineering (วิศวกรรมอุตสาหการ)

### 2 | Core Areas

#### 2.1 Operations Research & Optimization

| Topic | Application |
|---|---|
| **Linear Programming** | Resource allocation, production planning |
| **Integer Programming** | Facility location, scheduling |
| **Network Models** | Transportation, shortest path, max flow — logistics |
| **Queuing Theory** | Waiting lines — call centers, service counters, production buffers |
| **Simulation** | Discrete-event simulation (Arena, Simio) — "digital twin" of factories |

#### 2.2 Production Planning & Control

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Forecasting** | Moving average, exponential smoothing, seasonal models |
| **MRP / ERP** | Material Requirements Planning; SAP, Oracle |
| **Scheduling** | Job shop, flow shop, Gantt charts, priority rules |
| **Inventory Management** | EOQ, safety stock, ABC analysis, JIT (Just-in-Time) |
| **Lean Manufacturing** | Toyota Production System — 5S, Kaizen, Kanban, Poka-Yoke, value stream mapping |

#### 2.3 Quality Control

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Statistical Process Control (SPC)** | Control charts (X-bar, R, p, c), process capability (Cp, Cpk) |
| **Six Sigma** | DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control), belt certification |
| **Total Quality Management (TQM)** | Company-wide quality culture; Deming, Juran |
| **ISO 9001** | Quality management system standard — widely adopted in Thailand |
| **Measurement System Analysis** | Gauge R&R — is your measurement system reliable? |

#### 2.4 Supply Chain & Logistics

| Topic | Application |
|---|---|
| **Supply Chain Design** | Network optimization, facility location, distribution centers |
| **Transportation** | Route optimization, fleet management, multimodal logistics |
| **Warehousing** | Layout, material handling, WMS (Warehouse Management System) |
| **Procurement** | Supplier selection, negotiation, contract management |

**Thai context:** Thailand is a major global supply chain hub — automotive (Toyota, Honda, Ford, BMW), electronics (Western Digital, Seagate), food processing. Industrial engineers are the backbone of these industries.

#### 2.5 Ergonomics & Human Factors

| Topic | Application |
|---|---|
| **Physical ergonomics** | Workplace design, anthropometry, repetitive strain injury prevention |
| **Cognitive ergonomics** | Human-machine interface, decision-making under stress |
| **Work measurement** | Time study, predetermined motion time systems (MOST, MTM) |
| **Occupational safety** | Hazard analysis, PPE, OSHA compliance |

#### 2.6 Automation & Industry 4.0

| Technology | Role in IE |
|---|---|
| **PLC / SCADA** | Factory floor automation and monitoring |
| **Robotics** | Industrial robots (ABB, KUKA, Fanuc) — welding, assembly, material handling |
| **IoT (Internet of Things)** | Connected sensors for predictive maintenance and real-time monitoring |
| **Digital Twin** | Virtual replica of factory for simulation and optimization |
| **AI / Machine Learning** | Predictive quality, demand forecasting, maintenance scheduling |
