# 02 — Civil Engineering (วิศวกรรมโยธา)

> *"Doctors save lives. Civil engineers save lives by the thousands — through clean water, safe bridges, and buildings that don't fall down."*

Civil engineering is the oldest engineering discipline — and one of the most regulated. Every building, bridge, road, dam, and water system involves civil engineers who are legally responsible for public safety. In Thailand, civil engineering is a **controlled field** — you MUST be licensed to sign off on structural designs.

---

## 1 | Sub-Disciplines of Civil Engineering

### 1.1 Structural Engineering (วิศวกรรมโครงสร้าง)

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Structural Analysis** | Determinate vs. indeterminate structures; matrix stiffness method; influence lines |
| **Reinforced Concrete Design** | Beams, columns, slabs, footings — WSD (Working Stress Design) + SDM (Strength Design Method) per ACI / Thai standard (มยผ.) |
| **Steel Design** | Tension members, compression members, beams, connections — AISC / Thai standard |
| **Prestressed Concrete** | Pre-tensioning, post-tensioning — bridges, parking structures |
| **Structural Dynamics** | Earthquake engineering, wind loads, vibration analysis |
| **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** | Computer modeling of complex structures |
| **Foundation Engineering** | Shallow (spread footings, mat) + deep (piles, caissons) foundations |

**Thai context:** Thailand is not highly seismic, but building codes now incorporate earthquake provisions (มยผ. 1301/1302). Bangkok's soft clay poses unique foundation challenges.

### 1.2 Geotechnical Engineering (วิศวกรรมปฐพี)

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Soil Mechanics** | Soil classification, compaction, permeability, consolidation, shear strength |
| **Foundation Design** | Bearing capacity, settlement analysis, pile capacity, lateral earth pressure |
| **Slope Stability** | Landslides, retaining walls, embankments |
| **Ground Improvement** | Compaction, preloading with PVD, soil-cement columns, dynamic compaction |
| **Site Investigation** | Boreholes, SPT, CPT, laboratory testing |

**Thai context:** Bangkok clay (ดินเหนียวอ่อนกรุงเทพ) — very soft, highly compressible, famous worldwide in geotechnical circles. Settlement is a constant battle.

### 1.3 Transportation Engineering (วิศวกรรมขนส่ง)

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Highway Engineering** | Geometric design, pavement (flexible/rigid), drainage, traffic signs/markings |
| **Traffic Engineering** | Volume studies, level of service (LOS), signal timing, intersection design |
| **Pavement Design** | AASHTO method; asphalt + concrete pavements |
| **Railway Engineering** | Track geometry, stations, electrification (growing with Thai high-speed rail) |
| **Airport Engineering** | Runway design, taxiways, terminal planning |
| **Public Transit** | BRT, LRT, MRT — growing in Bangkok and major cities |

**Thai context:** Massive infrastructure boom — MRT lines expansion, high-speed rail (Thai-Chinese, Don Mueang–Suvarnabhumi–U-Tapao), motorway network, double-track railway.

### 1.4 Water Resources Engineering (วิศวกรรมแหล่งน้ำ)

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Hydrology** | Rainfall-runoff, flood frequency, unit hydrograph |
| **Hydraulics** | Open channel flow, pipe networks, pumps, hydraulic structures (weirs, spillways) |
| **Water Supply** | Treatment, distribution networks, reservoir design |
| **Wastewater** | Collection systems, treatment plants |
| **Flood Control** | Levees, detention basins, drainage systems, pumping stations |
| **Irrigation** | Canal systems, gates, water management |

**Thai context:** Flood management is CRITICAL — 2011 mega-flood was a wake-up call. Seasonal flooding in the Chao Phraya basin is an annual challenge. Royal Irrigation Department (กรมชลประทาน) is a major employer.

### 1.5 Construction Engineering & Management (วิศวกรรมก่อสร้างและการบริหาร)

| Topic | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Construction Methods** | Formwork, scaffolding, precast concrete, steel erection, excavation |
| **Project Management** | CPM scheduling, PERT, Gantt charts, resource leveling, earned value |
| **Cost Estimation** | Quantity takeoff, unit costs, bidding, BOQ (Bill of Quantities) |
| **Construction Law** | Contracts, claims, FIDIC, Thai government procurement regulations |
| **Safety** | OSHA, PPE, fall protection, confined space, excavation safety |
| **Quality Control** | Concrete testing, steel inspection, NDT methods |

## 2 | Key Standards & Codes (Thailand)

| Code | Description |
|---|---|
| **มยผ. (MyPhor.)** | Thai Engineering Standards — structural, geotechnical, drainage |
| **ก.พ.ด.** | Department of Public Works and Town & Country Planning standards |
| **วสท. (EIT) Standards** | Engineering Institute of Thailand practice guidelines |
| **AASHTO** | Highway design (US standard, adapted) |
| **ACI 318** | Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete |
| **AISC** | Steel Construction Manual |
| **ASTM** | Materials testing standards |
