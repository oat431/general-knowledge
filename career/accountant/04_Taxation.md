# 04 — Taxation

> *"In this world, nothing is certain except death and taxes."* — Benjamin Franklin

Taxation is one of the most practical and profitable specializations in accounting. Every individual and business must pay taxes — and the Thai Revenue Code is complex enough to keep tax professionals busy for a lifetime. Good tax planning saves clients money. Bad tax planning costs them dearly.

---

## 1 | The Thai Tax System

### 1.1 Key Tax Types

| Tax | Thai | Rate | Who Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal Income Tax** | ภาษีเงินได้บุคคลธรรมดา | 0–35% (progressive) | Individuals |
| **Corporate Income Tax** | ภาษีเงินได้นิติบุคคล | 20% (standard) | Companies |
| **Value Added Tax (VAT)** | ภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม | 7% (current) | Businesses + consumers |
| **Specific Business Tax** | ภาษีธุรกิจเฉพาะ | 0.1–3% | Banking, finance, real estate |
| **Stamp Duty** | อากรแสตมป์ | Varies | Various instruments |
| **Withholding Tax** | ภาษีหัก ณ ที่จ่าย | 0.5–15% | Payers of certain income |
| **Excise Tax** | ภาษีสรรพสามิต | Varies | Alcohol, tobacco, petroleum, cars, etc. |
| **Customs Duty** | ภาษีศุลกากร | Varies | Importers |
| **Property Tax** | ภาษีที่ดินและสิ่งปลูกสร้าง | 0.01–0.7% | Land/building owners |
| **Inheritance Tax** | ภาษีการรับมรดก | 5–10% | Inheritors (over ฿100M) |

### 1.2 Revenue Code Structure (ประมวลรัษฎากร)

| Book | Content |
|---|---|
| **Book 1** | General provisions, tax administration |
| **Book 2** | Personal income tax |
| **Book 3** | Corporate income tax |
| **Book 4** | VAT and Specific Business Tax |
| **Book 5** | Stamp Duty |
| **Book 6** | Tax administration, penalties |

## 2 | Personal Income Tax (PIT — ภาษีเงินได้บุคคลธรรมดา)

### 2.1 Progressive Tax Rates (2024)

| Taxable Income (฿) | Rate | Tax (฿) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 150,000 | 0% | 0 |
| 150,001 – 300,000 | 5% | 7,500 |
| 300,001 – 500,000 | 10% | 20,000 |
| 500,001 – 750,000 | 15% | 37,500 |
| 750,001 – 1,000,000 | 20% | 50,000 |
| 1,000,001 – 2,000,000 | 25% | 250,000 |
| 2,000,001 – 5,000,000 | 30% | 900,000 |
| Over 5,000,000 | 35% | — |

### 2.2 Deductions & Allowances

| Category | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Personal allowance** | ฿60,000 |
| **Spouse allowance** | ฿60,000 (if spouse has no income) |
| **Child allowance** | ฿30,000 per child (max 3) |
| **Parent care** | ฿30,000 per parent (max 2) |
| **Life insurance** | Up to ฿100,000 |
| **Provident fund / RMF** | Up to 15% of income (max ฿500,000 combined) |
| **SSF (Super Savings Fund)** | Up to ฿200,000 |
| **Mortgage interest** | Up to ฿100,000 |
| **Donation** | Up to 10% of income after other deductions |

## 3 | Corporate Income Tax (CIT — ภาษีเงินได้นิติบุคคล)

| Entity | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Standard rate** | 20% of net profit |
| **BOI-promoted companies** | 0% for 3–8 years, then 50% reduction for 5 years |
| **SMEs (capital ≤ ฿5M, revenue ≤ ฿30M)** | 0% (first ฿300K), 15% (฿300K–฿3M), 20% (over ฿3M) |
| **Listed companies** | 20% |

### 3.1 Taxable Income

$$\text{Taxable Income} = \text{Accounting Profit} + \text{Non-deductible Expenses} - \text{Exempt Income} - \text{Tax Loss Carry-forward}$$

**Key non-deductible expenses:**
- Entertainment expenses exceeding 0.3% of revenue (max ฿10M)
- Fines and penalties
- Personal expenses
- Reserves not allowed by law
- Related-party transactions not at arm's length

---

## 4 | Value Added Tax (VAT — ภาษีมูลค่าเพิ่ม)

| Concept | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | 7% (reduced from 10% by Royal Decree — effectively permanent) |
| **Registration threshold** | Revenue > ฿1.8 million/year must register |
| **Input VAT** | VAT paid on purchases — deductible |
| **Output VAT** | VAT collected on sales |
| **VAT payable** | Output VAT - Input VAT |
| **Filing** | Monthly (PP.30 form) by 15th of following month |
| **Exemptions** | Healthcare, education, agriculture, exports (zero-rated) |

---

## 5 | Withholding Tax (WHT — ภาษีหัก ณ ที่จ่าย)

| Income Type | WHT Rate |
|---|---|
| **Employment (salary)** | Progressive (same as PIT rates) |
| **Service fees (company)** | 3% (if paid to company), 5% (if paid to individual) |
| **Rent** | 5% |
| **Professional fees (individual)** | 3% (if paid by company) |
| **Interest** | 1% (bank), 15% (other) |
| **Dividends** | 10% |
| **Royalties** | 3% (company), 5% (individual) |
| **Freelance / Independent contractor** | 3% (if paid by company) |

## 6 | Tax Planning Strategies

| Strategy | Legality | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Timing** | ✅ Legal | Accelerate expenses, defer income to next year |
| **Entity structure** | ✅ Legal | Choose company vs. partnership vs. individual |
| **BOI promotion** | ✅ Legal | Apply for investment promotion (tax holidays) |
| **Transfer pricing** | ⚠️ Scrutinized | Arm's-length pricing between related parties |
| **Tax loss carry-forward** | ✅ Legal | Use losses to offset future profits (up to 5 years) |
| **Double taxation agreements** | ✅ Legal | Thailand has 60+ DTAs — reduce WHT on cross-border payments |
| **Donations** | ✅ Legal | Tax-deductible up to 10% of income |
| **Provident fund / RMF / SSF** | ✅ Legal | Retirement savings with tax benefits |
