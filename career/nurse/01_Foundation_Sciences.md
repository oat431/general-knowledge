# 01 — Foundation Sciences for Nursing

> *"To know what is normal, you must first understand what is normal. To treat the abnormal, you must first know the normal."*

Foundation sciences form the bedrock of nursing education. Before students learn to care for patients, they must understand how the human body works, how diseases disrupt it, and how medications restore balance. These subjects are concentrated in **Year 1-2** of the B.N.S. program.

---

## 1 | Core Foundation Subjects

### 1.1 Anatomy (กายวิภาคศาสตร์)

| Sub-topic | Content | Clinical Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **Gross anatomy** | Body regions, cavities, organ systems | Physical assessment, surgical nursing |
| **Musculoskeletal** | Bones, joints, muscles, movements | Orthopedic nursing, mobility care |
| **Nervous system** | Brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves | Neurological assessment (GCS), stroke care |
| **Cardiovascular** | Heart chambers, great vessels, circulation | Vital signs, ECG, cardiac emergencies |
| **Respiratory** | Airways, lungs, pleura, diaphragm | Oxygen therapy, ventilation, chest physio |
| **Digestive** | GI tract, liver, pancreas, gallbladder | NG tube, nutrition, GI assessment |
| **Urinary** | Kidneys, ureters, bladder, urethra | Catheterization, fluid balance |
| **Reproductive** | Male & female organs, pregnancy anatomy | OB/GYN nursing, family planning |

### 1.2 Physiology (สรีรวิทยา)

| System | Key Concepts |
|---|---|
| **Cell physiology** | Membrane transport, action potentials, cell signaling |
| **Homeostasis** | Negative/positive feedback, fluid-electrolyte balance |
| **Cardiovascular** | Cardiac cycle, blood pressure regulation, cardiac output |
| **Respiratory** | Gas exchange, O₂-CO₂ transport, respiratory control |
| **Renal** | Glomerular filtration, urine formation, acid-base balance |
| **Endocrine** | Hormone regulation, HPA axis, glucose metabolism |
| **Neurophysiology** | Synaptic transmission, autonomic nervous system, pain pathways |
| **Immune** | Innate & adaptive immunity, inflammation, hypersensitivity |

### 1.3 Biochemistry (ชีวเคมี)

| Topic | Nursing Application |
|---|---|
| **Carbohydrates, lipids, proteins** | Nutritional assessment, diabetic diet |
| **Enzymes & metabolism** | Understanding lab values (ALT, AST, CK-MB) |
| **Acid-base balance** | Interpreting ABG (Arterial Blood Gas) |
| **Electrolytes** | Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺ — critical values, replacement therapy |
| **Molecular genetics** | Genetic counseling, pharmacogenomics |

### 1.4 Microbiology & Immunology (จุลชีววิทยาและภูมิคุ้มกัน)

| Topic | Clinical Relevance |
|---|---|
| **Bacteria classification** | Gram +/-, aerobic/anaerobic → antibiotic selection |
| **Viruses** | HIV, hepatitis, influenza, COVID-19 — transmission & prevention |
| **Fungi & parasites** | Opportunistic infections, tropical diseases |
| **Normal flora** | Understanding when to treat vs. when to leave alone |
| **Infection control** | Standard precautions, isolation, sterilization |
| **Immune response** | Vaccination, autoimmune disease, anaphylaxis |

### 1.5 Pharmacology (เภสัชวิทยา)

| Topic | Nursing Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Pharmacokinetics** | Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion (ADME) |
| **Pharmacodynamics** | Drug-receptor interactions, dose-response |
| **Drug classifications** | Antibiotics, analgesics, antihypertensives, etc. |
| **Drug calculations** | Dosage, IV drip rates, pediatric dosing |
| **Medication safety** | **5 Rights** + 3 checks, high-alert drugs, look-alike/sound-alike |
| **Adverse drug reactions** | Side effects, allergies, anaphylaxis management |
| **Thai drug laws** | Controlled substances, narcotics handling (ยาเสพติดให้โทษ) |

### 1.6 Pathophysiology (พยาธิสรีรวิทยา)

| Topic | Application |
|---|---|
| **Cellular injury & adaptation** | Understanding disease mechanisms |
| **Inflammation & healing** | Wound care, post-op recovery |
| **Neoplasia** | Cancer nursing, chemotherapy side effects |
| **Genetic disorders** | Down syndrome, thalassemia (common in Thailand) |
| **System-specific pathology** | Heart failure, COPD, cirrhosis, CKD mechanisms |

### 1.7 Nutrition (โภชนาการ)

| Topic | Nursing Role |
|---|---|
| **Macronutrients & micronutrients** | Dietary assessment |
| **Therapeutic diets** | Diabetic, renal, cardiac, post-op diets |
| **Enteral & parenteral nutrition** | NG tube feeding, TPN management |
| **Malnutrition** | PEM (Protein-Energy Malnutrition in children), geriatric nutrition |

## 3 | Study Tips for Foundation Sciences

| Subject | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **Anatomy** | Use models, apps (Complete Anatomy, Visible Body), draw & label |
| **Physiology** | Understand mechanisms, not just memorize — draw flowcharts |
| **Biochemistry** | Focus on clinical correlations (e.g., why does diabetic ketoacidosis happen?) |
| **Pharmacology** | Group drugs by class and suffix (-olol = beta blocker, -pril = ACE inhibitor) |
| **Pathophysiology** | Connect back to normal anatomy/physiology — what changed? |
| **Microbiology** | Use mind maps for bug-drug relationships |
