# 04 — Maternal, Child & Mental Health Nursing

> *"To care for a mother is to care for two lives. To care for a child is to shape a future. To care for the mind is to care for the whole person."*

This domain covers three distinct but connected populations: women during pregnancy and childbirth, children from newborn to adolescent, and individuals experiencing mental health challenges. Taught primarily in **Year 3**.

---

## Part A: Maternal & Newborn Nursing (การพยาบาลมารดาและทารก)

### 1 | Antepartum (ระยะตั้งครรภ์)

| Assessment | Key Points |
|---|---|
| **GTPAL** | Gravida, Term, Preterm, Abortions, Living children |
| **Nägele's Rule** | EDC = LMP − 3 months + 7 days + 1 year |
| **Prenatal visits** | q4w up to wk 28 → q2w to wk 36 → q1w to delivery |
| **Fundal height** | cm ≈ weeks gestation (after 20 wks) |
| **Prenatal labs** | CBC, blood type/Rh, Rubella, HBsAg, VDRL, HIV, GCT/OGTT |
| **Ultrasound** | Dating, anatomy scan (18–22 wks), growth, biophysical profile |

| Complication | Nursing Care |
|---|---|
| **Hyperemesis gravidarum** | IV fluids, antiemetics, small frequent meals, monitor ketones |
| **GDM** | Diet, BG monitoring, insulin if needed |
| **Preeclampsia** | BP monitoring, bed rest (left lateral), MgSO₄ for seizure prophylaxis, deliver |
| **Placenta previa** | NO vaginal exam, bed rest, C-section |
| **Abruptio placentae** | Emergency C-section, monitor for DIC, blood products ready |

### 2 | Intrapartum (ระยะคลอด)

| Stage | Duration (Nullip) | Key Nursing Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1: Dilation** | 12–18 hrs | Monitor contractions, FHR, cervical dilation, pain management |
| **Stage 2: Expulsion** | 30 min–2 hrs | Coaching pushes, perineal support, prepare for delivery |
| **Stage 3: Placental** | 5–30 min | Signs of separation (gush of blood, cord lengthening), oxytocin |
| **Stage 4: Recovery** | 1–2 hrs | Fundal checks q15min, massage if boggy, monitor bleeding |

### 3 | Postpartum (ระยะหลังคลอด)

| Assessment | Key Points |
|---|---|
| **BUBBLE-HE** | Breasts, Uterus, Bladder, Bowel, Lochia, Episiotomy/Incision, Hemorrhoids/Homans, Emotions |
| **Fundus** | Should be firm, midline, at/below umbilicus — massage if boggy |
| **Lochia** | Rubra (1–3d) → Serosa (4–10d) → Alba (10d–6wk) |
| **Bonding** | Skin-to-skin, breastfeeding support, screen for postpartum depression |

### 4 | Newborn Care

| Assessment | Nursing Care |
|---|---|
| **APGAR** (1 & 5 min) | Appearance, Pulse, Grimace, Activity, Respiration (0-10) |
| **Thermoregulation** | Dry immediately, radiant warmer, hat, skin-to-skin |
| **Vitamin K + erythromycin** | Prevent hemorrhagic disease + ophthalmia neonatorum |
| **Newborn screening** | TSH (congenital hypothyroidism), PKU, hearing |
| **Jaundice** | Physiological vs. pathological, phototherapy, monitor bilirubin |

## Part B: Pediatric Nursing (การพยาบาลเด็ก)

### 5 | Growth & Development

| Age Group | Key Milestones |
|---|---|
| **Infant (0–12 mo)** | Head control → roll → sit → crawl → walk; stranger anxiety (8 mo) |
| **Toddler (1–3 yr)** | Autonomy, toilet training (18–24 mo), parallel play |
| **Preschool (3–6 yr)** | Initiative, magical thinking, associative play |
| **School-age (6–12 yr)** | Industry, concrete operations, cooperative play |
| **Adolescent (12–18 yr)** | Identity, formal operations, peer importance |

### 6 | Common Pediatric Conditions

| Condition | Key Nursing Care |
|---|---|
| **Acute respiratory infection** | Nasal saline, suction (NPO before), monitor RR/retractions/SpO₂ |
| **Diarrhea** | ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution), continue breastfeeding, zinc |
| **Febrile seizure** | Safety, antipyretics, reassure parents (usually benign) |
| **Dengue hemorrhagic fever (ไข้เลือดออก)** | Monitor for bleeding/shock, hematocrit, platelets, fluid management |
| **Thalassemia (ธาลัสซีเมีย)** | Common in Thailand — blood transfusions, iron chelation |
| **Asthma** | Inhaler/spacer technique, trigger avoidance, asthma action plan |
| **Leukemia** | Infection prevention (neutropenic precautions), chemotherapy support |

### 7 | Pediatric-Specific Considerations

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|
| **Weight-based dosing** | All meds calculated by mg/kg — double-check! |
| **Developmental approach** | Explain at child's level, use play, allow choices |
| **Family-centered care** | Parents as partners, rooming-in, sibling involvement |
| **Pain assessment** | FLACC (infants/young), FACES (>3 yr), numeric (>7 yr) |
| **Immunization** | EPI schedule (วัคซีนตามแผน) — BCG, DTP, OPV/IPV, MMR, etc. |

## Part C: Mental Health Nursing (การพยาบาลจิตเวช)

### 8 | Therapeutic Communication

| Technique | Example |
|---|---|
| **Active listening** | Nodding, eye contact, "I see," "Go on" |
| **Open-ended questions** | "Tell me more about how you've been feeling" |
| **Reflecting** | "It sounds like you're feeling overwhelmed" |
| **Validation** | "It makes sense you'd feel that way" |
| **Silence** | Give space — don't rush to fill |
| **AVOID:** | "Why?" (sounds accusatory), false reassurance, giving advice, changing subject |

### 9 | Major Psychiatric Conditions

| Condition | Key Nursing Care |
|---|---|
| **Major Depressive Disorder** | Safety (suicide risk!), therapeutic communication, med adherence, ECT care |
| **Schizophrenia** | Reality orientation, manage hallucinations/delusions, antipsychotic monitoring (EPS) |
| **Bipolar Disorder** | Mania: decrease stimuli, finger foods, safety; Depression: as above |
| **Anxiety disorders** | Relaxation techniques, systematic desensitization, CBT referral |
| **PTSD** | Trauma-informed care, grounding techniques, avoid re-traumatization |
| **Substance use disorder** | Withdrawal monitoring (CIWA for alcohol), harm reduction, motivational interviewing |

### 10 | Crisis & Safety

| Situation | Nursing Response |
|---|---|
| **Suicidal ideation** | Directly ask: "Are you thinking about suicide?" — contract for safety, 1:1 observation |
| **Violent/aggressive** | De-escalation first, then seclusion/restraint as last resort (q15m checks) |
| **Panic attack** | Stay calm, guide breathing, reassure "this will pass" |
| **Anorexia nervosa** | Refeeding protocol, monitor electrolytes, structured meal times, 1:1 after meals |

### 11 | Thai Mental Health Context

| Thai Term | Concept |
|---|---|
| **สุขภาพจิต** | Mental health — increasingly recognized as important |
| **ซึมเศร้า** | Depression — still stigmatized; patients may present with somatic complaints |
| **จิตเวช** | Psychiatry — often feared; educate to reduce stigma |
| **การทำจิตบำบัด** | Psychotherapy — growing availability through universal healthcare |
| **สายด่วนสุขภาพจิต 1323** | Mental Health Hotline — national crisis line |
