# Digital Etiquette — มารยาทในโลกออนไลน์

> *"Online, your words ARE your face. No wai, no smile — just the text you chose to send."*

Digital etiquette (มารยาทในโลกออนไลน์, netiquette) extends face-to-face courtesy into written digital communication: emails, chat, social media, comments, and class forums. The curriculum builds it from ม.ต้น (basic email structure, respectful posting) through ม.ปลาย (formal email conventions, tone management across platforms, digital footprint awareness). Thai students face specific friction points — Thai online chat uses different politeness rules than English chat, and English email formality levels are unforgiving in school and work contexts.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ม.1–3** | Email structure (subject, greeting, body, sign-off), polite vs rude posting, ALL CAPS shouting, comment-section respect, chat abbreviations and when they are inappropriate |

## 2 | The Email Register Ladder

| Level | Greeting | Body style | Sign-off | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Formal | Dear Mr. Smith, / Dear Sir or Madam, | Full sentences, no slang, clear paragraphs | Yours sincerely, / Best regards, | Teachers, officials, job applications |
| Semi-formal | Dear Korn, / Hi Anna, | Complete sentences, light contractions | Best, / Thanks, | Classmates, group projects |
| Informal | Hey! / Hi! | Short, emoji and abbreviation OK | Cheers, / CU later, | Close friends, chat |

**Register violation is the #1 digital etiquette error** — sending "Hey teach, where my grade at??" to a teacher reads as rude even without bad intent → [[31 Register & Formality]].

## 3 | Core Netiquette Rules

| Rule | Thai | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Remember the human | จำไว้ว่าปลายทางคือคน | Type to a person, not a screen |
| No SHOUTING | ไม่พิมพ์ตัวใหญ่ทั้งประโยค | ALL CAPS reads as yelling |
| Respect others' time | เคารพเวลาของผู้อื่น | Clear subject lines, concise bodies, no spam |
| Disagree with ideas, not people | วิจารณ์ความคิด ไม่ใช่คน | "I see it differently because..." |
| Check before you send | ตรวจก่อนส่ง | Spelling, tone, attachment, recipient list |
| Protect privacy | ปกป้องความเป็นส่วนตัว | Yours and others'; no sharing without consent |
| Credit sources | อ้างอิงแหล่งที่มา | Quote and attribute; plagiarism applies online |
| Beware the permanent record | ทุกอย่างอยู่บนอินเทอร์เน็ตตลอดไป | The digital footprint outlives the mood → [[70 English Online|English Online]] |

## 4 | Platform Norms Comparison

| Platform | Default register | Typical Thai student gap |
|---|---|---|
| Email (teacher/university) | Formal | Missing subject line, missing greeting, "pls" |
| Class forums / Google Classroom | Semi-formal | One-word replies without greeting |
| Chat (LINE, Messenger) | Informal OK | Copying chat style into email |
| Social media public posts | Depends on audience | Venting in English publicly, forgetting global audience |
| Comment sections | Civil disagreement | Insult escalation, no hedging |

## 5 | Common Thai-Speaker Digital Errors

| Error | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Chat style in email | "hi teacher, i want grade pls" | Full email format with subject → templates above |
| Missing subject line | (empty) — email unseen or spam-filtered | "ม.5/2 Thanawat — Question about homework due Friday" |
| Over-apology or over-demand | "SORRY SORRY SORRY" / "ANSWER NOW" | Neutral professional tone |
| Direct disagreement | "That's wrong." | "I might be misunderstanding, but I thought..." |
| Emoji overload in formal mail | 😊🙏🙏 per line | Reserve emoji for informal channels |
