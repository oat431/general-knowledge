# English for Careers — ภาษาอังกฤษเพื่ออาชีพ

> *"In the Thai job market, English is not a subject — it is a salary multiplier."*

Standard อ 4.1 requires students to use English in real-life situations in the community and workplace; standard อ 4.2 asks them to use English as the basis for further education and careers. The career English strand concentrates in ม.ปลาย: job advertisements, resumes/CVs, application letters, interviews, workplace communication (emails, meetings, phone), and the service-industry English that dominates Thailand's tourism economy.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ม.1–3** | Occupations vocabulary, describing jobs and duties, simple workplace scenarios, why-English-matters awareness |
| **ม.4–6** | Reading job ads, writing resume/CV and application letter, job interviews, business emails, meetings and telephone English, service-industry phrases, workplace etiquette |

## 2 | The Job Application Toolkit

| Document | Structure | Key language |
|---|---|---|
| Resume / CV | Personal data → education → skills → experience → references | Action verbs: managed, organized, assisted, achieved |
| Application letter | Position → where found → why me → availability | I am writing to apply for the position of... |
| Interview answers | Greeting → strengths with evidence → questions | My greatest strength is... For example, ... |
| Follow-up email | Thanks → interest → availability | Thank you for the opportunity to interview for... |

## 3 | The Job Interview Question Bank

| Question | What they want | Strong answer pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself. | Relevant summary, not life story | Present role → key skills → why here |
| What are your strengths? | Self-awareness + proof | Strength + short example |
| What is your weakness? | Honesty + improvement plan | Real weakness + what you do about it |
| Why do you want this job? | Motivation + research | Company fact + personal fit |
| Where do you see yourself in 5 years? | Ambition + realism | Growth within the field |
| Do you have any questions? | Engagement | Ask about training, team, expectations |

## 4 | Workplace English Situations

| Situation | Core phrases |
|---|---|
| Business email | Dear Mr. X, I am writing regarding... / Please find attached... / Best regards, |
| Meetings | opening: Let's get started / opinions: From my point of view / closing: To sum up |
| Telephone | May I ask who's calling? / Could you hold, please? / I'll put you through |
| Customer service | How may I help you? / I'm afraid we can't... but we can... / Would you like a receipt? |
| Hotel and tourism | Check-in dialog, recommending attractions, handling complaints politely |

## 5 | English and the Thai Labor Market

| Sector | English role |
|---|---|
| Tourism and hotels | Front-line service language; higher pay for speakers |
| Manufacturing and logistics | International clients, documentation in English |
| Business and banking | Reports, meetings, overseas communication |
| IT and startups | The working language of code, tools, and clients |
| Government and aviation | International cooperation, ICAO English for pilots/ATC |

**Job-ad reality:** "Good command of English" appears in a large share of Thai professional job postings; the English Skill TOEIC section exists because companies quantify this with test scores → [[46 Business Vocabulary]], [[49 TOEIC Scoring & Strategy]].

## 6 | Common Errors

| Error | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CV style errors | Full sentences, "I", photos (in international format) | Use resume conventions → [[31 Register & Formality]] |
| Interview directness | "I want more money." | Frame professionally: "I am seeking a role with growth potential" |
| Email register | "Hey, send me the file" | Formal request patterns → [[59 Digital Etiquette]] |
| Overclaiming | "I am expert in English" | "I have good working knowledge of English" |
