# English for Health and PE — ภาษาอังกฤษกับสุขศึกษาและพลศึกษา

> *"Sports are the friendliest English classroom: rules must be communicated, and everyone wants to play."*

Standard อ 3.1 links English to health education and physical education: body vocabulary first (ป.1), then sports rules and instructions, health vocabulary, and by ม.ปลาย the language of health issues, sports commentary, and international sporting events. Movement-based activities make this strand the most kinesthetic of the content-and-language bridges.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Body parts and actions (touch, jump, run), hygiene words (wash hands, brush teeth), healthy food vs junk food, TPR movement games |
| **ป.4–6** | Sports names and basic rules, giving game instructions, feelings and illness (headache, fever), first-aid basics, healthy habits sentences |
| **ม.1–3** | Detailed rules of popular sports, describing exercise routines, diet and fitness vocabulary, injury language, reading health advice texts |
| **ม.4–6** | Health issues in the news (smoking, obesity, mental health), sports commentary basics, international events (Olympics, SEA Games), discussing health policy in simple terms |

## 3 | Language Patterns for Health and Sport

| Function | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instructions in games | imperative + sequence | **Stand in a circle. Pass** the ball **clockwise.** |
| Rules | must / have to / can't | Players **must not touch** the ball with their hands. |
| Health advice | should / shouldn't + verb | You **should drink** water after exercise. |
| Describing symptoms | I have a... / My ... hurts | I **have a sore throat and a runny nose**. |
| Results and scores | won / lost / beat / drew by | Thailand **beat** Malaysia 2–1 in the semi-final. |

## 5 | Why This Bridge Matters in Thailand

| Context | English use |
|---|---|
| International sport events | Athletes, referees, and media operate in English |
| Health tourism | Hospitals in Thailand serve foreign patients in English |
| Fitness industry | Trainers use English terminology (sets, reps, cardio) |
| Public health campaigns | Global guidance (WHO) arrives in English first → [[03_Disease_Prevention]] |

## 6 | Common Errors

| Error | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "play" with all sports | "play swimming" | Go swimming / play football / do yoga — three verbs |
| Body-part articles | "I hurt my a leg" | Possessive: "my leg," no article → [[02 Articles]] |
| Illness expressions | "I have fever" | "I have a fever / a cold / a headache" |
| Advice modals | "You must to exercise" | should / must + base verb → [[12 Modals]] |
