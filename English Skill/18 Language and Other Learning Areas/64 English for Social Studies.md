# English for Social Studies — ภาษาอังกฤษกับสังคมศึกษา

> *"Geography, history, and civics in English train the skill Thailand's graduates need most: explaining their country and region to the world."*

Standard อ 3.1 extends English into social studies: geography vocabulary, historical timelines, civic concepts, and — critically for Thai students — **ASEAN knowledge in English**. Social studies content is often more language-dependent than math or science (abstract concepts, long texts), so this strand emphasizes reading strategies and content-specific vocabulary while building toward presentations about Thailand and Southeast Asia.

## 1 | Grade Band Breakdown

| Grade | Key Content |
|---|---|
| **ป.1–3** | Community places (school, hospital, market), transport words, simple maps (left/right/near), national symbols |
| **ป.4–6** | World geography basics: continents, oceans, countries and capitals, compass directions; reading simple maps and timelines in English |
| **ม.1–3** | Geography vocabulary (climate, population, resources), historical time expressions (century, BC/AD, dynasty), ASEAN member states and capitals, civic terms (rights, duties, democracy) |
| **ม.4–6** | Reading current-affairs articles, economic and social statistics language, ASEAN Community pillars, comparative country profiles, presenting Thai history/culture in English |

## 3 | Language Patterns for Social Studies

| Function | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Location | is located in / borders / lies to the... | Thailand **is located in** Southeast Asia and **borders** four countries. |
| Time in history | in + year / during / by | **During** the Ayutthaya period..., **By** 1932 the government had changed. |
| Comparison of countries | comparatively / whereas / in contrast | Singapore is small, **whereas** Indonesia spans thousands of islands. |
| Statistics | accounts for / the majority of / per cent | Tourism **accounts for** about 12 per cent of GDP. |
| Civics description | has the right to / is required to | Every citizen **has the right to** education. |

## 4 | ASEAN Knowledge in English

| Fact set | What students learn to say |
|---|---|
| Members | "ASEAN has ten member states" (Timor-Leste's accession in progress — teach the current official ten + observer status) |

| Pillars | Political-Security Community, Economic Community, Socio-Cultural Community |
| Goals | peace, economic growth, cultural exchange in Southeast Asia |

## 6 | Common Errors

| Error | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Country/nationality confusion | "I am Thailand" | Nationality adjectives: Thai (person/language), Thailand (country) |
| Article with countries | "the Thailand" | No article with most countries; "the United States," "the Philippines" are exceptions |
| Historical tense drift | "Ayutthaya is the capital 400 years ago" | Past time = past tense → [[05 12 English Tense Overview]] |
| Statistics verbs | "The population is increase" | Use rise/increase/reach + correct form → [[41 IELTS Task 1 - Describing Data]] |
