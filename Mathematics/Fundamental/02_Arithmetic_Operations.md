# Arithmetic Operations — การดำเนินการทางคณิตศาสตร์

> *"Arithmetic is where numbers come alive — it is the grammar of mathematics."*

The four basic operations — addition (การบวก), subtraction (การลบ), multiplication (การคูณ), and division (การหาร) — form the computational core of all mathematics. Students progress from concrete manipulation and mental math to algorithm mastery with larger numbers, learning the deep relationships between operations along the way.

## 3 | Key Properties

### 3.1 Addition (การบวก)

| Property | Statement | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Commutative** | a + b = b + a | Order doesn't matter |
| **Associative** | (a + b) + c = a + (b + c) | Grouping doesn't matter |
| **Identity** | a + 0 = a | Adding zero changes nothing |
| **Inverse** | a + (−a) = 0 | Every number has an opposite |

### 3.2 Subtraction (การลบ)

- a − b = a + (−b) — **Subtraction IS adding the opposite**
- NOT commutative: 5 − 3 ≠ 3 − 5
- NOT associative: (10 − 5) − 2 ≠ 10 − (5 − 2)

### 3.3 Multiplication (การคูณ)

| Property | Statement | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Commutative** | a × b = b × a | Order doesn't matter |
| **Associative** | (a × b) × c = a × (b × c) | Grouping doesn't matter |
| **Identity** | a × 1 = a | Multiplying by one changes nothing |
| **Zero** | a × 0 = 0 | Anything times zero is zero |
| **Distributive** | a(b + c) = ab + ac | Links × and + |

### 3.4 Division (การหาร)

- a ÷ b = a × (1/b), where b ≠ 0
- NOT commutative: 12 ÷ 3 ≠ 3 ÷ 12
- a ÷ 1 = a
- a ÷ a = 1 (for a ≠ 0)
- **Division by zero is UNDEFINED**

### 3.5 Order of Operations (ลำดับการดำเนินการ)

| Priority | Operation | Thai |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parentheses ( ) | วงเล็บ |
| 2 | Exponents | ยกกำลัง |
| 3 | Multiplication & Division (L→R) | คูณ/หาร |
| 4 | Addition & Subtraction (L→R) | บวก/ลบ |

**Mnemonic (EN):** Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally (Parentheses, Exponents, Multiply/Divide, Add/Subtract)

**Mnemonic (TH):** ( ) → ยกกำลัง → คูณ/หาร (ซ้ายไปขวา) → บวก/ลบ (ซ้ายไปขวา)

## 4 | Fact Families

A fact family is a set of related arithmetic facts showing the relationship between three numbers:

$$\begin{aligned}
3 + 4 &= 7 \\
4 + 3 &= 7 \\
7 - 3 &= 4 \\
7 - 4 &= 3
\end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
3 \times 4 &= 12 \\
4 \times 3 &= 12 \\
12 \div 3 &= 4 \\
12 \div 4 &= 3
\end{aligned}$$

Fact families build the crucial understanding that addition/subtraction and multiplication/division are **inverse operations**.

## 5 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Multi-digit Addition
> 12,456 + 8,789 = ?

```
  12,456
+  8,789
--------
  21,245
```

**Answer:** 21,245

### Type 2: Distributive Property
> Find 23 × 47 using the distributive property.

$$23 \times 47 = 23 \times (40 + 7) = (23 \times 40) + (23 \times 7) = 920 + 161 = \mathbf{1{,}081}$$

### Type 3: Division with Remainder
> Divide 1,234 by 25.

$$25 \times 49 = 1{,}225 \qquad 1{,}234 - 1{,}225 = 9$$

**Answer:** Quotient = 49, Remainder = 9, or 49 R9

### Type 4: Order of Operations
> Simplify: 15 + 3 × 4 − 8 ÷ 2

| Step | Operation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 × 4 = 12 | 15 + 12 − 8 ÷ 2 |
| 2 | 8 ÷ 2 = 4 | 15 + 12 − 4 |
| 3 | 15 + 12 = 27 | 27 − 4 |
| 4 | 27 − 4 = 23 | **23** |

### Type 5: Exponent Rules (ม.2)
> Simplify: $$2^3 \times 2^5$$

$$2^3 \times 2^5 = 2^{3+5} = 2^8 = 256$$
