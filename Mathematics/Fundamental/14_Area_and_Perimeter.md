# Area and Perimeter — พื้นที่และเส้นรอบรูป

> *"From counting squares to deriving formulas — the study of area and perimeter teaches students that mathematics can quantify the space we inhabit."*

Area and perimeter are fundamental geometric measurements. Students progress from counting unit squares to applying and deriving formulas for increasingly complex shapes, including circles and composite figures.

## 3 | Key Formulas

### 3.1 Area Formulas

| Shape | Area Formula | Thai Name |
|---|---|---|
| **Square** | A = s² (side × side) | สี่เหลี่ยมจัตุรัส |
| **Rectangle** | A = l × w (length × width) | สี่เหลี่ยมผืนผ้า |
| **Triangle** | A = ½ × b × h | สามเหลี่ยม |
| **Parallelogram** | A = b × h | สี่เหลี่ยมด้านขนาน |
| **Trapezoid** | A = ½ × (a + b) × h | สี่เหลี่ยมคางหมู |
| **Circle** | A = π × r² | วงกลม |
| **Sector** | A = (θ/360°) × πr² | เซกเตอร์ |

### 3.2 Perimeter/Circumference Formulas

| Shape | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Any polygon** | Sum of all side lengths |
| **Rectangle** | P = 2(l + w) |
| **Square** | P = 4s |
| **Circle (circumference)** | C = 2πr = πd |

### 3.3 Triangle Area — Three Versions

$$A = \frac{1}{2} \times b \times h$$

| If you know... | Use formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Base and height | A = ½ × b × h | b=8, h=5 → A=20 |
| Three sides (Heron's formula) | A = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)] where s = (a+b+c)/2 | ม.3 |
| Two sides and included angle | A = ½ × a × b × sin(C) | ม.3 |

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Rectangle Area
> A rectangle has length 12 cm and width 5 cm. Find area and perimeter.

Area = 12 × 5 = **60 cm²**. Perimeter = 2(12 + 5) = **34 cm**.

### Type 2: Triangle Area
> A triangle has base 10 cm and height 6 cm. Find area.

A = ½ × 10 × 6 = **30 cm²**.

### Type 3: Trapezoid Area
> A trapezoid has parallel sides of 8 cm and 12 cm, height 5 cm. Find area.

A = ½ × (8 + 12) × 5 = ½ × 20 × 5 = **50 cm²**.

### Type 4: Circle Area and Circumference
> A circle has radius 7 cm. Find area and circumference (use π ≈ 22/7).

C = 2 × (22/7) × 7 = **44 cm**. A = (22/7) × 7² = (22/7) × 49 = **154 cm²**.

### Type 5: Composite Shape
> Find the area of a shape made of a 10×6 rectangle with a semicircle (r=3) on one of the 10 cm sides.

Rectangle area = 10 × 6 = 60. Semicircle = ½ × π × 3² = ½ × π × 9 ≈ 14.14. **Total ≈ 74.14 cm²**.
