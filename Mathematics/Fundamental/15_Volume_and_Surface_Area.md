# Volume and Surface Area — ปริมาตรและพื้นที่ผิว

> *"Volume measures the space a 3D object occupies; surface area measures the 'skin' that wraps around it. Together they teach students to think in three dimensions."*

Volume and surface area extend measurement into the third dimension. Students move from counting unit cubes to deriving and applying formulas for prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, and spheres.

## 3 | Key Formulas

### 3.1 Volume

| Shape | Volume Formula | Thai |
|---|---|---|
| **Cube** | V = s³ | ลูกบาศก์ |
| **Rectangular Prism** | V = l × w × h | ปริซึมสี่เหลี่ยม |
| **Prism (general)** | V = B × h | ปริซึม |
| **Cylinder** | V = πr² × h | ทรงกระบอก |
| **Pyramid** | V = ⅓ × B × h | พีระมิด |
| **Cone** | V = ⅓ × πr² × h | กรวย |
| **Sphere** | V = ⁴⁄₃ × π × r³ | ทรงกลม |

### 3.2 Surface Area

| Shape | Surface Area |
|---|---|
| **Cube** | SA = 6 × s² |
| **Rectangular Prism** | SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) |
| **Cylinder** | SA = 2πr² + 2πrh (two circles + curved) |
| **Cone** | SA = πr² + πrl (base + curved, l = slant height) |
| **Sphere** | SA = 4 × π × r² |

### 3.3 Key Relationships

- **Prism vs Pyramid:** Vpyramid = ⅓ × Vprism (same base and height)
- **Cylinder vs Cone:** Vcone = ⅓ × Vcylinder (same base and height)
- **Capacity:** 1 cm³ = 1 mL; 1,000 cm³ = 1 L; 1 m³ = 1,000 L

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Rectangular Prism
> A box is 10 cm × 6 cm × 4 cm. Find volume and surface area.

V = 10 × 6 × 4 = **240 cm³**. SA = 2(60 + 40 + 24) = 2(124) = **248 cm²**.

### Type 2: Cylinder
> A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Find volume and surface area (π ≈ 22/7).

V = π × 7² × 10 = (22/7) × 490 = **1,540 cm³**.
SA = 2πr² + 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 49 + 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 10 = 308 + 440 = **748 cm²**.

### Type 3: Pyramid
> A square pyramid has base side 6 cm and height 8 cm. Find volume.

B = 6² = 36. V = ⅓ × 36 × 8 = **96 cm³**.

### Type 4: Sphere
> Find the volume of a sphere with radius 3 cm.

V = ⁴⁄₃ × π × 3³ = ⁴⁄₃ × π × 27 = **36π ≈ 113.1 cm³**.
