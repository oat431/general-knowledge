# Percentages — ร้อยละ

> *"Percent simply means 'per hundred' — a brilliantly simple idea that lets us compare quantities, measure change, and make financial decisions using a common scale."*

Percentages are a special type of fraction with denominator 100. This topic covers the full spectrum: from understanding what a percentage means, through conversion between forms, to real-world applications including discounts, profit/loss, interest, and statistical interpretation.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 The Three Basic Percentage Problems

| Type | Given | Find | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type 1: Find the Part** | % and Whole | Part | Part = (% ÷ 100) × Whole |
| **Type 2: Find the Percent** | Part and Whole | % | % = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100 |
| **Type 3: Find the Whole** | % and Part | Whole | Whole = Part ÷ (% ÷ 100) |

### 3.2 Conversions

| From | To | Method | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Percent → Decimal | Divide by 100 | 75% = 0.75 |
| Decimal → Percent | Multiply by 100 | 0.625 = 62.5% |
| Percent → Fraction | Write over 100, simplify | 25% = 25/100 = 1/4 |
| Fraction → Percent | Convert to decimal, × 100 | 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5% |

### 3.3 Percentage Change

$$\text{Percentage Change} = \frac{\text{New} - \text{Original}}{\text{Original}} \times 100\%$$

- **Positive:** Increase
- **Negative:** Decrease

**Example:** Price increases from 200 ฿ to 250 ฿.

$$\frac{250 - 200}{200} \times 100\% = \frac{50}{200} \times 100\% = 25\% \text{ increase}$$

### 3.4 Discount and Sale Price

$$\text{Discount} = \text{Original} \times \frac{\text{Discount\%}}{100}$$

$$\text{Sale Price} = \text{Original} - \text{Discount}$$

Or directly:

$$\text{Sale Price} = \text{Original} \times \left(1 - \frac{\text{Discount\%}}{100}\right)$$

**Example:** A 500 ฿ item with 20% off:
- $$\text{Discount} = 500 \times 0.20 = 100 \text{ ฿}$$
- $$\text{Sale Price} = 500 - 100 = 400 \text{ ฿}$$
- Or: $$500 \times 0.80 = 400 \text{ ฿}$$

### 3.5 Profit and Loss

| Term | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Profit (กำไร)** | $$\text{SP} - \text{CP}$$ (when SP > CP) |
| **Loss (ขาดทุน)** | $$\text{CP} - \text{SP}$$ (when CP > SP) |
| **Profit %** | $$\frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}} \times 100\%$$ |
| **Loss %** | $$\frac{\text{Loss}}{\text{CP}} \times 100\%$$ |

### 3.6 Simple Interest (ดอกเบี้ย)

$$I = P \times r \times t$$

Where: I = Interest, P = Principal, r = rate (as decimal), t = time (in years)

**Example:** 10,000 ฿ at 5% for 3 years:

$$I = 10{,}000 \times 0.05 \times 3 = 1{,}500 \text{ ฿}$$

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Find the Part
> What is 30% of 450?

(30 ÷ 100) × 450 = 0.30 × 450 = **135**

### Type 2: Find the Percent
> 36 is what percent of 240?

(36 ÷ 240) × 100% = 0.15 × 100% = **15%**

### Type 3: Find the Whole
> 45 is 15% of what number?

45 ÷ (15 ÷ 100) = 45 ÷ 0.15 = **300**

### Type 4: Discount
> A shirt originally priced at 800 ฿ is on sale for 25% off. What is the sale price?

Discount = 0.25 × 800 = 200 ฿. Sale price = 800 − 200 = **600 ฿**.

### Type 5: Profit
> A merchant buys a watch for 1,200 ฿ and sells it for 1,500 ฿. What is the profit percent?

Profit = 1,500 − 1,200 = 300 ฿. Profit % = (300 ÷ 1,200) × 100% = **25%**.

### Type 6: VAT
> A product is priced at 2,140 ฿ including 7% VAT. What is the price before VAT?

Price before VAT = 2,140 ÷ 1.07 = **2,000 ฿**.
