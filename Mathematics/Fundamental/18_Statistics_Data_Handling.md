# Statistics and Data Handling — สถิติและการจัดการข้อมูล

> *"In a world awash with data, statistics is the art of making sense of it — collecting, organizing, analyzing, and drawing meaningful conclusions from numbers."*

Statistics develops data literacy from the earliest grades. Students progress from simple tally marks and pictographs to calculating measures of central tendency and interpreting complex data displays. This topic builds critical thinking skills essential for science, social studies, and informed citizenship.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Types of Graphs

| Graph Type | Best For | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Pictograph** | Simple data, young learners | 😊😊😊😊😊 = 5 students |
| **Bar graph** | Comparing categories | Favorite fruits by count |
| **Line graph** | Trends over time | Temperature over a week |
| **Pie chart** | Parts of a whole | Budget allocation (%) |
| **Histogram** | Distribution of numerical data | Test score ranges |

### 3.2 Measures of Central Tendency

| Measure | How to Find | Example: {3, 7, 7, 8, 10} |
|---|---|---|
| **Mean** (ค่าเฉลี่ย) | Sum ÷ count | (3+7+7+8+10) ÷ 5 = **7** |
| **Median** (มัธยฐาน) | Middle value (sort first) | Sorted: 3, 7, **7**, 8, 10 → **7** |
| **Mode** (ฐานนิยม) | Most frequent value | **7** (appears twice) |
| **Range** (พิสัย) | Max − Min | 10 − 3 = **7** |

> ⚠️ **Outliers** — the mean can be skewed by extreme values; the median is more robust.

### 3.3 Choosing the Right Measure

| Data Characteristic | Best Measure |
|---|---|
| Data is symmetric (no outliers) | Mean |
| Data has outliers or is skewed | Median |
| Categorical data | Mode |

### 3.4 Interpreting Statistical Claims

- **Correlation ≠ Causation** (ความสัมพันธ์ไม่ใช่สาเหตุ)
- **Sample size matters** — small samples may be misleading
- **Beware of cherry-picked data** and exaggerated axes on graphs

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Find Mean, Median, Mode
> Find the mean, median, and mode of: 5, 8, 8, 10, 14

Sorted: 5, 8, 8, 10, 14.
Mean = (5+8+8+10+14) ÷ 5 = 45 ÷ 5 = **9**.
Median = **8** (middle). Mode = **8** (most frequent).

### Type 2: Read a Bar Graph
> Given a bar graph of monthly rainfall, which month had the most rain?

Read the tallest bar. If September is tallest, answer: **September**.

### Type 3: Create Frequency Table
> Test scores: 85, 72, 85, 90, 72, 85, 78.

| Score | Frequency |
|---|---|
| 72 | 2 |
| 78 | 1 |
| 85 | 3 |
| 90 | 1 |

### Type 4: Missing Value
> The mean of 5 numbers is 12. Four numbers are 10, 11, 13, 14. Find the fifth.

Sum = 5 × 12 = 60. Known sum = 10+11+13+14 = 48. Fifth = 60 − 48 = **12**.
