# Ratios and Proportions — อัตราส่วนและสัดส่วน

> *"A ratio compares; a proportion equates. Together they form the backbone of proportional reasoning — perhaps the most practically useful mathematical skill in everyday life."*

Ratios express the relationship between quantities, and proportions extend this to solve problems involving scale, speed, mixtures, and more. This topic builds from simple ratio notation through direct and inverse proportion to real-world applications.

## 3 | Key Concepts

### 3.1 Ratio Definition

A **ratio** compares two or more quantities of the same kind:

$$a : b \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{a}{b}$$

Ratios have **no units** — they are pure numbers.

**Simplifying ratios** (like simplifying fractions):

$$12 : 18 = \frac{12}{6} : \frac{18}{6} = 2 : 3$$

### 3.2 Proportion Definition

A **proportion** is an equation stating that two ratios are equal:

$$\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$$

**Cross-multiplication:** If two ratios are proportional, then $$ad = bc$$.

### 3.3 Direct Proportion (สัดส่วนตรง)

When two quantities vary **directly**, as one increases, the other increases at the same rate:

$$y \propto x \quad \Rightarrow \quad y = kx$$

**The ratio y/x is constant (= k).**

| x | y | y/x |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 6 | 3 |
| 4 | 12 | 3 |
| 6 | 18 | 3 |
| 10 | 30 | 3 |

### 3.4 Inverse Proportion (สัดส่วนผกผัน)

When two quantities vary **inversely**, as one increases, the other decreases:

$$y \propto \frac{1}{x} \quad \Rightarrow \quad y = \frac{k}{x}$$

**The product xy is constant (= k).**

| Workers | Days | Workers × Days |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 12 | 24 |
| 3 | 8 | 24 |
| 4 | 6 | 24 |
| 6 | 4 | 24 |

### 3.5 The Unitary Method (วิธีเทียบบัญญัติไตรยางศ์)

**Find the value of 1 unit first, then multiply by the desired quantity.**

> 5 notebooks cost 120 ฿. How much do 8 notebooks cost?

1 notebook = 120 ÷ 5 = 24 ฿. 8 notebooks = 24 × 8 = **192 ฿**.

### 3.6 Scale (มาตราส่วน)

$$\text{Scale} = \frac{\text{Drawing Distance}}{\text{Actual Distance}}$$

> A map has scale 1 : 50,000. Two cities are 8 cm apart on the map. What is the actual distance?

Actual = 8 × 50,000 = 400,000 cm = **4 km**.

### 3.7 Speed, Distance, Time

$$\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$$
$$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$$
$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$

## 4 | Common Problem Types

### Type 1: Simplifying Ratios
> Simplify 36 : 48.

GCD(36, 48) = 12. 36/12 : 48/12 = **3 : 4**.

### Type 2: Sharing in a Ratio
> Share 500 ฿ in the ratio 2 : 3.

Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5. One part = 500 ÷ 5 = 100 ฿.
- First share: 2 × 100 = **200 ฿**
- Second share: 3 × 100 = **300 ฿**

### Type 3: Direct Proportion
> If 5 pens cost 75 ฿, how much do 12 pens cost?

5 pens → 75 ฿. 1 pen → 75/5 = 15 ฿. 12 pens → 12 × 15 = **180 ฿**.

### Type 4: Inverse Proportion
> 4 workers can complete a job in 6 days. How long would 6 workers take?

Total work = 4 × 6 = 24 worker-days. With 6 workers: 24 ÷ 6 = **4 days**.

### Type 5: Speed Problem
> A car travels 240 km in 3 hours. How long to travel 400 km at the same speed?

Speed = 240 ÷ 3 = 80 km/h. Time = 400 ÷ 80 = **5 hours**.

## 5 | Direct vs Inverse Proportion — Quick Reference

| Feature | Direct (y = kx) | Inverse (y = k/x) |
|---|---|---|
| **As x increases** | y increases | y decreases |
| **Constant** | y/x = k | xy = k |
| **Graph** | Straight line through origin | Hyperbola |
| **Real examples** | Price per item, distance at constant speed | Workers and time, speed and time |
